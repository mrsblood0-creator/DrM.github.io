Privacy policy

This policy explains how Dr M Courses (drmcourses.com) handles information when you browse lectures, watch videos, answer questions, or use flashcards.

Information and study progress

You can use the study tools without creating an account. Bookmarks, lecture progress, question results, flashcard review schedules, preferences, and your edited notes are saved in your browser’s local storage. The study tools do not upload this saved study data to a Dr M account or synchronize it between devices.

Local data remains until you clear it, reset the relevant tool, or your browser removes it. Clearing site data may permanently erase your progress and personal notes. Download any notes or study exports you want to keep first.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request and retain correspondence as needed to handle it and meet applicable obligations. Please do not include patient-identifying information or sensitive medical records in messages or study notes.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand site usage and improve the learning experience. Google may process cookie identifiers, pages viewed, interaction information, referring pages, and browser, device, and approximate location information.

Our custom page-view tracking removes URL query parameters and does not intentionally send the contents of your personal study notes or search input. Google’s own collection depends on its technology and the Analytics settings in use.

Learn how Google uses information from sites using its services, read the Google Privacy Policy, or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Analytics retention depends on the account settings; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion period for Google-held data.

Google AdSense and advertising

Our website includes Google AdSense advertising code. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver and measure advertisements. Advertising cookies can allow Google and its partners to personalize ads using visits to this website and other websites.

You can manage Google ad personalization through My Ad Center. Participating third-party advertising providers offer additional opt-out choices through YourAdChoices. Opting out of personalization does not necessarily stop all advertisements or all advertising-related data processing.

See Google’s advertising technology information for more details. Where a consent message is displayed, use its available controls to manage your choices. Browser cookie settings and advertising opt-outs serve different purposes and are not a substitute for consent where consent is required.

YouTube videos, images, and website hosting

Lecture videos are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced player domain. Loading or interacting with a player can connect your browser to Google or YouTube and disclose technical information, such as your IP address and browser details. Playing videos may involve cookies or similar storage. Privacy-enhanced mode does not mean that no data is processed.

Video thumbnails may load from YouTube servers. External links take you to services governed by their own privacy policies.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and delivery providers may process technical request data, including IP addresses and requested URLs, to deliver and secure the website. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices and privacy requests

Use to allow or reject Analytics, advertising, and YouTube players separately. Optional services remain blocked until allowed. Your choice is saved on this browser for up to 180 days. Changing your choice stops future loading; it does not erase information already received by third parties. Withdrawing Analytics or advertising permission reloads the page and attempts to clear accessible first-party measurement cookies. Third-party cookies can be removed through browser settings.

YouTube thumbnails still load as ordinary images and may disclose technical request information even when video players are disabled.

  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies and clear this site’s local storage. Blocking storage can affect saved progress and study tools.
  • Use Google’s Analytics and advertising controls linked above to manage those services.
  • Contact us about information you have sent directly to us. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, request portability, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it.

We cannot retrieve locally stored study notes or progress from your device. For information held by Google, YouTube, or GitHub, their privacy policies describe their request procedures. Depending on your location, you may also be able to complain to your local data protection authority.

Service providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy policies explain their processing locations and applicable safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children and changes to this policy

This website is intended for medical learners and educators, rather than children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
