Chloramphenicol: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A neonate exposed to chloramphenicol develops abdominal distension, gray discoloration and circulatory collapse. Drug concentrations are unexpectedly high despite a dose that would be better tolerated in an older patient. Which developmental pharmacokinetic limitation explains this?

  1. Unpredictable aplastic anemia
  2. Reserve use when safer effective alternatives are unsuitable
  3. Monitor blood counts and clinical toxicity
  4. Immature neonatal chloramphenicol elimination
  5. Reversible dose-related marrow suppression
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Immature neonatal chloramphenicol elimination

Limited neonatal metabolism and elimination can cause chloramphenicol accumulation and gray baby syndrome.

Understanding the options

Unpredictable aplastic anemia
