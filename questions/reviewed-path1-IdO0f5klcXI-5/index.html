Chalky deposits around the pancreas

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with severe acute pancreatitis undergoes surgery for a complication. The surgeon finds chalky white areas in surrounding adipose tissue. Which reaction most directly explains the gross appearance of these deposits?

  1. Calcium combines with fatty acids released by lipases
  2. Iron accumulates in macrophage lysosomes
  3. Bilirubin precipitates throughout the adipose tissue
  4. Fibrin and immune complexes collect in all adipocytes
  5. Intact triglycerides crystallize without enzymatic injury
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Calcium combines with fatty acids released by lipases

Saponification follows enzymatic fat destruction.

Understanding the options

Calcium combines with fatty acids released by lipases
Saponification follows enzymatic fat destruction.
Iron accumulates in macrophage lysosomes
Hemosiderin is brown and does not account for chalky calcium soaps.
Bilirubin precipitates throughout the adipose tissue
Bile pigment does not explain the described peripancreatic pattern.
Fibrin and immune complexes collect in all adipocytes
Fibrinoid injury is associated with vessel walls.
Intact triglycerides crystallize without enzymatic injury
The characteristic lesion follows lipase-mediated release of fatty acids.
