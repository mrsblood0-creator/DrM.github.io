Hernia treatment principles: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with a previously manageable groin hernia develops severe persistent pain, tenderness and vomiting. The clinician suspects ischemic trapped bowel. Which care priority is appropriate rather than arranging only routine elective review?

  1. Shared decision-making about watchful waiting in selected patients
  2. Reinforcement of the abdominal-wall defect
  3. Preperitoneal plane
  4. Individualize mesh and repair choice to contamination and patient factors
  5. Urgent surgical assessment for suspected strangulation
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Urgent surgical assessment for suspected strangulation

Severe pain, tenderness and systemic or ischemic signs must not be managed as a routine elective bulge.
