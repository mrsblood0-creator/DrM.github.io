Acute liver failure: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient’s acute liver failure follows excessive acetaminophen exposure. Alongside critical care and specialist assessment, which cause-specific antidotal therapy is relevant to limiting injury from the reactive metabolite responsible for the poisoning?

  1. Hepatocellular injury pattern
  2. Cholestatic pattern
  3. Impaired hepatic synthetic function
  4. Albumin reflects more than acute hepatic injury
  5. N-acetylcysteine for acetaminophen-associated injury
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

N-acetylcysteine for acetaminophen-associated injury

Acetaminophen-related acute liver failure requires prompt antidotal and specialist management.

Understanding the options

Hepatocellular injury pattern
Predominant AST and ALT elevation indicates hepatocyte injury, not a direct measurement of total liver function. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Cholestatic pattern
Predominant ALP elevation with supportive GGT findings suggests a hepatobiliary source and impaired bile flow. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Impaired hepatic synthetic function
An elevated INR can reflect reduced clotting-factor synthesis, after considering anticoagulation and other causes. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Albumin reflects more than acute hepatic injury
Albumin has a relatively long half-life and is influenced by inflammation, nutrition and protein loss. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
