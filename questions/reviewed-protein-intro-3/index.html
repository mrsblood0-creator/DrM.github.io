An initiation-complex inhibitor

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An isolate of a resistant gram-positive organism is treated with linezolid after susceptibility review. The drug binds bacterial 23S rRNA and prevents assembly of a functional initiation complex. Which ribosomal subunit contains the binding site described?

  1. 80S as an isolated bacterial subunit
  2. 50S
  3. 30S
  4. 40S
  5. 60S
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

50S

Linezolid binds 23S rRNA in the bacterial 50S subunit.

Understanding the options

50S
Linezolid binds 23S rRNA in the bacterial 50S subunit.
30S
This is the bacterial small subunit targeted by classes such as tetracyclines.
40S
This is the mammalian cytoplasmic small subunit.
60S
This is the mammalian cytoplasmic large subunit.
80S as an isolated bacterial subunit
80S describes the complete mammalian cytoplasmic ribosome, not a bacterial subunit.
