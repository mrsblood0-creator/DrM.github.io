Synthesis inhibition

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A researcher compares two asthma medicines. One blocks leukotriene receptors, whereas the other inhibits the enzyme responsible for initiating leukotriene synthesis from arachidonic acid. Which drug belongs to the second group rather than the receptor-antagonist group?

  1. Ipratropium
  2. Albuterol
  3. Zileuton
  4. Montelukast
  5. Zafirlukast
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Zileuton

Zileuton inhibits 5-lipoxygenase and reduces leukotriene synthesis.

Understanding the options

Zileuton
Zileuton inhibits 5-lipoxygenase and reduces leukotriene synthesis.
Montelukast
Montelukast is a leukotriene receptor antagonist.
Zafirlukast
Zafirlukast also blocks leukotriene receptors.
Ipratropium
It blocks muscarinic receptors.
Albuterol
It stimulates beta-2 receptors.
