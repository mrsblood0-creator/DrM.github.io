Closed fracture treatment: 4

A selected fracture is temporarily managed by a controlled longitudinal pulling force to improve or maintain alignment while definitive planning continues. Which treatment method best describes the use of this sustained force?

  1. Stability-guided rehabilitation
  2. Traction
  3. Closed reduction
  4. Open reduction
  5. Cast or splint immobilization
Answer and explanation

Traction

A controlled pulling force can maintain or improve alignment in selected circumstances.

Option explanations

Traction: A controlled pulling force can maintain or improve alignment in selected circumstances.

Closed reduction: Alignment is restored without surgically exposing the fracture site. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Open reduction: The fracture is exposed surgically to restore alignment when indicated. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Cast or splint immobilization: External immobilization maintains suitable alignment while monitoring swelling, skin and neurovascular status. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Stability-guided rehabilitation: Joint movement and loading progress according to fracture stability and the treatment plan. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
