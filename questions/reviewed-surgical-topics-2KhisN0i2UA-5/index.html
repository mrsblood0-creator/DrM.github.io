Omphalocele: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A second newborn instead has uncovered bowel protruding beside a normally inserted umbilical cord, usually on the right. Which defect is this, distinguishing the finding from the membrane-covered midline lesion discussed in the lecture?

  1. Staged reduction when immediate closure would be unsafe
  2. Gastroschisis
  3. Omphalocele
  4. Assess associated structural and chromosomal abnormalities
  5. Protect exposed or vulnerable viscera and prevent heat and fluid loss
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Gastroschisis

Usually right paraumbilical uncovered bowel protrusion suggests gastroschisis.
