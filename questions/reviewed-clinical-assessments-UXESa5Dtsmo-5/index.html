CURB-65: 5

A 78-year-old with community-acquired pneumonia is newly confused. Urea is 12 mmol/L, respiratory rate 34/min and blood pressure 84/56 mmHg. What CURB-65 score results when all the stated criteria are evaluated?

  1. 3
  2. 5
  3. 0
  4. 1
  5. 2
Answer and explanation

5

All five criteria are present. Low systolic and diastolic pressure together still count as only one blood-pressure point.

Option explanations

0: CURB-65 assigns one point each for confusion, urea >7 mmol/L, respiratory rate ≥30/min, systolic pressure <90 or diastolic ≤60 mmHg, and age ≥65. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

1: CURB-65 assigns one point each for confusion, urea >7 mmol/L, respiratory rate ≥30/min, systolic pressure <90 or diastolic ≤60 mmHg, and age ≥65. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

2: CURB-65 assigns one point each for confusion, urea >7 mmol/L, respiratory rate ≥30/min, systolic pressure <90 or diastolic ≤60 mmHg, and age ≥65. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

3: CURB-65 assigns one point each for confusion, urea >7 mmol/L, respiratory rate ≥30/min, systolic pressure <90 or diastolic ≤60 mmHg, and age ≥65. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

5: All five criteria are present. Low systolic and diastolic pressure together still count as only one blood-pressure point.
