Nefopam: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient starts nefopam and develops a rapid heartbeat with troublesome sweating. Other causes are assessed, but the symptoms fit recognized autonomic effects. Which listed adverse-effect pair best matches this medication history?

  1. Seizure risk
  2. MAO inhibitor interaction
  3. Antimuscarinic-type adverse effects
  4. Tachycardia and sweating
  5. Central nonopioid analgesia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Tachycardia and sweating

Nefopam can produce autonomic adverse effects including tachycardia and sweating.
