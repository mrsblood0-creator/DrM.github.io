Tigecycline: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient has persistent bloodstream infection, and tigecycline monotherapy is questioned despite laboratory susceptibility. The drug distributes extensively into tissues, leaving relatively low circulating concentrations. Which pharmacokinetic concern best explains the team’s hesitation?

  1. Inhibition of aminoacyl-tRNA binding to 30S
  2. Pseudomonas aeruginosa
  3. Increased all-cause mortality signal
  4. Nausea and vomiting
  5. Low serum concentrations relative to tissue distribution
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Low serum concentrations relative to tissue distribution

Extensive tissue distribution limits the suitability of tigecycline alone for bloodstream infection.

Understanding the options
