Pigment after a bruise

Several weeks after a large hematoma resolves, macrophages remain in the affected tissue. They contain coarse golden-brown cytoplasmic granules that turn blue with a Prussian blue stain. Which substance accumulated following erythrocyte breakdown?

  1. Bilirubin
  2. Hemosiderin
  3. Melanin
  4. Lipofuscin
  5. Carbon
Answer and explanation

Hemosiderin

Macrophages degrade erythrocytes and can store the released iron as hemosiderin, demonstrated by Prussian blue staining.

Option explanations

Hemosiderin: Macrophages degrade erythrocytes and can store the released iron as hemosiderin, demonstrated by Prussian blue staining.

Melanin: Melanin is not an iron-storage pigment.

Lipofuscin: Lipofuscin is a residual lipid-protein pigment, not typically Prussian-blue positive.

Carbon: Carbon is exogenous black particulate material.

Bilirubin: Bilirubin is a heme catabolite but does not give the characteristic iron-stain reaction.
