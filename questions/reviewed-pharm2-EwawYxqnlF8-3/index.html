New hypertension

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 51-year-old woman starts bupropion and a nicotine patch during a supervised smoking-cessation attempt. Two weeks later, repeated blood pressure readings are 166/98 mm Hg; her baseline was 122/76 mm Hg. She has no chest pain or neurologic symptoms. Which monitoring measure was particularly important when this regimen began?

  1. Serial serum lithium concentrations
  2. Daily international normalized ratios
  3. Routine weekly serum prolactin levels
  4. Baseline and follow-up blood pressure measurement
  5. Weekly absolute neutrophil counts
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Baseline and follow-up blood pressure measurement

Both the treatment history and the new elevation warrant blood pressure review and reassessment of the regimen.

Understanding the options

Baseline and follow-up blood pressure measurement
Both the treatment history and the new elevation warrant blood pressure review and reassessment of the regimen.
Weekly absolute neutrophil counts
Routine neutrophil surveillance is associated with other drugs such as clozapine.
Serial serum lithium concentrations
She is not receiving lithium.
Daily international normalized ratios
Neither medication is a vitamin K antagonist requiring INR-guided dosing.
Routine weekly serum prolactin levels
Hyperprolactinemia is not the characteristic concern for this regimen.
