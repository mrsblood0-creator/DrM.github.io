Contrasting analgesic mechanisms

A medical student compares tramadol with a conventional pure mu-opioid agonist. Both can produce opioid adverse effects, but tramadol has an additional monoaminergic action relevant to analgesia and drug interactions. Which paired action correctly describes that additional mechanism?

  1. Reduced reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine
  2. Increased reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine
  3. Blockade of serotonin synthesis and dopamine synthesis
  4. Direct activation of GABA-A and GABA-B receptors
  5. Inhibition of histamine degradation and acetylcholine release
Answer and explanation

Reduced reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine

Tramadol also inhibits serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake.

Option explanations

Reduced reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine: Tramadol also inhibits serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake.

Increased reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine: This reverses the monoaminergic action.

Blockade of serotonin synthesis and dopamine synthesis: This is not its analgesic mechanism.

Direct activation of GABA-A and GABA-B receptors: These are not its characteristic monoaminergic targets.

Inhibition of histamine degradation and acetylcholine release: This does not describe tramadol’s additional analgesic action.
