Pathological fractures: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with known breast cancer develops a femoral fracture through a destructive bone lesion after minimal trauma. Which underlying process should be strongly considered when classifying and planning care for this pathological fracture?

  1. Metastatic bone disease
  2. Osteoporosis
  3. Multiple myeloma
  4. Osteomalacia
  5. Characterize the lesion before definitive fixation when feasible
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Metastatic bone disease

A destructive lesion in a patient with a known malignancy raises concern for metastatic pathological fracture.

Understanding the options

Metastatic bone disease
A destructive lesion in a patient with a known malignancy raises concern for metastatic pathological fracture.
Osteoporosis
Reduced bone strength can cause fragility fractures after low-energy trauma. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Multiple myeloma
Lytic lesions with anemia, renal dysfunction and a monoclonal protein suggest plasma-cell disease. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Osteomalacia
Defective mineralization can produce bone pain and insufficiency fractures. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Characterize the lesion before definitive fixation when feasible
A suspicious unexplained bone lesion requires planned oncologic assessment; an unplanned biopsy or fixation can compromise treatment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
