Persistent nonunion

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 37-year-old construction worker reports chronic wrist pain and reduced motion months after an untreated scaphoid injury. Imaging shows persistent nonunion with degenerative change around the wrist. Which long-term complication is most consistent with this course?

  1. De Quervain tenosynovitis
  2. Isolated median nerve entrapment
  3. Primary inflammatory polyarthritis
  4. Post-traumatic wrist arthritis
  5. Kienbock disease
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Post-traumatic wrist arthritis

Unhealed scaphoid injury can disrupt wrist mechanics and lead to degenerative change.

Understanding the options

Post-traumatic wrist arthritis
Unhealed scaphoid injury can disrupt wrist mechanics and lead to degenerative change.
Kienbock disease
This concerns avascular necrosis of the lunate, rather than degenerative change secondary to scaphoid nonunion.
De Quervain tenosynovitis
This involves the first dorsal tendon compartment and does not explain the nonunion-associated joint degeneration.
Isolated median nerve entrapment
Nerve compression may cause sensory symptoms but does not explain this structural radiographic pattern.
Primary inflammatory polyarthritis
The localized degenerative findings following an unhealed fracture favor a post-traumatic process.
