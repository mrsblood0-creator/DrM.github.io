Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A motorcyclist has deformity of the mid-forearm. Radiographs demonstrate complete shaft fractures of both radius and ulna, with normal alignment of the radial head and distal radioulnar joint. Which description best characterizes the primary bony injury pattern?

  1. Both-bone forearm shaft fracture
  2. Galeazzi fracture-dislocation
  3. Monteggia fracture-dislocation
  4. Essex-Lopresti injury
  5. Isolated ulnar shaft fracture
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Both-bone forearm shaft fracture

Fractures of both radial and ulnar shafts can disrupt the alignment required for pronation and supination.

Understanding the options

Both-bone forearm shaft fracture
Fractures of both radial and ulnar shafts can disrupt the alignment required for pronation and supination.
Galeazzi fracture-dislocation
A radial shaft fracture is associated with disruption of the distal radioulnar joint. This is not the pattern specified in this case.
Monteggia fracture-dislocation
An ulnar fracture or deformity is accompanied by radial head dislocation. This is not the pattern specified in this case.
Essex-Lopresti injury
A radial head fracture is associated with interosseous membrane disruption and distal radioulnar joint injury. This is not the pattern specified in this case.
Isolated ulnar shaft fracture
A direct blow may fracture the ulnar shaft without radial fracture or associated proximal or distal joint disruption. This is not the pattern specified in this case.
