Clearance decreases during infusion

A hypothetical drug is infused at a constant rate and has reached steady state under linear kinetics. The patient's total drug clearance falls by half, while the distribution volume and desired target concentration remain unchanged. Which adjustment preserves the previous average concentration in this simplified model?

  1. Leave the infusion rate unchanged indefinitely
  2. Give an additional loading dose while keeping the infusion unchanged
  3. Increase the target concentration by half
  4. Reduce the maintenance infusion rate by half
  5. Double the maintenance infusion rate
Answer and explanation

Reduce the maintenance infusion rate by half

At steady state, infusion rate equals clearance multiplied by the target concentration.

Option explanations

Reduce the maintenance infusion rate by half: At steady state, infusion rate equals clearance multiplied by the target concentration.

Double the maintenance infusion rate: This would further increase exposure when clearance has fallen.

Leave the infusion rate unchanged indefinitely: The eventual concentration would double under the stated assumptions.

Give an additional loading dose while keeping the infusion unchanged: An extra loading dose does not correct excessive ongoing input relative to clearance.

Increase the target concentration by half: Changing the intended target does not preserve the previous concentration.
