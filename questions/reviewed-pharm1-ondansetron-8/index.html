Chemotherapy-triggered peripheral signaling

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

During development of an antiemetic protocol, investigators demonstrate that intestinal enterochromaffin cells release serotonin after cytotoxic injury. Ondansetron decreases the associated afferent neural activity. Which pathway is most directly interrupted before the signal reaches the brainstem?

  1. Acetylcholine activation of skeletal muscle nicotinic receptors
  2. Dopamine activation of renal D1 receptors
  3. Gastrin activation of parietal-cell CCK-B receptors
  4. Serotonin activation of vagal afferent 5-HT3 receptors
  5. Histamine activation of vestibular H1 receptors
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Serotonin activation of vagal afferent 5-HT3 receptors

Serotonin released in the intestine can activate vagal afferents and trigger emetic signaling through 5-HT3 receptors.

Understanding the options

Serotonin activation of vagal afferent 5-HT3 receptors
Serotonin released in the intestine can activate vagal afferents and trigger emetic signaling through 5-HT3 receptors.
Histamine activation of vestibular H1 receptors
Vestibular histamine is a different nausea pathway.
Acetylcholine activation of skeletal muscle nicotinic receptors
Neuromuscular transmission does not mediate this gut signal.
Dopamine activation of renal D1 receptors
Renal D1 signaling is unrelated to emetic afferents.
Gastrin activation of parietal-cell CCK-B receptors
Gastrin stimulates acid secretion rather than this neural emetic pathway.
