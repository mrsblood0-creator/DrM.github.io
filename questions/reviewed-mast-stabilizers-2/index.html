An attack already underway

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient prescribed inhaled cromolyn for preventive treatment arrives with acute wheezing and difficulty speaking. He says he has repeatedly used cromolyn because it is an asthma medicine. Why has this strategy not provided rapid relief?

  1. Cromolyn causes permanent beta-2 receptor blockade
  2. Every asthma medicine has an identical onset of effect
  3. Cromolyn does not reverse established bronchospasm as a rescue bronchodilator
  4. Cromolyn works only when swallowed during an attack
  5. Cromolyn must be mixed with another solution before every use
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Cromolyn does not reverse established bronchospasm as a rescue bronchodilator

An acute attack needs appropriate reliever treatment and assessment; preventive medication does not replace rescue care.

Understanding the options

Cromolyn does not reverse established bronchospasm as a rescue bronchodilator
An acute attack needs appropriate reliever treatment and assessment; preventive medication does not replace rescue care.
Cromolyn works only when swallowed during an attack
Changing to an oral route does not turn it into a rescue bronchodilator.
Cromolyn must be mixed with another solution before every use
Unverified mixing is not the explanation and may not be safe.
Cromolyn causes permanent beta-2 receptor blockade
It does not block beta-2 receptors.
Every asthma medicine has an identical onset of effect
Controller and reliever drugs differ in mechanism and onset.
