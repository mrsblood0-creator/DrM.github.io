Choosing antibiotics: 4

A patient remains febrile despite an antibiotic active against the cultured organism. Imaging shows a large undrained abscess. Which treatment principle addresses the persistent focus rather than simply adding progressively broader antibiotics?

  1. Narrow therapy using reliable microbiology
  2. Clarify the allergy reaction and timing
  3. Source control
  4. Drug penetration at the infection site
  5. Renal function and individualized dosing
Answer and explanation

Source control

An abscess, infected device or obstructed infected system may require drainage, removal or relief in addition to antibiotics.

Option explanations

Source control: An abscess, infected device or obstructed infected system may require drainage, removal or relief in addition to antibiotics.

Drug penetration at the infection site: An agent must reach effective concentrations where the infection is located; an in-vitro susceptible result alone is insufficient. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Renal function and individualized dosing: Renal impairment can alter antimicrobial exposure and require dose or interval adjustment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Narrow therapy using reliable microbiology: Once the pathogen and susceptibilities are known, an effective narrower regimen may reduce unnecessary exposure. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Clarify the allergy reaction and timing: A detailed allergy history distinguishes intolerance from immediate or severe delayed hypersensitivity and informs safe selection. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
