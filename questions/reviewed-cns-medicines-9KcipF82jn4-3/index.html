Antiparkinsonian drugs: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient experiences wearing-off before the next levodopa dose. A specialist adds a COMT inhibitor to prolong levodopa availability. Which listed adjunct acts through inhibition of catechol-O-methyltransferase rather than direct dopamine receptor stimulation?

  1. Pramipexole
  2. Entacapone
  3. Levodopa
  4. Carbidopa
  5. Selegiline
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Entacapone

Entacapone inhibits COMT and can extend the effect of a levodopa dose.

Understanding the options

Entacapone
Entacapone inhibits COMT and can extend the effect of a levodopa dose.
Levodopa
Levodopa crosses the blood-brain barrier and is converted to dopamine within the CNS. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Carbidopa
Carbidopa inhibits peripheral aromatic L-amino-acid decarboxylase, reducing peripheral levodopa conversion. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Selegiline
Selegiline inhibits MAO-B at usual Parkinson-treatment doses and reduces dopamine breakdown. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Pramipexole
Pramipexole directly stimulates dopamine receptors and can cause impulse-control disorders or sudden sleep episodes. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
