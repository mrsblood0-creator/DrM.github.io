Selective alpha blockers: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An older patient taking terazosin also receives another blood-pressure-lowering medicine and develops a fall soon after standing. Which class-related hemodynamic effect should be assessed when reviewing this new combination and the fall?

  1. Beta1 blockade reducing heart rate
  2. Orthostatic hypotension
  3. Intraoperative floppy iris syndrome
  4. Establish alpha blockade before adding beta blockade
  5. Beta2 blockade causing bronchoconstriction
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Orthostatic hypotension

Alpha1 blockade reduces the vasoconstrictor response to standing.

Understanding the options

Orthostatic hypotension
Alpha1 blockade reduces the vasoconstrictor response to standing.
Intraoperative floppy iris syndrome
Alpha1 antagonists, especially tamsulosin, are associated with floppy iris during cataract surgery. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Establish alpha blockade before adding beta blockade
In pheochromocytoma, beta blockade before adequate alpha blockade can worsen vasoconstriction. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Beta2 blockade causing bronchoconstriction
Nonselective beta blockers can aggravate bronchospasm in susceptible patients. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Beta1 blockade reducing heart rate
Cardiac beta1 blockade slows rate and can worsen bradycardia or conduction disease. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
