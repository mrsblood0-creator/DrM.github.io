Patient factors and drug response: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient taking a prescribed sedative regularly develops tremor and severe symptoms when it is abruptly stopped. The history does not establish compulsive use. Which physiological adaptation best explains the withdrawal reaction?

  1. Physical dependence
  2. Tolerance
  3. Rebound effect
  4. Reduced renal elimination
  5. Pharmacogenetic variation
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Physical dependence

Physiological adaptation can produce withdrawal when drug exposure is abruptly reduced.

Understanding the options

Physical dependence
Physiological adaptation can produce withdrawal when drug exposure is abruptly reduced.
Tolerance
A reduced response after repeated exposure may require more drug for the previous effect; it is not synonymous with addiction. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Rebound effect
Symptoms can temporarily exceed baseline after withdrawal of a suppressive treatment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Reduced renal elimination
Impaired kidney function can raise exposure to a predominantly renally eliminated drug. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Pharmacogenetic variation
Inherited differences in drug metabolism or targets can alter efficacy and toxicity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
