Inspiratory noise

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 35-year-old woman develops difficulty breathing shortly after a medication exposure. She has tongue swelling, a hoarse voice and a high-pitched sound that is loudest over the neck during inspiration. Which finding best explains this sound?

  1. Narrowing of the upper airway
  2. Diffuse lower-airway smooth-muscle constriction
  3. Fluid within alveoli
  4. Secretions within the oropharynx
  5. Vibration of relaxed pharyngeal soft tissue
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Narrowing of the upper airway

Inspiratory stridor with tongue swelling and voice change indicates upper-airway narrowing and possible deterioration.

Understanding the options

Narrowing of the upper airway
Inspiratory stridor with tongue swelling and voice change indicates upper-airway narrowing and possible deterioration.
Diffuse lower-airway smooth-muscle constriction
Bronchospasm typically produces expiratory wheezing over the chest; it does not best explain neck-predominant inspiratory stridor.
Fluid within alveoli
Alveolar fluid more often produces crackles and hypoxemia rather than this focal inspiratory sound.
Secretions within the oropharynx
Secretions produce wet gurgling rather than a high-pitched inspiratory sound.
Vibration of relaxed pharyngeal soft tissue
This typically produces low-pitched snoring rather than stridor.
