Proximal humerus and Neer classification: 5

Following a severe proximal humeral fracture, the humeral head later collapses despite initial alignment. The fracture pattern had threatened the head’s blood supply. Which complication best explains this delayed structural failure of the articular segment?

  1. Nonunion
  2. Post-traumatic osteoarthritis
  3. Acute compartment syndrome
  4. Osteonecrosis
  5. Joint stiffness
Answer and explanation

Osteonecrosis

Disruption of blood supply can lead to death and collapse of an injured bony segment.

Option explanations

Osteonecrosis: Disruption of blood supply can lead to death and collapse of an injured bony segment.

Joint stiffness: Capsular contracture, adhesions and prolonged immobilization can restrict motion after injury. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Nonunion: Persistent failure of fracture healing requires assessment of stability, biology, infection and patient factors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Post-traumatic osteoarthritis: Articular incongruity or cartilage damage can cause later degenerative joint changes. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Acute compartment syndrome: Rising compartment pressure impairs perfusion; escalating pain and pain on passive stretch require urgent assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
