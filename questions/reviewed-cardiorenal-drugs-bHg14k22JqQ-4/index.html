Statins: 4

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient stable on simvastatin develops muscle symptoms after a strong CYP3A4 inhibitor is prescribed. The statin dose was unchanged. Which pharmacokinetic explanation best accounts for the new toxicity after adding the other medicine?

  1. HMG-CoA reductase inhibition
  2. Increased hepatic LDL receptor expression
  3. Statin-associated muscle injury
  4. Reduction of atherosclerotic cardiovascular risk
  5. Increased statin exposure through a drug interaction
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Increased statin exposure through a drug interaction

CYP3A4 inhibitors can increase exposure to susceptible statins such as simvastatin.

Understanding the options

Increased statin exposure through a drug interaction
CYP3A4 inhibitors can increase exposure to susceptible statins such as simvastatin.
HMG-CoA reductase inhibition
Statins inhibit the rate-limiting enzyme in hepatic cholesterol synthesis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Increased hepatic LDL receptor expression
Reduced hepatic cholesterol promotes LDL receptor expression and circulating LDL clearance. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Statin-associated muscle injury
New muscle pain with marked CK elevation requires assessment for myopathy or rhabdomyolysis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Reduction of atherosclerotic cardiovascular risk
Appropriate statin therapy reduces cardiovascular events, not merely the laboratory LDL number. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
