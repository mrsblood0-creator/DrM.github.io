Suture materials: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A surgeon chooses a suture intended to lose strength and be broken down after temporary tissue support is no longer required. Which broad material category describes this behavior rather than permanent persistence of the thread?

  1. Monofilament construction
  2. Multifilament or braided construction
  3. More zeros indicate a smaller diameter in USP notation
  4. Absorbable suture
  5. Hydrolysis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Absorbable suture

Absorbable materials lose strength and are degraded over time; the rate depends on the product.

Understanding the options

Absorbable suture
Absorbable materials lose strength and are degraded over time; the rate depends on the product.
Hydrolysis
Many synthetic absorbable sutures degrade predominantly by hydrolysis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Monofilament construction
A single filament has different handling, memory and capillarity from braided material. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Multifilament or braided construction
Multiple fibers provide handling and knot characteristics but also interstices and capillarity that affect selection. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
More zeros indicate a smaller diameter in USP notation
For example, 5-0 is thinner than 3-0; size must still provide sufficient tissue support. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
