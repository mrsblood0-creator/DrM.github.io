Fracture displacement: 5

A radiograph shows fracture ends pulled apart with a visible gap between them. Their axes remain broadly aligned and there is no overlap. Which displacement term describes this separation of the opposing bone ends?

  1. Shortening from overlap
  2. Distraction
  3. Translation
  4. Angulation
  5. Rotation
Answer and explanation

Distraction

Distraction separates the fracture ends and creates a gap.

Option explanations

Distraction: Distraction separates the fracture ends and creates a gap.

Translation: Translation is sideways displacement of one fragment relative to the other. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Angulation: Angulation describes a change in the angle between fragment axes. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Rotation: Rotational displacement changes axial orientation and may require clinical assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Shortening from overlap: Overriding fragments reduce the effective length of the bone. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
