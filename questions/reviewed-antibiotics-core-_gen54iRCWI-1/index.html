Linezolid: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An adult with a susceptible vancomycin-resistant enterococcal infection receives linezolid. Unlike beta-lactams, the selected agent acts on the bacterial ribosome early in translation. Which molecular process does this drug directly inhibit?

  1. 50S initiation-complex formation
  2. Penicillin-binding proteins
  3. D-Ala-D-Ala termini
  4. 30S aminoacyl-tRNA attachment
  5. DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

50S initiation-complex formation

Linezolid binds 23S RNA within the 50S subunit and prevents functional initiation-complex formation.

Understanding the options

D-Ala-D-Ala termini

30S aminoacyl-tRNA attachment
Tetracyclines prevent aminoacyl-tRNA binding to the 30S subunit. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV
