Thompson-Epstein classification: 4

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

CT after a posterior hip dislocation shows a fracture involving the acetabular floor. The clinician is applying the Thompson-Epstein system to describe the associated bony damage. Which grade corresponds to acetabular-floor involvement?

  1. Thompson-Epstein III
  2. Thompson-Epstein V
  3. Thompson-Epstein IV
  4. Thompson-Epstein I
  5. Thompson-Epstein II
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Thompson-Epstein IV

Posterior dislocation is associated with fracture of the acetabular floor.

Understanding the options

Thompson-Epstein IV
Posterior dislocation is associated with fracture of the acetabular floor.
Thompson-Epstein I
Posterior hip dislocation has no significant acetabular fracture or only a minor fragment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Thompson-Epstein II
Posterior dislocation accompanies a single large posterior acetabular wall fragment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Thompson-Epstein III
Posterior dislocation accompanies a comminuted posterior acetabular wall fracture. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Thompson-Epstein V
Posterior dislocation is associated with femoral head fracture. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
