Dependence on intestinal bacteria

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A laboratory compares lactulose incubated with small-intestinal mucosal enzymes against lactulose incubated with colonic bacteria. The bacterial preparation generates organic acids; the mucosal preparation leaves most lactulose intact. Which property explains the drug’s site of action?

  1. Complete absorption followed by biliary resecretion
  2. Activation by gastric pepsin
  3. Conversion into a lipid-soluble prodrug in enterocytes
  4. Selective filtration into the colon by the kidney
  5. Limited digestion by human small-intestinal enzymes
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Limited digestion by human small-intestinal enzymes

Colonic bacterial metabolism produces the acids responsible for much of its local effect.

Understanding the options

Limited digestion by human small-intestinal enzymes
Colonic bacterial metabolism produces the acids responsible for much of its local effect.
Complete absorption followed by biliary resecretion
Extensive absorption and biliary recycling are not required.
Activation by gastric pepsin
Pepsin digests proteins rather than activating this disaccharide.
Conversion into a lipid-soluble prodrug in enterocytes
Lactulose is not activated through this pathway.
Selective filtration into the colon by the kidney
Renal filtration does not deliver drugs directly into the bowel lumen.
