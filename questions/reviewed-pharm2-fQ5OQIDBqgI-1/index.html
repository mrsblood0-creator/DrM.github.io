Clonus after an interaction

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 34-year-old woman taking sertraline begins tramadol after a dental procedure. Within a day she develops agitation, sweating, diarrhea and a temperature of 38.8 C. Examination shows brisk reflexes and inducible ankle clonus. Which diagnosis best explains the combined mental, autonomic and neuromuscular findings?

  1. Anticholinergic toxicity
  2. Opioid intoxication
  3. Uncomplicated panic attack
  4. Serotonin syndrome
  5. Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Serotonin syndrome

The acute serotonergic drug combination and clonus strongly support serotonin toxicity.

Understanding the options

Serotonin syndrome
The acute serotonergic drug combination and clonus strongly support serotonin toxicity.
Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
Dopamine blockade, marked generalized rigidity and a slower evolution would fit better.
Anticholinergic toxicity
Dry skin, urinary retention and reduced bowel activity are more typical than diaphoresis and diarrhea.
Opioid intoxication
Respiratory depression and reduced consciousness would be more typical.
Uncomplicated panic attack
Fever and objective clonus are not explained by a panic attack.
