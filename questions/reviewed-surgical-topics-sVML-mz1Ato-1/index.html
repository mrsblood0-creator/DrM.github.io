Surgical needle components: 1

During a skills session, a student identifies the needle region that first enters tissue and whose shape influences penetration and cutting. Which component is being described rather than the curved shaft or suture attachment?

  1. Needle point
  2. Needle body
  3. Swaged attachment
  4. Reverse-cutting needle
  5. Taper-point needle
Answer and explanation

Needle point

The point initiates tissue penetration and its geometry is selected for tissue characteristics.

Option explanations

Needle point: The point initiates tissue penetration and its geometry is selected for tissue characteristics.

Needle body: The body provides the shape and strength used to carry the needle through tissue. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Swaged attachment: A swaged needle is manufactured attached to the suture without a separate threaded eye. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Reverse-cutting needle: The main cutting edge lies on the convex curvature and is commonly used for skin. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Taper-point needle: A rounded taper separates rather than cuts tissue and is used for many easily penetrated soft tissues. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
