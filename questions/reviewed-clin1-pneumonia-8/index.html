Short-course treatment under a specified guideline

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 5-year-old in the United Kingdom has non-severe community-acquired pneumonia without underlying disease or complications. He is clinically stable and can take oral treatment. Under NICE NG250, what initial antibiotic duration is recommended for this group?

  1. Three days, extending treatment if clinical instability persists
  2. One day, extending only if the initial radiograph is abnormal
  3. Five days for every child in this age group
  4. Seven days regardless of clinical response
  5. Ten days, with extension until cough has completely resolved
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Three days, extending treatment if clinical instability persists

NICE recommends a three-day course for uncomplicated non-severe disease in children aged three months to eleven years, with clinical reassessment.

Understanding the options

Three days, extending treatment if clinical instability persists
NICE recommends a three-day course for uncomplicated non-severe disease in children aged three months to eleven years, with clinical reassessment.
One day, extending only if the initial radiograph is abnormal
A single day is not the recommended initial course for this specified group.
Five days for every child in this age group
Five days may apply in other age groups or circumstances; NICE specifies a shorter initial course for this uncomplicated group.
Seven days regardless of clinical response
A fixed seven-day course exceeds the recommended initial duration for the stated population.
Ten days, with extension until cough has completely resolved
Cough can outlast active infection and does not by itself justify this prolonged initial course.
