Quinupristin-dalfopristin: 5

A patient receiving a narrow-therapeutic-index CYP3A4 substrate starts quinupristin-dalfopristin. The pharmacist recommends careful interaction review because concentrations of the existing medicine may rise. Which property of the antibiotic combination explains this concern?

  1. Arthralgia and myalgia
  2. CYP3A4-mediated drug interactions
  3. Synergistic binding at the 50S ribosome
  4. Susceptible Enterococcus faecium
  5. Enterococcus faecalis
Answer and explanation

CYP3A4-mediated drug interactions

Quinupristin-dalfopristin inhibits CYP3A4 and can raise exposure to susceptible interacting medicines.

Option explanations

CYP3A4-mediated drug interactions: Quinupristin-dalfopristin inhibits CYP3A4 and can raise exposure to susceptible interacting medicines.

Synergistic binding at the 50S ribosome: Quinupristin and dalfopristin are streptogramins whose combined actions inhibit bacterial protein synthesis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Susceptible Enterococcus faecium: Quinupristin-dalfopristin has activity against selected E. faecium isolates but not dependable E. faecalis activity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Enterococcus faecalis: E. faecalis is intrinsically resistant to quinupristin-dalfopristin and must not be confused with susceptible E. faecium. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Arthralgia and myalgia: Joint and muscle pain can limit quinupristin-dalfopristin treatment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
