Fracture mechanisms: 3

An adolescent suddenly contracts a muscle during a jump, and a small bony fragment separates at its tendon attachment. There was no direct blow to the site. Which force mechanism best explains the fracture?

  1. Bending force
  2. Axial compression
  3. Fatigue fracture
  4. Traction avulsion
  5. Torsional loading
Answer and explanation

Traction avulsion

A forceful tendon or ligament pull can detach a bony attachment.

Option explanations

Traction avulsion: A forceful tendon or ligament pull can detach a bony attachment.

Torsional loading: Twisting forces commonly produce spiral shaft fractures. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Bending force: Bending creates tension on one side and compression on the other and may produce a wedge fragment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Axial compression: Axial loading can cause impaction or collapse in susceptible bone. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Fatigue fracture: Repetitive abnormal loading can fracture otherwise normal bone. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
