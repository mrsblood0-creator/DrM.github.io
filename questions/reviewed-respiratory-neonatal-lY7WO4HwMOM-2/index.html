Neonatal sepsis: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A neonatal team designs an empirical treatment protocol for suspected early-onset bacterial sepsis, using local resistance information. Which pair of organisms represents important perinatal pathogens that the protocol must consider rather than assuming adult community pathogens predominate?

  1. Lumbar puncture when indicated and safe after stabilization
  2. Group B streptococcus and Escherichia coli
  3. Maternal-to-infant pathogen transmission around birth
  4. Possible neonatal infection requiring prompt assessment
  5. Obtain blood cultures promptly and start indicated antibiotics without avoidable delay
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Group B streptococcus and Escherichia coli

These are important early-onset neonatal pathogens; local epidemiology and individual risk guide empirical therapy.
