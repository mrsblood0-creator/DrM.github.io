Interaction with valproate

A 30-year-old man whose seizures are treated with valproate is prescribed lamotrigine. His clinician selects a lower starting dose and slower escalation than for a patient taking neither drug. What is the main reason for this difference?

  1. Both drugs bind irreversibly to albumin
  2. Lamotrigine requires valproate for activation
  3. Valproate reduces lamotrigine glucuronidation
  4. Valproate accelerates lamotrigine urinary filtration
  5. Valproate prevents all intestinal absorption
Answer and explanation

Valproate reduces lamotrigine glucuronidation

Valproate inhibits lamotrigine clearance and raises exposure, increasing toxicity and rash risk.

Option explanations

Valproate reduces lamotrigine glucuronidation: Valproate inhibits lamotrigine clearance and raises exposure, increasing toxicity and rash risk.

Valproate accelerates lamotrigine urinary filtration: The important interaction decreases metabolic clearance.

Valproate prevents all intestinal absorption: Lamotrigine remains absorbed; exposure increases.

Both drugs bind irreversibly to albumin: Irreversible albumin binding is not the mechanism.

Lamotrigine requires valproate for activation: Lamotrigine is active without valproate.
