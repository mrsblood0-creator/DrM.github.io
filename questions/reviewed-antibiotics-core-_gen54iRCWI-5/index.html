ORIGINAL USMLE-STYLE PRACTICE

Linezolid: 5

After an extended linezolid course, a patient reports progressive tingling and burning in both feet. Examination shows reduced distal sensation without a focal root pattern. Which recognized toxicity should be considered during medication reassessment?

  1. Serotonin syndrome
  2. Peripheral neuropathy
  3. QT prolongation
  4. Clostridioides difficile colitis
  5. Ototoxicity
Answer and explanation

Peripheral neuropathy

Prolonged exposure to some antimicrobials, including metronidazole and linezolid, can cause sensory neuropathy.

Option explanations

Peripheral neuropathy: Prolonged exposure to some antimicrobials, including metronidazole and linezolid, can cause sensory neuropathy.

QT prolongation: Delayed repolarization increases torsades risk, especially with electrolyte disturbances or other QT-prolonging drugs. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Clostridioides difficile colitis: Antibiotic-associated disruption of intestinal flora can permit toxin-producing C. difficile to cause colitis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Ototoxicity: Aminoglycosides can injure cochlear or vestibular structures, causing hearing loss, tinnitus or imbalance. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Serotonin syndrome: Serotonergic interactions can produce agitation, autonomic instability, hyperreflexia and clonus. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
