ORIGINAL USMLE-STYLE PRACTICE

Metronidazole: 3

A traveler develops persistent diarrhea, bloating and malabsorption after drinking untreated water. Giardia is identified, and metronidazole is selected when appropriate. Which category of nonbacterial organisms is also susceptible to this medication?

  1. Anaerobic bacteria
  2. Atypical respiratory pathogens
  3. Resistant Gram-positive bacteria
  4. Aerobic Gram-negative bacilli
  5. Selected protozoal infections
Answer and explanation

Selected protozoal infections

Metronidazole has activity against organisms such as Giardia and Trichomonas.

Option explanations

Selected protozoal infections: Metronidazole has activity against organisms such as Giardia and Trichomonas.

Anaerobic bacteria: Metronidazole is active against many susceptible anaerobes; it does not replace aerobic coverage in a mixed infection. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Atypical respiratory pathogens: Macrolides and tetracyclines can cover organisms such as Mycoplasma that are not treated by cell-wall inhibitors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Resistant Gram-positive bacteria: Linezolid and vancomycin are used for selected resistant Gram-positive infections, depending on susceptibility and site. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Aerobic Gram-negative bacilli: Aminoglycosides are useful against selected aerobic Gram-negative organisms; oxygen-dependent uptake limits anaerobic activity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
