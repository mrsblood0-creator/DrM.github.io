Metronidazole: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An abdominal abscess is drained, and cultures show both anaerobic and aerobic organisms. The patient is receiving metronidazole alone. Which principle should guide adjustment of the regimen rather than assuming all isolated organisms are covered?

  1. Stop the suspected drug and assess the adverse reaction
  2. Separate dosing from polyvalent-cation products
  3. Use culture and susceptibility results to refine treatment
  4. Monitor the complete blood count
  5. Monitor renal function and drug exposure
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Use culture and susceptibility results to refine treatment

Definitive antimicrobial choice should reflect the organism, susceptibility, infection site and patient factors.
