Iron sequestration and oxidative injury

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A cell exposed to excess available iron increases production of an iron-storage protein. The protein packages iron within a mineral core, limiting the readily reactive intracellular iron pool. Which protein provides this storage function?

  1. Ferritin
  2. Transferrin receptor
  3. Albumin
  4. Ubiquitin
  5. Collagen
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Ferritin

Ferritin sequesters iron in a storage complex, limiting freely available iron.

Understanding the options

Ferritin
Ferritin sequesters iron in a storage complex, limiting freely available iron.
Transferrin receptor
The receptor mediates iron uptake rather than forming the intracellular storage core.
Albumin
Albumin is not the principal intracellular iron-storage protein.
Ubiquitin
Ubiquitin marks proteins for regulatory or degradative pathways.
Collagen
Collagen is a structural extracellular-matrix protein.
