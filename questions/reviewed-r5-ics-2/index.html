Inflammation rather than immediate relaxation

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient says her corticosteroid inhaler must be ineffective because it does not produce the same immediate sensation as a bronchodilator. Which explanation best describes the main purpose of the corticosteroid component of her asthma regimen?

  1. Immediate neutralization of all inhaled allergens
  2. Replacement of oxygen in the alveoli
  3. Suppression of airway inflammation and reduction of exacerbation risk
  4. Direct beta-2 receptor stimulation within seconds
  5. Competitive M3 receptor antagonism
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Suppression of airway inflammation and reduction of exacerbation risk

ICS-containing treatment addresses the inflammatory component of asthma.

Understanding the options

Suppression of airway inflammation and reduction of exacerbation risk
ICS-containing treatment addresses the inflammatory component of asthma.
Direct beta-2 receptor stimulation within seconds
This is the role of a beta agonist.
Competitive M3 receptor antagonism
This is the action of antimuscarinic bronchodilators.
Immediate neutralization of all inhaled allergens
ICS does not physically neutralize allergens.
Replacement of oxygen in the alveoli
ICS is not supplemental oxygen.
