Joint injury terminology: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

After shoulder trauma, imaging shows partial loss of joint congruity while some contact between the humeral head and glenoid remains. Which term distinguishes this alignment abnormality from a complete separation of the joint surfaces?

  1. Subluxation
  2. Ligament sprain
  3. Muscle or tendon strain
  4. Dislocation
  5. Hemarthrosis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Subluxation

Subluxation is partial loss of joint congruity with some articular contact maintained.

Understanding the options

Subluxation
Subluxation is partial loss of joint congruity with some articular contact maintained.
Ligament sprain
A sprain injures a ligament supporting a joint. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Muscle or tendon strain
A strain injures a muscle-tendon unit rather than a ligament. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Dislocation
Dislocation is complete loss of normal joint congruity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Hemarthrosis
Blood within a joint produces an effusion after injuries such as ligament disruption or intra-articular fracture. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
