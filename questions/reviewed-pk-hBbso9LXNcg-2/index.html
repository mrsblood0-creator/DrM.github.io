A fixed elimination rate

A toxicology teaching model describes removal of a hypothetical drug at a constant rate of 10 mg/hour while the amount falls from 200 mg to 100 mg. There is no further input. How much time does that decrease require in this zero-order range?

  1. 5 hours
  2. 20 hours
  3. 2 hours
  4. The time is independent of the initial amount for every zero-order drug
  5. 10 hours
Answer and explanation

10 hours

Removing 100 mg at 10 mg/hour takes 10 hours; the calculation uses a fixed amount rate.

Option explanations

10 hours: Removing 100 mg at 10 mg/hour takes 10 hours; the calculation uses a fixed amount rate.

5 hours: Only 50 mg would be removed in five hours at the stated rate.

20 hours: Twenty hours would remove 200 mg if the same model remained valid.

2 hours: Only 20 mg would be removed in two hours.

The time is independent of the initial amount for every zero-order drug: The time to remove a specified fraction depends on the starting amount in zero-order kinetics.
