Adaptation to pressure overload

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with longstanding aortic stenosis develops a thickened left ventricular wall. Biopsy shows enlarged cardiomyocytes with increased contractile-protein content rather than a substantial increase in the number of cardiomyocytes. Which adaptation accounts for the finding?

  1. Hypertrophy
  2. Hyperplasia
  3. Metaplasia
  4. Atrophy
  5. Dysplasia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Hypertrophy

Pressure-loaded cardiomyocytes adapt predominantly by increasing cell size.

Understanding the options

Hypertrophy
Pressure-loaded cardiomyocytes adapt predominantly by increasing cell size.
Hyperplasia
Hyperplasia is increased cell number.
Metaplasia
Metaplasia replaces one differentiated cell type with another.
Atrophy
Atrophy decreases cell size or tissue mass.
Dysplasia
Dysplasia concerns disordered growth and cellular atypia.
