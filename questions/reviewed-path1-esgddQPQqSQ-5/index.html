Adaptation to pressure overload

A patient with longstanding aortic stenosis develops a thickened left ventricular wall. Biopsy shows enlarged cardiomyocytes with increased contractile-protein content rather than a substantial increase in the number of cardiomyocytes. Which adaptation accounts for the finding?

  1. Hypertrophy
  2. Hyperplasia
  3. Metaplasia
  4. Atrophy
  5. Dysplasia
Answer and explanation

Hypertrophy

Pressure-loaded cardiomyocytes adapt predominantly by increasing cell size.

Option explanations

Hypertrophy: Pressure-loaded cardiomyocytes adapt predominantly by increasing cell size.

Hyperplasia: Hyperplasia is increased cell number.

Metaplasia: Metaplasia replaces one differentiated cell type with another.

Atrophy: Atrophy decreases cell size or tissue mass.

Dysplasia: Dysplasia concerns disordered growth and cellular atypia.
