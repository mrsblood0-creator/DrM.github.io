Gastroschisis: 4

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

The bowel cannot be safely returned into a newborn’s small abdominal cavity immediately without excessive pressure. Which treatment principle supports gradual reduction and closure rather than forcing a one-stage reduction regardless of physiology?

  1. Protect exposed or vulnerable viscera and prevent heat and fluid loss
  2. Staged reduction when immediate closure would be unsafe
  3. Omphalocele
  4. Gastroschisis
  5. Assess associated structural and chromosomal abnormalities
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Staged reduction when immediate closure would be unsafe

Forcing large volumes of viscera into a small abdominal cavity can compromise ventilation and circulation.

Understanding the options
