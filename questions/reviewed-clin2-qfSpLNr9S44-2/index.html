Pressure support

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with acute hypercapnic respiratory failure receives bilevel NIV with an inspiratory pressure of 14 cm H2O and an expiratory pressure of 6 cm H2O. The clinician discusses the assistance delivered during inspiration. What is the pressure-support difference at these settings?

  1. 6 cm H2O
  2. 14 cm H2O
  3. 20 cm H2O
  4. 2.3 cm H2O
  5. 8 cm H2O
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

8 cm H2O

Inspiratory pressure support is IPAP minus EPAP: 14 minus 6 equals 8 cm H2O.

Understanding the options

8 cm H2O
Inspiratory pressure support is IPAP minus EPAP: 14 minus 6 equals 8 cm H2O.
6 cm H2O
This is the expiratory pressure, not the inspiratory-expiratory difference.
14 cm H2O
This is the absolute inspiratory pressure, not pressure support above EPAP.
20 cm H2O
Adding the two values does not calculate pressure support.
2.3 cm H2O
The ratio of the pressures is not the pressure-support difference.
