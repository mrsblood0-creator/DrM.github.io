Unexpected opioid toxicity

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 26-year-old adult becomes profoundly sleepy with a respiratory rate of 7/min after prescribed tramadol doses. Emergency ventilatory support is started. Testing later identifies multiple functional copies of CYP2D6. Which finding best explains the unexpectedly strong opioid effect?

  1. Accelerated renal removal of M1
  2. Blockade of mu receptors by tramadol
  3. Reduced absorption of the administered dose
  4. Rapid formation of the active M1 metabolite
  5. Failure to form any active metabolite
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Rapid formation of the active M1 metabolite

CYP2D6 ultrarapid metabolism can increase active-metabolite exposure and respiratory toxicity.

Understanding the options

Rapid formation of the active M1 metabolite
CYP2D6 ultrarapid metabolism can increase active-metabolite exposure and respiratory toxicity.
Failure to form any active metabolite
This does not explain enhanced metabolite-mediated opioid effects.
Accelerated renal removal of M1
More rapid removal would reduce exposure.
Blockade of mu receptors by tramadol
Opioid antagonism would not explain this toxidrome.
Reduced absorption of the administered dose
Reduced absorption would generally lower exposure.
