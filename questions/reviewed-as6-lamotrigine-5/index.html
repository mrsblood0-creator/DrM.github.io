Restarting after an interruption

A 28-year-old man stopped lamotrigine several weeks ago after losing his prescription. He now has a new supply and asks whether he should immediately resume his previous maintenance dose. Which recommendation best addresses the main medication-specific concern?

  1. Resume the full dose and add a second dose that evening
  2. Crush the full weekly dose into one drink
  3. Use the previous dose only when a seizure starts
  4. Take an antihistamine to make rapid restarting safe
  5. Contact the prescriber to restart with an appropriate titration
Answer and explanation

Contact the prescriber to restart with an appropriate titration

After a prolonged interruption, restarting at a high dose can increase serious-rash risk; initial titration may be needed.

Option explanations

Contact the prescriber to restart with an appropriate titration: After a prolonged interruption, restarting at a high dose can increase serious-rash risk; initial titration may be needed.

Resume the full dose and add a second dose that evening: High-dose restarting increases toxicity risk.

Crush the full weekly dose into one drink: This causes excessive exposure.

Use the previous dose only when a seizure starts: Lamotrigine is not an as-needed rescue drug.

Take an antihistamine to make rapid restarting safe: An antihistamine does not prevent serious lamotrigine reactions.
