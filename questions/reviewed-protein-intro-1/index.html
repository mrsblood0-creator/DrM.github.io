A small-subunit antibiotic

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A laboratory compares the targets of two antibiotics used for a susceptible bacterial infection. One is doxycycline; the other is a beta-lactam. The investigator measures inhibition of bacterial translation after doxycycline exposure. Which target best explains this effect?

  1. DNA gyrase
  2. Dihydrofolate reductase
  3. The bacterial 50S ribosomal subunit
  4. The bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit
  5. Penicillin-binding proteins
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

The bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit

Doxycycline is a tetracycline that inhibits protein synthesis through the bacterial small subunit.

Understanding the options

The bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit
Doxycycline is a tetracycline that inhibits protein synthesis through the bacterial small subunit.
Penicillin-binding proteins
These are the principal targets of beta-lactams, not doxycycline.
DNA gyrase
This is a target of fluoroquinolones.
Dihydrofolate reductase
This is the target of trimethoprim.
The bacterial 50S ribosomal subunit
Several other antibiotic classes act at 50S; doxycycline is a 30S agent.
