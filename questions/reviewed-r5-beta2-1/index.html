A patient’s bronchospasm improves rapidly after inhaled albuterol. In airway smooth muscle, the activated beta-2 receptor couples to a G protein that increases a second messenger. Which signaling change best explains this bronchodilator effect?

  1. Direct inhibition of all glucocorticoid receptors
  2. Activation of M3 receptors by acetylcholine
  3. Covalent inhibition of cyclooxygenase
  4. Increased adenylyl cyclase activity and cAMP
  5. Inhibition of adenylyl cyclase through Gi
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Increased adenylyl cyclase activity and cAMP

Beta-2 receptors signal through Gs to increase cAMP and promote smooth-muscle relaxation.

Understanding the options

Increased adenylyl cyclase activity and cAMP
Beta-2 receptors signal through Gs to increase cAMP and promote smooth-muscle relaxation.
Inhibition of adenylyl cyclase through Gi
This is not the characteristic beta-2 pathway.
Direct inhibition of all glucocorticoid receptors
This does not explain bronchodilation.
Activation of M3 receptors by acetylcholine
This favors bronchoconstriction.
Covalent inhibition of cyclooxygenase
This is an aspirin mechanism, not albuterol signaling.
