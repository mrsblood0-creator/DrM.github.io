Empiric treatment and resistance

A treatment-naive adult in the United States has confirmed H. pylori infection. Antibiotic susceptibility is unknown, and there are no contraindications to the standard components. Under the 2024 ACG approach, which empiric regimen is a recommended option?

  1. Amoxicillin alone without acid suppression
  2. Optimized bismuth quadruple therapy for 14 days
  3. Clarithromycin triple therapy without susceptibility evidence
  4. A PPI alone for 14 days
  5. Bismuth alone for two days
Answer and explanation

Optimized bismuth quadruple therapy for 14 days

This is a recommended empiric regimen when susceptibility is unknown; local guidance and individual factors still matter.

Option explanations

Optimized bismuth quadruple therapy for 14 days: This is a recommended empiric regimen when susceptibility is unknown; local guidance and individual factors still matter.

Clarithromycin triple therapy without susceptibility evidence: Empiric clarithromycin triple therapy is discouraged under this guidance.

A PPI alone for 14 days: Acid suppression alone does not eradicate the infection.

Bismuth alone for two days: This is not an eradication regimen.

Amoxicillin alone without acid suppression: This is not the recommended regimen described.
