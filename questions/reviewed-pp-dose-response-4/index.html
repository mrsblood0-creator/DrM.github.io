Therapeutic index

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

In a population study, the median effective dose of a drug is 5 mg and the median toxic dose is 50 mg for a defined toxicity. Using TD50 divided by ED50, what is the drug’s therapeutic index?

  1. 0.1
  2. 5
  3. 45
  4. 250
  5. 10
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

10

Therapeutic index = 50/5 = 10.

Understanding the options

5
This gives ED50 alone.
10
Therapeutic index = 50/5 = 10.
45
This subtracts rather than divides.
250
This multiplies rather than divides.
0.1
This reverses the ratio.
