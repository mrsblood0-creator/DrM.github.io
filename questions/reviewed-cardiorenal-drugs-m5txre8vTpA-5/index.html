Thiazide diuretics: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient taking hydrochlorothiazide develops muscle cramps and a potassium concentration below the reference range. Which recognized electrolyte effect of the medication best accounts for these findings through increased distal potassium secretion?

  1. Metabolic acidosis from bicarbonate loss
  2. Contraction-associated metabolic alkalosis
  3. Hypokalemia
  4. Hyperkalemia
  5. Hyponatremia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Hypokalemia

Loop and thiazide diuretics can increase distal potassium loss.

Understanding the options

Hypokalemia
Loop and thiazide diuretics can increase distal potassium loss.
Hyperkalemia
Potassium-sparing diuretics can raise potassium, especially with renal impairment or other potassium-raising medicines. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Hyponatremia
Thiazides can impair urinary dilution and cause clinically important hyponatremia. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Metabolic acidosis from bicarbonate loss
Acetazolamide promotes bicarbonaturia and can cause hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Contraction-associated metabolic alkalosis
Volume and chloride depletion with loop or thiazide treatment can promote metabolic alkalosis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
