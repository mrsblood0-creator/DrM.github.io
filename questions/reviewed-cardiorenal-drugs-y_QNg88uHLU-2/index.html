Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

During acetazolamide treatment, serum bicarbonate falls and chloride rises without a raised anion gap. Which acid-base effect best explains the laboratory pattern through the medication’s renal action on bicarbonate reabsorption in this patient?

  1. Hyperkalemia
  2. Hyponatremia
  3. Contraction-associated metabolic alkalosis
  4. Metabolic acidosis from bicarbonate loss
  5. Hypokalemia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Metabolic acidosis from bicarbonate loss

Acetazolamide promotes bicarbonaturia and can cause hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis.
