Chlorpromazine: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

After chlorpromazine initiation, a patient develops dry mouth, constipation and difficulty urinating. These symptoms occur together without an obstructing lesion. Which receptor blockade most directly explains this characteristic cluster of autonomic adverse effects?

  1. GABA-A positive allosteric modulation
  2. Orexin receptor antagonism
  3. Melatonin receptor signaling
  4. 5-HT1A partial agonism
  5. Muscarinic receptor blockade
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Muscarinic receptor blockade

Antimuscarinic effects include dry mouth, constipation, blurred vision and urinary retention.

Understanding the options

Muscarinic receptor blockade
Antimuscarinic effects include dry mouth, constipation, blurred vision and urinary retention.
GABA-A positive allosteric modulation
Benzodiazepines and Z-drugs enhance GABA-A-mediated inhibition through the benzodiazepine site. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Orexin receptor antagonism
Suvorexant blocks wake-promoting orexin signaling at OX1 and OX2 receptors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Melatonin receptor signaling
Melatonin acts on circadian signaling, including MT1 and MT2 receptors, rather than acting as a benzodiazepine. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
5-HT1A partial agonism
Buspirone’s anxiolytic action involves serotonergic modulation, especially partial agonism at 5-HT1A receptors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
