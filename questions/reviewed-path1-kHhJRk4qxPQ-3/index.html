Why ischemia is more damaging than hypoxemia

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Two cell cultures receive equally little oxygen. One continues to receive glucose with removal of metabolites; the other has no perfusion. The nonperfused culture dies sooner. Which additional consequence best explains the difference?

  1. Selective activation of collagen production
  2. Increased circulating hemoglobin
  3. Immediate conversion of all cells into adipocytes
  4. Loss of nutrient delivery and waste removal
  5. Higher oxygen saturation
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Loss of nutrient delivery and waste removal

Ischemia restricts both oxygen and substrate delivery and prevents clearance of metabolic products, limiting anaerobic compensation.

Understanding the options

Loss of nutrient delivery and waste removal
Ischemia restricts both oxygen and substrate delivery and prevents clearance of metabolic products, limiting anaerobic compensation.
Higher oxygen saturation
Higher saturation would not explain injury under absent perfusion.
Selective activation of collagen production
Collagen production is not the immediate metabolic consequence.
Increased circulating hemoglobin
Hemoglobin changes cannot compensate for absent flow to the culture.
Immediate conversion of all cells into adipocytes
Adipocyte conversion is not an acute response to ischemia.
