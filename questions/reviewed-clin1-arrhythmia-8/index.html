Shockable pediatric arrest

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A child abruptly collapses during sports and is unresponsive with no palpable central pulse. The monitor shows rapid, regular, wide QRS complexes. High-quality CPR is underway and a defibrillator is available. Which rhythm-specific intervention is required?

  1. Unsynchronized defibrillation
  2. Synchronized cardioversion
  3. Transcutaneous pacing
  4. Adenosine as the initial rhythm intervention
  5. Amiodarone before the first shock
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Unsynchronized defibrillation

Pulseless ventricular tachycardia is a shockable cardiac-arrest rhythm.

Understanding the options

Unsynchronized defibrillation
Pulseless ventricular tachycardia is a shockable cardiac-arrest rhythm.
Synchronized cardioversion
Synchronization is used for selected tachyarrhythmias with a pulse; pulseless VT requires defibrillation.
Transcutaneous pacing
Pacing is not effective treatment for pulseless ventricular tachycardia.
Adenosine as the initial rhythm intervention
Adenosine is not the primary treatment for a pulseless ventricular rhythm.
Amiodarone before the first shock
Antiarrhythmics may be used for shock-refractory arrest, but should not delay early defibrillation.
