Administration routes: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A medicine is delivered directly into a vein, so no absorption barrier separates administration from systemic circulation. Which route provides complete systemic bioavailability by definition, unlike routes that require absorption from a separate site?

  1. Intravenous administration
  2. Oral administration
  3. Sublingual administration
  4. Inhaled administration
  5. Transdermal administration
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Intravenous administration

IV dosing delivers drug directly into systemic circulation with complete bioavailability.

Understanding the options

Intravenous administration
IV dosing delivers drug directly into systemic circulation with complete bioavailability.
Oral administration
Oral drugs require gastrointestinal absorption and may undergo first-pass metabolism. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Sublingual administration
Absorption through sublingual mucosa can provide rapid systemic delivery while initially bypassing portal first-pass metabolism. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Inhaled administration
Inhalation can target airways and reduce the dose required for a local pulmonary effect. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Transdermal administration
Transdermal delivery can provide sustained systemic exposure; absorption depends on formulation and skin factors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
