A rash during initiation

A 22-year-old woman develops a spreading rash and painful oral erosions two weeks after beginning lamotrigine. The dose was increased faster than prescribed. She has fever but no respiratory symptoms. Which response is most appropriate?

  1. Increase lamotrigine to suppress the reaction
  2. Treat only with a moisturizer and continue unchanged
  3. Restart at twice the dose after one missed tablet
  4. Stop lamotrigine and obtain urgent medical assessment
  5. Continue until the rash covers the whole body
Answer and explanation

Stop lamotrigine and obtain urgent medical assessment

Mucosal lesions and systemic symptoms raise concern for a serious cutaneous reaction; rapid escalation increases risk.

Option explanations

Stop lamotrigine and obtain urgent medical assessment: Mucosal lesions and systemic symptoms raise concern for a serious cutaneous reaction; rapid escalation increases risk.

Continue until the rash covers the whole body: Waiting risks progression of a severe reaction.

Increase lamotrigine to suppress the reaction: Higher exposure does not treat this adverse effect.

Treat only with a moisturizer and continue unchanged: Mucosal lesions and fever require urgent evaluation.

Restart at twice the dose after one missed tablet: Re-exposure and high doses may worsen the reaction.
