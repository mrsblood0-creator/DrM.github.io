Improving the seal

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An unconscious adult with a palpable pulse requires bag-mask ventilation. One rescuer is holding the mask and squeezing the bag, but air leaks around the cheeks and chest rise is poor. A second trained rescuer arrives. Which change is most likely to improve ventilation immediately?

  1. Increase the rate to 40 breaths per minute
  2. Remove the oxygen supply to reduce resistance
  3. Use a two-handed mask seal and jaw lift while the second rescuer squeezes the bag
  4. Squeeze the bag harder without correcting the leak
  5. Remove all airway positioning support
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Use a two-handed mask seal and jaw lift while the second rescuer squeezes the bag

Separating mask holding from bag compression improves the seal and airway opening.

Understanding the options

Use a two-handed mask seal and jaw lift while the second rescuer squeezes the bag
Separating mask holding from bag compression improves the seal and airway opening.
Squeeze the bag harder without correcting the leak
Greater pressure does not correct poor positioning and can increase gastric inflation.
Remove all airway positioning support
Loss of airway opening may worsen obstruction.
Increase the rate to 40 breaths per minute
Increasing rate does not fix ineffective tidal delivery and risks overventilation.
Remove the oxygen supply to reduce resistance
This does not address the mask leak or airway position.
