Metabolic shift in a threatened myocardium

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A coronary artery is briefly occluded during an experimental study. Before extensive membrane rupture develops, cardiomyocytes accumulate lactate, consume glycogen stores, and show a fall in intracellular pH. Which metabolic response accounts for these early findings?

  1. Increased ketone oxidation in mitochondria
  2. Suppression of glucose utilization
  3. Increased reliance on anaerobic glycolysis
  4. Accelerated fatty-acid oxidation
  5. Increased oxidative phosphorylation
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Increased reliance on anaerobic glycolysis

Reduced oxygen availability impairs oxidative ATP production, increasing reliance on glycolysis and lactate formation.

Understanding the options

Increased reliance on anaerobic glycolysis
Reduced oxygen availability impairs oxidative ATP production, increasing reliance on glycolysis and lactate formation.
Accelerated fatty-acid oxidation
Fatty-acid oxidation depends on mitochondrial oxidative metabolism.
Increased oxidative phosphorylation
Oxidative phosphorylation decreases when oxygen delivery is inadequate.
Increased ketone oxidation in mitochondria
Ketone oxidation still requires functioning oxidative metabolism.
Suppression of glucose utilization
Suppressing glucose use would not explain lactate accumulation.
