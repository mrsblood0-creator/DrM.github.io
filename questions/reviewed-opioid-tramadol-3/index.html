Seizure risk during analgesic selection

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 47-year-old man with epilepsy and a recent breakthrough seizure needs treatment for persistent pain. His clinician considers tramadol but reviews its neurologic adverse effects before prescribing. Which concern is especially relevant to this patient’s history?

  1. Tramadol selectively abolishes absence seizures
  2. Tramadol causes seizures only after years of use
  3. Tramadol can provoke seizures
  4. Tramadol reliably protects against focal seizures
  5. Tramadol prevents seizures through dopamine blockade
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Tramadol can provoke seizures

Seizure risk is increased in patients with epilepsy or other seizure predispositions.

Understanding the options

Tramadol can provoke seizures
Seizure risk is increased in patients with epilepsy or other seizure predispositions.
Tramadol reliably protects against focal seizures
It is not an antiseizure medication.
Tramadol prevents seizures through dopamine blockade
Dopamine blockade is not its therapeutic mechanism.
Tramadol selectively abolishes absence seizures
This describes neither its indication nor its action.
Tramadol causes seizures only after years of use
Seizures are not restricted to long-term exposure.
