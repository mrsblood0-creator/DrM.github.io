ORIGINAL USMLE-STYLE PRACTICE

Interaction after an antibiotic

A patient on stable warfarin begins a medicine that inhibits its metabolism. Several days later, bruising increases and INR rises substantially above target. Which mechanism most directly explains the change in anticoagulant intensity?

  1. Reduced clearance increases warfarin exposure
  2. Increased renal elimination removes active drug
  3. Enhanced vitamin K intake overcomes antagonism
  4. Direct conversion of warfarin into heparin
  5. Loss of drug absorption prevents anticoagulation
Answer and explanation

Reduced clearance increases warfarin exposure

Metabolic inhibition can raise warfarin exposure and bleeding risk.

Option explanations

Reduced clearance increases warfarin exposure: Metabolic inhibition can raise warfarin exposure and bleeding risk.

Increased renal elimination removes active drug: Faster elimination would tend to reduce exposure.

Enhanced vitamin K intake overcomes antagonism: More vitamin K generally reduces INR response.

Direct conversion of warfarin into heparin: Warfarin is not converted into heparin.

Loss of drug absorption prevents anticoagulation: Reduced absorption would not explain the increased INR.
