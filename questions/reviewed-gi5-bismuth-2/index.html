A salicylate precaution

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A parent wants to give bismuth subsalicylate to a 12-year-old recovering from influenza. The pharmacist advises against the product. Which serious condition underlies this warning for salicylate-containing products in this context?

  1. Reye syndrome
  2. Kawasaki disease caused by every salicylate dose
  3. Cystic fibrosis
  4. Intussusception as the specific salicylate warning
  5. Congenital pyloric stenosis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Reye syndrome

Children and teenagers with or recovering from flu-like illness or chickenpox should avoid this product.

Understanding the options

Reye syndrome
Children and teenagers with or recovering from flu-like illness or chickenpox should avoid this product.
Kawasaki disease caused by every salicylate dose
This is not the relevant labeled warning.
Cystic fibrosis
This inherited disease is not caused by the medication.
Intussusception as the specific salicylate warning
That is not the warning described.
Congenital pyloric stenosis
This does not explain the concern in an adolescent.
