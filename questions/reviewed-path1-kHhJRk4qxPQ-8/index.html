Enzyme release after muscle injury

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient is rescued after a limb was compressed beneath debris for several hours. The injured muscles are swollen, and serum creatine kinase rises markedly. Which cellular event most directly allows this normally intracellular enzyme to appear in blood?

  1. Increased rough-ER ribosome attachment
  2. Conversion of muscle fibers to collagen
  3. Physiologic enlargement of intact myocytes
  4. Loss of plasma-membrane integrity
  5. Increased lysosomal acidity alone
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Loss of plasma-membrane integrity

Membrane disruption permits intracellular proteins to escape into extracellular fluid and circulation.

Understanding the options

Loss of plasma-membrane integrity
Membrane disruption permits intracellular proteins to escape into extracellular fluid and circulation.
Increased lysosomal acidity alone
Lysosomal acidity alone does not provide an exit across the plasma membrane.
Increased rough-ER ribosome attachment
Ribosome attachment affects synthesis rather than leakage.
Conversion of muscle fibers to collagen
Fibrosis is a later replacement process.
Physiologic enlargement of intact myocytes
Intact hypertrophied cells do not release large quantities of cytosolic enzymes simply because they are larger.
