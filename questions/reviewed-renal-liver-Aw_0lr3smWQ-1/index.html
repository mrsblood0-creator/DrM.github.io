Alcohol-associated hepatitis: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with sustained heavy alcohol exposure develops recent jaundice and systemic illness. Other causes of liver injury are being assessed. Which clinical syndrome is suggested by this history but still requires careful diagnostic evaluation?

  1. Alcohol-associated hepatitis
  2. Supportive but not diagnostic AST predominance
  3. Support sustained alcohol abstinence
  4. Assess and treat malnutrition
  5. Evaluate infection and treatment contraindications
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Alcohol-associated hepatitis

Recent jaundice after sustained heavy alcohol exposure can suggest alcohol-associated hepatitis, but competing causes must be assessed.
