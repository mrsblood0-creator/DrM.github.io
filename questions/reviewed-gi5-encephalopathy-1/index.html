Finding a precipitant

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 58-year-old man with cirrhosis develops confusion and asterixis after several days of constipation. Glucose is normal and there is no focal weakness. Alongside evaluating other causes of altered consciousness, which factor is a plausible precipitant of hepatic encephalopathy?

  1. Resolution of an infection
  2. Constipation
  3. Improved adherence to prescribed lactulose
  4. Successful control of gastrointestinal bleeding
  5. Correction of hypokalemia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Constipation

Reduced intestinal clearance of nitrogenous material can precipitate HE in a susceptible patient.

Understanding the options

Constipation
Reduced intestinal clearance of nitrogenous material can precipitate HE in a susceptible patient.
Improved adherence to prescribed lactulose
Appropriate lactulose use generally helps prevent recurrence.
Successful control of gastrointestinal bleeding
Controlling bleeding removes a potential precipitant.
Correction of hypokalemia
Correcting an electrolyte trigger generally reduces risk.
Resolution of an infection
Treating infection removes another common trigger.
