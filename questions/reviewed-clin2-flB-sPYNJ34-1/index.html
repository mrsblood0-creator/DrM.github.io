An irregular pulse in a healthy student

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A healthy 19-year-old has an irregular pulse during a routine examination. ECG shows identical upright P waves before every narrow QRS and a stable PR interval. The R-R interval shortens during inspiration and lengthens during expiration. He has no symptoms. Which diagnosis best fits?

  1. Respiratory sinus arrhythmia
  2. Atrial fibrillation
  3. Mobitz type I AV block
  4. Premature atrial contractions
  5. Complete heart block
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Respiratory sinus arrhythmia

Sinus activation is preserved while the cycle length varies with breathing, a common physiologic pattern in young people.

Understanding the options

Respiratory sinus arrhythmia
Sinus activation is preserved while the cycle length varies with breathing, a common physiologic pattern in young people.
Atrial fibrillation
Consistent sinus P waves and respiratory-linked variation argue against atrial fibrillation.
Mobitz type I AV block
Wenckebach would show progressive PR prolongation and a dropped QRS.
Premature atrial contractions
PACs are premature beats, often with altered P-wave morphology, rather than a smooth breathing-linked pattern.
Complete heart block
Complete block produces AV dissociation, not consistent conduction of every sinus P wave.
