Possible neck injury

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An unconscious patient is found after a motor vehicle collision. Snoring suggests upper-airway obstruction, and cervical injury is possible. A trained clinician begins a manual airway-opening maneuver while another supports the head. Which maneuver is preferred initially?

  1. Jaw thrust without head extension
  2. Forceful neck hyperextension
  3. Pressing the soft tissues beneath the chin
  4. Flexing the neck until the chin touches the chest
  5. Waiting for imaging before opening the airway
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Jaw thrust without head extension

Jaw thrust opens the airway while limiting cervical movement compared with head tilt.

Understanding the options

Jaw thrust without head extension
Jaw thrust opens the airway while limiting cervical movement compared with head tilt.
Forceful neck hyperextension
This needlessly increases neck movement in suspected injury.
Pressing the soft tissues beneath the chin
This can worsen airway obstruction rather than lifting the mandible.
Flexing the neck until the chin touches the chest
This can narrow the airway and move a potentially injured spine.
Waiting for imaging before opening the airway
Airway obstruction requires immediate treatment.
