Persistent mitochondrial failure after reperfusion

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

In an experiment, ischemic cells are reoxygenated and supplied with glucose. One group recovers, while another has persistent mitochondrial dysfunction and extensive membrane damage despite restored conditions. Which interpretation best describes the second group?

  1. The injury has crossed into an irreversible state
  2. All changes are necessarily reversible because oxygen returned
  3. The cells have undergone physiologic hypertrophy
  4. The findings establish an exclusively apoptotic mechanism
  5. ATP depletion never contributed to the injury
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

The injury has crossed into an irreversible state

Persistent inability to restore mitochondrial function with severe membrane damage supports irreversible injury.

Understanding the options

The injury has crossed into an irreversible state
Persistent inability to restore mitochondrial function with severe membrane damage supports irreversible injury.
All changes are necessarily reversible because oxygen returned
Restoring the environment cannot rescue cells that have passed the irreversible threshold.
The cells have undergone physiologic hypertrophy
Hypertrophy is adaptive growth rather than failure of viability.
The findings establish an exclusively apoptotic mechanism
Severe membrane damage is compatible with necrosis and does not establish apoptosis alone.
ATP depletion never contributed to the injury
Earlier ATP depletion can initiate the cascade leading to irreversible damage.
