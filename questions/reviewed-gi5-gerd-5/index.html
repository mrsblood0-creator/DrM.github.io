Persistent symptoms need reassessment

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient reports continuing reflux symptoms despite prescribed acid suppression. Before changing therapy, the clinician reviews medication timing, adherence, symptoms and alternative diagnoses. Why is that review appropriate rather than automatically increasing medication indefinitely?

  1. Treatment timing and adherence never matter
  2. No alternative diagnosis can mimic reflux
  3. Persistent symptoms may reflect incorrect use or another diagnosis
  4. Every symptom persisting on a PPI proves cancer
  5. All persistent symptoms mean acid production is completely absent
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Persistent symptoms may reflect incorrect use or another diagnosis

Apparent treatment failure requires reassessment, including delivery, adherence and diagnostic accuracy.

Understanding the options

Persistent symptoms may reflect incorrect use or another diagnosis
Apparent treatment failure requires reassessment, including delivery, adherence and diagnostic accuracy.
Every symptom persisting on a PPI proves cancer
Persistent symptoms do not establish malignancy by themselves.
All persistent symptoms mean acid production is completely absent
Symptoms alone cannot establish acid physiology.
Treatment timing and adherence never matter
Both can affect response.
No alternative diagnosis can mimic reflux
Other esophageal and non-esophageal disorders can resemble reflux.
