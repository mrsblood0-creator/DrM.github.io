Cephalosporin pharmacology: 1

A susceptible bacterial infection is treated with cefazolin. Microscopy shows impaired integrity of newly formed cell walls during bacterial growth. Which biochemical process is directly inhibited by this cephalosporin, as with other beta-lactam antibiotics?

  1. Binding to D-Ala-D-Ala
  2. Hydrolysis of the beta-lactam ring
  3. Altered penicillin-binding proteins
  4. Absence of a peptidoglycan cell wall
  5. Inhibition of peptidoglycan cross-linking
Answer and explanation

Inhibition of peptidoglycan cross-linking

Beta-lactams bind PBPs and inhibit transpeptidation during cell-wall synthesis.

Option explanations

Inhibition of peptidoglycan cross-linking: Beta-lactams bind PBPs and inhibit transpeptidation during cell-wall synthesis.

Binding to D-Ala-D-Ala: Vancomycin binds the terminal dipeptide of peptidoglycan precursors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Hydrolysis of the beta-lactam ring: Beta-lactamases inactivate susceptible beta-lactam drugs by ring hydrolysis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Altered penicillin-binding proteins: Changes in PBPs can reduce beta-lactam affinity; PBP2a is central to methicillin resistance. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Absence of a peptidoglycan cell wall: Mycoplasma lacks the target required for beta-lactam activity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
