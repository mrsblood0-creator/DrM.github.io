A contraceptive interaction

A 26-year-old woman taking a combined oral contraceptive begins carbamazepine for epilepsy. She asks why the pharmacist recommends discussing an alternative contraceptive method. Which mechanism best explains reduced contraceptive reliability in this setting?

  1. Hepatic enzyme induction accelerates hormone metabolism
  2. Irreversible antagonism at progesterone receptors
  3. Complete suppression of carbamazepine absorption by estrogen
  4. Reduced renal elimination of estrogen
  5. Direct destruction of the contraceptive tablet in the stomach
Answer and explanation

Hepatic enzyme induction accelerates hormone metabolism

Carbamazepine can lower hormonal-contraceptive exposure through enzyme induction.

Option explanations

Hepatic enzyme induction accelerates hormone metabolism: Carbamazepine can lower hormonal-contraceptive exposure through enzyme induction.

Irreversible antagonism at progesterone receptors: This is not the interaction mechanism.

Complete suppression of carbamazepine absorption by estrogen: The concern is reduced contraceptive hormone exposure.

Reduced renal elimination of estrogen: This would tend to increase hormone exposure.

Direct destruction of the contraceptive tablet in the stomach: This is not the established mechanism.
