Peptic stricture

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with years of poorly controlled reflux develops gradually worsening solid-food dysphagia. Endoscopy shows a benign distal esophageal narrowing with inflammatory scarring. Which complication of reflux best explains this mechanical obstruction?

  1. An isolated gastric ulcer
  2. Acute viral gastroenteritis
  3. Duodenal obstruction
  4. Peptic esophageal stricture
  5. Achalasia without a fixed narrowing
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Peptic esophageal stricture

Repeated acid injury can heal with fibrosis and narrowing.

Understanding the options

Peptic esophageal stricture
Repeated acid injury can heal with fibrosis and narrowing.
Achalasia without a fixed narrowing
Achalasia is a motility disorder, unlike the described scarred segment.
An isolated gastric ulcer
A gastric ulcer does not directly create this distal esophageal scar.
Acute viral gastroenteritis
This does not cause chronic fibrotic esophageal narrowing.
Duodenal obstruction
The identified narrowing is in the esophagus.
