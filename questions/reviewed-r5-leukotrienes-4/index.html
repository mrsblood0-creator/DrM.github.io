Monitoring a synthesis inhibitor

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 38-year-old patient is being considered for zileuton. Baseline tests show active liver disease with substantially elevated aminotransferases. Which organ-specific toxicity is most relevant when deciding whether this medication is appropriate?

  1. Hepatotoxicity
  2. Retinal pigment deposition
  3. Irreversible ototoxicity
  4. Hemorrhagic cystitis
  5. Gingival hyperplasia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Hepatotoxicity

Zileuton can cause liver injury and requires attention to baseline and follow-up liver tests.

Understanding the options

Hepatotoxicity
Zileuton can cause liver injury and requires attention to baseline and follow-up liver tests.
Retinal pigment deposition
This is not the characteristic monitoring issue for zileuton.
Irreversible ototoxicity
This is more characteristic of drugs such as aminoglycosides.
Hemorrhagic cystitis
This is associated with certain chemotherapy agents rather than zileuton.
Gingival hyperplasia
This is classically associated with phenytoin.
