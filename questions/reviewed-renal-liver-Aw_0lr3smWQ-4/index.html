Alcohol-associated hepatitis: 4

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient hospitalized with alcohol-associated hepatitis has weight loss and poor food intake. Which supportive-care priority should be assessed directly rather than assuming calories from alcohol have prevented clinically important nutritional deficits?

  1. Support sustained alcohol abstinence
  2. Evaluate infection and treatment contraindications
  3. Assess and treat malnutrition
  4. Alcohol-associated hepatitis
  5. Supportive but not diagnostic AST predominance
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Assess and treat malnutrition

Nutritional deficits are common and need active assessment and support.

Understanding the options
