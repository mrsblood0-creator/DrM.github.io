Position of a definitive airway

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

During resuscitation of an unconscious adult, a trainee compares the positions of an oropharyngeal airway, a supraglottic airway and a cuffed endotracheal tube on an anatomic model. Which feature specifically distinguishes the correctly positioned cuffed endotracheal tube from the other two devices?

  1. It prevents every episode of aspiration
  2. It requires no confirmation after placement
  3. It eliminates the need to assess ventilation
  4. The tube enters the trachea with its cuff below the vocal cords
  5. It rests entirely above the vocal cords
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

The tube enters the trachea with its cuff below the vocal cords

A cuffed tracheal tube passes through the glottis; oral and supraglottic devices do not.

Understanding the options

The tube enters the trachea with its cuff below the vocal cords
A cuffed tracheal tube passes through the glottis; oral and supraglottic devices do not.
It rests entirely above the vocal cords
That describes a supraglottic location, not a correctly positioned tracheal tube.
It prevents every episode of aspiration
A cuff reduces gross leakage but does not eliminate all aspiration risk.
It requires no confirmation after placement
Tracheal placement must be confirmed and monitored.
It eliminates the need to assess ventilation
Tube placement does not ensure adequate ventilation or exclude later displacement.
