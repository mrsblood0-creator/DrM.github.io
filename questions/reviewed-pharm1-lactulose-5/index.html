Bloating after starting lactulose

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with constipation notices increased flatus and abdominal bloating shortly after beginning lactulose, although stool passage improves. There is no bleeding, fever or severe pain. Which process most plausibly produces this common effect?

  1. Histamine release from gastric enterochromaffin-like cells
  2. Bacterial fermentation of the disaccharide
  3. Immediate neutralization of acid by bicarbonate in the tablet
  4. Acute inhibition of pancreatic lipase
  5. Suppression of all colonic bacterial metabolism
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Bacterial fermentation of the disaccharide

Colonic fermentation generates gas as well as organic acids.

Understanding the options

Bacterial fermentation of the disaccharide
Colonic fermentation generates gas as well as organic acids.
Immediate neutralization of acid by bicarbonate in the tablet
Lactulose is not a bicarbonate antacid.
Acute inhibition of pancreatic lipase
Pancreatic lipase inhibition is not its target.
Suppression of all colonic bacterial metabolism
Fermentation, rather than its complete suppression, is central to the effect.
Histamine release from gastric enterochromaffin-like cells
Gastric histamine secretion does not directly explain colonic fermentation gas.
