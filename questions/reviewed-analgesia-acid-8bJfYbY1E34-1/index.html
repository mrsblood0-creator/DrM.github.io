Proton pump inhibitors: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient receives omeprazole for an appropriate acid-related condition. The medicine blocks the final common secretory step in the gastric parietal cell rather than only blocking histamine signaling. Which molecular target explains this effect?

  1. Coordinate conventional PPI dosing with meal-related pump activation
  2. Hypomagnesemia
  3. Acute interstitial nephritis
  4. Review the continuing indication and lowest effective regimen
  5. Gastric H+/K+-ATPase inhibition
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Gastric H+/K+-ATPase inhibition

PPIs inhibit the final proton pump responsible for gastric acid secretion.

Understanding the options

Gastric H+/K+-ATPase inhibition

Coordinate conventional PPI dosing with meal-related pump activation

Hypomagnesemia
Prolonged PPI exposure can be associated with low magnesium, particularly with other risk factors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Acute interstitial nephritis
PPIs can cause drug-associated interstitial kidney inflammation. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
