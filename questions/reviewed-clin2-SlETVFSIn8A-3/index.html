Failure of initial therapy

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 73-year-old with sinus bradycardia remains confused and hypotensive after atropine and correction of hypoxemia. His pulse is 32/min. The team prepares a temporary transvenous pacing system. Which intervention can provide a bridge while definitive pacing is arranged?

  1. Unsynchronized defibrillation
  2. Adenosine administration
  3. Carotid sinus massage
  4. Transcutaneous pacing
  5. Synchronized cardioversion
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Transcutaneous pacing

External pacing can support rate while temporary transvenous pacing is prepared.

Understanding the options

Transcutaneous pacing
External pacing can support rate while temporary transvenous pacing is prepared.
Synchronized cardioversion
Cardioversion treats selected tachyarrhythmias.
Unsynchronized defibrillation
Defibrillation is for shockable arrest rhythms, not a perfusing slow sinus rhythm.
Adenosine administration
Adenosine may further slow conduction.
Carotid sinus massage
Vagal stimulation may worsen the bradycardia.
