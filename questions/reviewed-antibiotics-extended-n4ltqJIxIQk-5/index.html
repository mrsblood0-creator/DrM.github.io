Urinary antiseptics: 5

A patient receiving prolonged nitrofurantoin develops a new dry cough and breathlessness. Imaging shows interstitial changes, and the clinician reviews medication toxicity alongside other causes. Which recognized adverse effect is relevant to this presentation?

  1. Upper urinary tract infection requiring tissue-active therapy
  2. Nitrofurantoin-associated pulmonary toxicity
  3. Nitrofurantoin
  4. Methenamine
  5. Fosfomycin
Answer and explanation

Nitrofurantoin-associated pulmonary toxicity

Nitrofurantoin can cause acute or chronic pulmonary reactions, requiring prompt assessment when respiratory symptoms arise.

Option explanations

Nitrofurantoin-associated pulmonary toxicity: Nitrofurantoin can cause acute or chronic pulmonary reactions, requiring prompt assessment when respiratory symptoms arise.

Nitrofurantoin: Nitrofurantoin achieves useful bladder urine concentrations but inadequate renal tissue concentrations for pyelonephritis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Methenamine: Methenamine releases formaldehyde in sufficiently acidic urine and is used in selected prevention strategies. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Fosfomycin: Oral fosfomycin inhibits an early step in peptidoglycan synthesis and is used for selected lower urinary infections. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Upper urinary tract infection requiring tissue-active therapy: Fever and flank pain suggest pyelonephritis; bladder-focused therapy alone is inadequate. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
