Opioid adverse effects during cough treatment

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An adult using codeine for a dry cough reports fewer bowel movements and increasing constipation after several days. Examination reveals no abdominal guarding or vomiting. Which action of the medicine most directly explains the change in bowel function?

  1. Reduced gastrointestinal propulsive motility
  2. Increased secretion of intestinal chloride
  3. Inhibition of intestinal glucose absorption
  4. Direct osmotic retention of water in stool
  5. Selective destruction of colonic bacteria
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Reduced gastrointestinal propulsive motility

Opioid receptor activation decreases propulsive intestinal motility.

Understanding the options

Reduced gastrointestinal propulsive motility
Opioid receptor activation decreases propulsive intestinal motility.
Increased secretion of intestinal chloride
Increased chloride secretion usually promotes fluid-rich stool.
Inhibition of intestinal glucose absorption
Glucose malabsorption tends to cause osmotic diarrhea.
Direct osmotic retention of water in stool
Osmotic water retention softens stool rather than causing typical opioid constipation.
Selective destruction of colonic bacteria
Codeine is not an intestinal antibacterial drug.
