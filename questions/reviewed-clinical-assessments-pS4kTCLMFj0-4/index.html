ASIA impairment scale: 4

A patient has motor-incomplete spinal cord injury with sacral motor sparing. At least half of key muscle functions below the single neurological level have strength of at least grade 3. Which AIS category applies?

  1. AIS D
  2. AIS A
  3. AIS B
  4. AIS C
  5. AIS E
Answer and explanation

AIS D

Motor-incomplete injury is present and at least half of key muscles below the single neurological level have grade at least 3.

Option explanations

AIS D: Motor-incomplete injury is present and at least half of key muscles below the single neurological level have grade at least 3.

AIS A: No sacral sensory or motor function is preserved at S4-S5. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

AIS B: Sacral sensory sparing is present, but motor-incomplete criteria are not met. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

AIS C: Motor-incomplete injury is present and fewer than half of key muscles below the single neurological level have grade at least 3. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

AIS E: Sensory and motor examination is normal in a patient who previously had SCI-related deficits. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
