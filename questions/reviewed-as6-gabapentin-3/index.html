Combined respiratory depressants

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 69-year-old man with chronic lung disease takes oxycodone for cancer pain. Gabapentin is added for a neuropathic component. His family is instructed to watch for excessive sedation and slow breathing. Which interaction explains this precaution?

  1. Increased neuromuscular acetylcholine release
  2. Additive central nervous system and respiratory depression
  3. Competitive reversal of opioid effects
  4. Acute acetylcholinesterase inhibition
  5. Rapid CYP induction lowering oxycodone exposure
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Additive central nervous system and respiratory depression

Opioids and respiratory impairment increase the risk of serious breathing problems with gabapentin.

Understanding the options

Additive central nervous system and respiratory depression
Opioids and respiratory impairment increase the risk of serious breathing problems with gabapentin.
Competitive reversal of opioid effects
Gabapentin does not act as an opioid antagonist.
Acute acetylcholinesterase inhibition
Neither drug combination causes a cholinergic toxidrome by this mechanism.
Rapid CYP induction lowering oxycodone exposure
This is not the reason for the breathing warning.
Increased neuromuscular acetylcholine release
This does not explain sedation and respiratory suppression.
