Increasing reliever use

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient previously needed occasional albuterol but now uses it many times each day and wakes at night with wheeze. He requests only a larger supply. What is the most appropriate interpretation of this change?

  1. Proof that the controller dose should always be stopped
  2. Evidence that no further assessment is needed
  3. A guarantee that the disease is becoming milder
  4. An indication to replace all therapy with an oral antihistamine
  5. Worsening control requiring prompt reassessment of asthma management
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Worsening control requiring prompt reassessment of asthma management

Increasing reliever need is a warning sign, not merely a reason to continue unlimited SABA use.

Understanding the options

Worsening control requiring prompt reassessment of asthma management
Increasing reliever need is a warning sign, not merely a reason to continue unlimited SABA use.
Proof that the controller dose should always be stopped
Stopping anti-inflammatory control may worsen risk.
Evidence that no further assessment is needed
Frequent symptoms require evaluation.
A guarantee that the disease is becoming milder
More symptoms indicate the opposite.
An indication to replace all therapy with an oral antihistamine
Antihistamines do not substitute for asthma control.
