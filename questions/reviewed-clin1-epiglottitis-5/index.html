A stable patient with suspected epiglottitis undergoes a lateral neck radiograph after appropriate airway assessment. The epiglottic shadow is enlarged and rounded. Which commonly used descriptive sign corresponds to this appearance?

  1. Thumb sign
  2. Steeple sign
  3. Air crescent sign
  4. Deep sulcus sign
  5. Silhouette sign
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Thumb sign

An enlarged epiglottis can resemble a thumb on the lateral view.

Understanding the options

Thumb sign
An enlarged epiglottis can resemble a thumb on the lateral view.
Steeple sign
The steeple sign describes subglottic narrowing associated with croup.
Air crescent sign
An air crescent is a pulmonary cavitary imaging feature.
Deep sulcus sign
The deep sulcus sign can indicate pneumothorax on a supine chest radiograph.
Silhouette sign
The silhouette sign concerns loss of normal chest interfaces, not epiglottic shape.
