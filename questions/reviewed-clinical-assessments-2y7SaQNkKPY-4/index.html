Depression assessment: 4

During a severe depressive episode, a patient develops a fixed false belief that the body has irreversibly decayed. Which clinical feature category should be recognized because it changes the severity assessment and treatment approach?

  1. Immediate suicide-risk assessment and safety planning
  2. A recurrent seasonal pattern
  3. Depression with psychotic symptoms
  4. Assess for a depressive episode
  5. Assess prior mania or hypomania
Answer and explanation

Depression with psychotic symptoms

Mood episodes may include delusions or hallucinations; psychotic features change assessment and treatment urgency.

Option explanations

Depression with psychotic symptoms: Mood episodes may include delusions or hallucinations; psychotic features change assessment and treatment urgency.

Assess for a depressive episode: Persistent low mood or loss of interest with associated symptoms and impairment requires a full diagnostic assessment, usually over at least two weeks. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Assess prior mania or hypomania: A history of elevated mood, reduced need for sleep and increased activity may indicate bipolar disorder rather than unipolar depression. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Immediate suicide-risk assessment and safety planning: Current suicidal intent, planning or inability to stay safe requires urgent assessment and protective support. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

A recurrent seasonal pattern: Repeated episodes linked to a particular season, with remission at other times, support a seasonal course pattern after alternatives are assessed. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
