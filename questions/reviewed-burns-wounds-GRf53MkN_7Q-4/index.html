Superficial burns: 4

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A superficial epidermal burn resolves without a deep scar as viable epithelial cells restore the surface. Which repair mechanism explains healing when the underlying regenerative sources remain preserved rather than being destroyed through the dermis?

  1. Inflammatory vasodilation
  2. Re-epithelialization from preserved viable epidermal sources
  3. Exclude purely superficial burns from resuscitation TBSA
  4. Include partial- and full-thickness areas in TBSA
  5. Cool the burn safely and assess severity
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Re-epithelialization from preserved viable epidermal sources

Superficial injuries retain capacity for epithelial regeneration; deeper loss reduces this reserve.

Understanding the options

Exclude purely superficial burns from resuscitation TBSA
Burn-area estimates used for major burn assessment count partial- and full-thickness injury, not simple superficial erythema. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Include partial- and full-thickness areas in TBSA
Dermal and deeper burns contribute to the burn area used for severity and resuscitation assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Cool the burn safely and assess severity
Appropriate cool running water first aid and protection are used; ice and additional cold injury should be avoided. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Inflammatory vasodilation
