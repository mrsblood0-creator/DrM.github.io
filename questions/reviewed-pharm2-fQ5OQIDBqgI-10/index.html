Symptoms before an antidepressant switch

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 36-year-old woman taking sertraline now sleeps very little without feeling tired, speaks rapidly and describes special powers. Her family reports an earlier episode with similar behavior before she received any antidepressant. Which diagnosis should be reassessed before treating this as ordinary medication-related insomnia?

  1. Simple transient insomnia
  2. Isolated SSRI-associated nausea
  3. Antidepressant discontinuation syndrome
  4. Opioid withdrawal
  5. Bipolar disorder with a manic episode
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Bipolar disorder with a manic episode

The episodic mood and behavioral change, especially the prior untreated episode, calls for bipolar assessment.

Understanding the options

Bipolar disorder with a manic episode
The episodic mood and behavioral change, especially the prior untreated episode, calls for bipolar assessment.
Simple transient insomnia
Reduced need for sleep with grandiosity is more than difficulty sleeping.
Isolated SSRI-associated nausea
A gastrointestinal adverse effect does not explain the behavioral syndrome.
Antidepressant discontinuation syndrome
She is still taking the drug, and the history supports recurrent mood episodes.
Opioid withdrawal
There is no opioid history or characteristic withdrawal pattern.
