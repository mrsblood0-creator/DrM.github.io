Extended-release tablets and swallowing difficulty

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 48-year-old man has difficulty swallowing a large extended-release guaifenesin tablet. He plans to crush it and mix the powder into food while keeping the same dosing interval. Which advice best preserves appropriate drug delivery?

  1. Chew the tablet to prolong its duration of action
  2. Dissolve the crushed tablet and double the interval without review
  3. Take two crushed tablets to compensate for loss during mixing
  4. Use a suitable alternative formulation instead of crushing the extended-release tablet
  5. Crush the tablet because the release mechanism is unaffected
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Use a suitable alternative formulation instead of crushing the extended-release tablet

Extended-release guaifenesin tablets should be swallowed whole; a suitable alternative formulation can be discussed.

Understanding the options

Use a suitable alternative formulation instead of crushing the extended-release tablet
A formulation change avoids disrupting the extended-release design.
Crush the tablet because the release mechanism is unaffected
Crushing can alter how the dose is released.
Chew the tablet to prolong its duration of action
Chewing does not preserve the intended extended-release behavior.
Dissolve the crushed tablet and double the interval without review
An arbitrary interval change does not establish safe or equivalent delivery.
Take two crushed tablets to compensate for loss during mixing
Doubling a dose is not an appropriate solution to a formulation problem.
