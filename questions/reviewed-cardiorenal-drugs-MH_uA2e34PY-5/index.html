Amlodipine contraindications and precautions: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A student labels every condition mentioned in an amlodipine safety lecture an absolute contraindication. Some are instead circumstances requiring monitoring or dose adjustment. Which prescribing principle prevents this overgeneralization from the teaching list?

  1. Arteriolar L-type calcium-channel blockade
  2. Precapillary arteriolar dilation
  3. Vasodilatory flushing
  4. Vasodilatory headache
  5. Distinguish a precaution from an absolute contraindication
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Distinguish a precaution from an absolute contraindication

Clinical cautions require individualized assessment; a lecture list should not turn every risk factor into an absolute prohibition.

Arteriolar L-type calcium-channel blockade
Amlodipine predominantly relaxes vascular smooth muscle and lowers systemic resistance. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Precapillary arteriolar dilation
Preferential arteriolar dilation increases capillary hydrostatic pressure and can cause dependent edema without primary fluid overload. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Vasodilatory flushing
Cutaneous vasodilation can cause warmth and flushing. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Vasodilatory headache
Headache can occur with vasodilator therapy and must be interpreted in clinical context. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
