Quinupristin-dalfopristin: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A trainee assumes quinupristin-dalfopristin covers all enterococci because it can treat selected E. faecium infections. The supervisor identifies a species with intrinsic resistance that invalidates this assumption. Which organism is the important exception?

  1. Susceptible Enterococcus faecium
  2. Arthralgia and myalgia
  3. CYP3A4-mediated drug interactions
  4. Enterococcus faecalis
  5. Synergistic binding at the 50S ribosome
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Enterococcus faecalis

E. faecalis is intrinsically resistant to quinupristin-dalfopristin and must not be confused with susceptible E. faecium.
