Protein reclamation by renal tubules

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with marked glomerular proteinuria has a kidney biopsy. Proximal tubular epithelial cells contain eosinophilic cytoplasmic droplets, while the glomerular barrier is abnormally permeable to plasma proteins. Which mechanism most likely produced the tubular inclusions?

  1. Storage of neutral triglyceride
  2. Accumulation of iron after local hemorrhage
  3. Retention of inhaled carbon particles
  4. Endocytosis and lysosomal accumulation of filtered protein
  5. Intracellular synthesis of amyloid fibrils
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Endocytosis and lysosomal accumulation of filtered protein

Proximal tubular cells reabsorb filtered proteins; a large protein load produces visible reabsorption droplets.

Understanding the options

Endocytosis and lysosomal accumulation of filtered protein
Proximal tubular cells reabsorb filtered proteins; a large protein load produces visible reabsorption droplets.
Intracellular synthesis of amyloid fibrils
Amyloid is characteristically extracellular, not tubular protein-reabsorption droplets.
Storage of neutral triglyceride
Triglyceride produces lipid vacuoles rather than eosinophilic protein droplets.
Accumulation of iron after local hemorrhage
Hemosiderin would be iron-positive brown pigment.
Retention of inhaled carbon particles
Carbon deposition follows inhalation and macrophage transport, not glomerular protein leakage.
