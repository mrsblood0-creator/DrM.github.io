Tigecycline: 2

A patient’s culture identifies Pseudomonas aeruginosa. Tigecycline is proposed because it is described as broad spectrum, but the infectious-disease team rejects that assumption. Which organism is the clinically important spectrum gap in this proposal?

  1. Nausea and vomiting
  2. Low serum concentrations relative to tissue distribution
  3. Pseudomonas aeruginosa
  4. Inhibition of aminoacyl-tRNA binding to 30S
  5. Increased all-cause mortality signal
Answer and explanation

Pseudomonas aeruginosa

Tigecycline does not provide reliable Pseudomonas coverage.

Option explanations

Pseudomonas aeruginosa: Tigecycline does not provide reliable Pseudomonas coverage.

Inhibition of aminoacyl-tRNA binding to 30S: Tigecycline is a glycylcycline related to tetracyclines and inhibits bacterial protein synthesis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Increased all-cause mortality signal: Tigecycline carries a mortality warning and should be reserved for situations where alternatives are unsuitable. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Nausea and vomiting: Gastrointestinal intolerance is common with tigecycline. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Low serum concentrations relative to tissue distribution: Extensive tissue distribution limits the suitability of tigecycline alone for bloodstream infection. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
