Weak-acid ionization

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A weakly acidic drug has a pKa of 4.0. A researcher places it in a solution with pH 6.0 and assumes equilibrium without protein binding. What is the approximate ratio of ionized to unionized drug in that solution?

  1. 100:1
  2. 1:100
  3. 1:1
  4. 10:1
  5. 1:10
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

100:1

For a weak acid, A−/HA = 10^(pH−pKa) = 100.

Understanding the options

100:1
For a weak acid, A−/HA = 10^(pH−pKa) = 100.
1:100
This reverses the weak-acid relationship.
1:1
Equal forms occur when pH equals pKa.
10:1
A one-unit, not two-unit, pH difference gives this ratio.
1:10
This has both the wrong direction and magnitude.
