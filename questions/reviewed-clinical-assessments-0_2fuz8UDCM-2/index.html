Infectious mononucleosis: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Blood film examination in EBV mononucleosis shows large atypical lymphocytes. Which cell population chiefly produces this reactive appearance, rather than assuming every unusual circulating lymphocyte is an EBV-infected malignant B cell?

  1. Splenic rupture risk
  2. Reactive cytotoxic T lymphocytes
  3. Epstein-Barr virus infectious mononucleosis
  4. Early negative heterophile testing does not exclude EBV
  5. An aminopenicillin-associated rash during EBV infection
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Reactive cytotoxic T lymphocytes

The atypical lymphocytes in EBV mononucleosis are largely activated T cells responding to infected B cells.
