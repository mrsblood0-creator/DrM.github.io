Growth-plate injury: 2

A child’s fracture passes along the growth plate and exits through the metaphysis, leaving a small metaphyseal fragment. The articular epiphysis is not split. Which Salter-Harris type best describes the observed fracture route?

  1. Salter-Harris II
  2. Salter-Harris I
  3. Salter-Harris III
  4. Salter-Harris IV
  5. Salter-Harris V
Answer and explanation

Salter-Harris II

The fracture crosses the physis and exits through the metaphysis.

Option explanations

Salter-Harris II: The fracture crosses the physis and exits through the metaphysis.

Salter-Harris I: The fracture traverses the physis without a metaphyseal or epiphyseal component. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Salter-Harris III: The fracture crosses the physis and epiphysis into the joint. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Salter-Harris IV: The fracture traverses metaphysis, physis and epiphysis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Salter-Harris V: A compressive physeal injury can be initially subtle and later cause growth arrest. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
