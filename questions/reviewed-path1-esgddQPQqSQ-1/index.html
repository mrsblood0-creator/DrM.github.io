Radiation and reactive intermediates

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Cultured intestinal epithelial cells are irradiated in an aqueous medium. Investigators detect reactive intermediates formed from water, followed by membrane and DNA damage. Which reactive species is especially important in the indirect cellular injury caused by this exposure?

  1. Hydroxyl radical
  2. Molecular nitrogen
  3. Bicarbonate ion
  4. Chloride ion
  5. Ferritin protein
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Hydroxyl radical

Water radiolysis can generate highly reactive hydroxyl radicals that damage nearby cellular molecules.

Understanding the options

Hydroxyl radical
Water radiolysis can generate highly reactive hydroxyl radicals that damage nearby cellular molecules.
Molecular nitrogen
Nitrogen is not the principal damaging intermediate in water radiolysis.
Bicarbonate ion
Bicarbonate is a buffer and is not the highly reactive species sought here.
Chloride ion
Ordinary chloride is not the characteristic water-derived free radical.
Ferritin protein
Ferritin stores iron; it is not a radical produced by irradiating water.
