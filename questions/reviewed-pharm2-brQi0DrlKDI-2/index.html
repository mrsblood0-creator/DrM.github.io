Peripheral cough suppression

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A pharmacology experiment measures cough responses to airway stretch while separately assessing central opioid receptor activity. A test drug reduces cough by anesthetizing respiratory sensory receptors without activating opioid receptors. Which drug best fits this profile?

  1. Benzonatate
  2. Codeine
  3. Morphine
  4. Guaifenesin
  5. Acetylcysteine
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Benzonatate

Benzonatate reduces afferent signaling from respiratory stretch receptors.

Understanding the options

Benzonatate
Benzonatate reduces afferent signaling from respiratory stretch receptors.
Codeine
Codeine suppresses cough centrally through opioid-related actions.
Morphine
Morphine is an opioid agonist rather than a peripheral airway anesthetic.
Guaifenesin
Guaifenesin is an expectorant, not a sensory local anesthetic.
Acetylcysteine
Acetylcysteine reduces mucus viscosity by disrupting disulfide bonds.
