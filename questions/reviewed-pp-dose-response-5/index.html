Maximum response

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

In an isolated tissue experiment, drug X has a lower EC50 than drug Y but cannot produce as large a maximal response, even at saturating concentrations. Which conclusion distinguishes the two pharmacodynamic properties correctly in this experiment?

  1. X is more potent and more efficacious
  2. X and Y must have equal efficacy
  3. X must be a neutral antagonist
  4. X is more potent but less efficacious than Y
  5. X is less potent but more efficacious
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

X is more potent but less efficacious than Y

Potency follows EC50, whereas efficacy follows the achievable maximum in the specified system.

Understanding the options

X is more potent but less efficacious than Y
Potency follows EC50, whereas efficacy follows the achievable maximum in the specified system.
X is less potent but more efficacious
Both relationships are reversed.
X is more potent and more efficacious
Its lower maximum indicates lower efficacy.
X and Y must have equal efficacy
The measured maxima differ.
X must be a neutral antagonist
X produces a response and therefore is not a neutral antagonist.
