Pathological fractures: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient has multiple lytic lesions, anemia, renal impairment and a monoclonal protein, then sustains a low-energy fracture. Which underlying disease best unifies the skeletal and laboratory findings in this pathological fracture presentation?

  1. Osteoporosis
  2. Metastatic bone disease
  3. Osteomalacia
  4. Characterize the lesion before definitive fixation when feasible
  5. Multiple myeloma
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Multiple myeloma

Lytic lesions with anemia, renal dysfunction and a monoclonal protein suggest plasma-cell disease.

Understanding the options

Multiple myeloma
Lytic lesions with anemia, renal dysfunction and a monoclonal protein suggest plasma-cell disease.
Osteoporosis
Reduced bone strength can cause fragility fractures after low-energy trauma. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Metastatic bone disease
A destructive lesion in a patient with a known malignancy raises concern for metastatic pathological fracture. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Osteomalacia
Defective mineralization can produce bone pain and insufficiency fractures. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Characterize the lesion before definitive fixation when feasible
A suspicious unexplained bone lesion requires planned oncologic assessment; an unplanned biopsy or fixation can compromise treatment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
