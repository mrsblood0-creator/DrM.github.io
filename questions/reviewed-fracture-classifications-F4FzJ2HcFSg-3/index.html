Thompson-Epstein classification: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A posterior hip dislocation is associated with a posterior acetabular wall broken into multiple fragments. A larger fragment may also be present, but comminution is the defining feature. Which Thompson-Epstein grade applies?

  1. Thompson-Epstein II
  2. Thompson-Epstein IV
  3. Thompson-Epstein V
  4. Thompson-Epstein III
  5. Thompson-Epstein I
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Thompson-Epstein III

Posterior dislocation accompanies a comminuted posterior acetabular wall fracture.

Understanding the options

Thompson-Epstein III
Posterior dislocation accompanies a comminuted posterior acetabular wall fracture.
Thompson-Epstein I
Posterior hip dislocation has no significant acetabular fracture or only a minor fragment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Thompson-Epstein II
Posterior dislocation accompanies a single large posterior acetabular wall fragment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Thompson-Epstein IV
Posterior dislocation is associated with fracture of the acetabular floor. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Thompson-Epstein V
Posterior dislocation is associated with femoral head fracture. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
