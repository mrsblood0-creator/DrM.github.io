Aminoglycosides: 4

A clinician avoids relying on gentamicin alone for a strictly anaerobic infection. The antibiotic cannot efficiently enter these organisms under anaerobic conditions. Which property of aminoglycoside transport explains this important limitation of its spectrum?

  1. Alteration of the ribosomal binding site
  2. Oxygen-dependent bacterial uptake
  3. Poor systemic absorption after oral administration
  4. High oral bioavailability
  5. Increased bacterial drug efflux
Answer and explanation

Oxygen-dependent bacterial uptake

Aminoglycosides require active oxygen-dependent transport into susceptible bacteria.

Option explanations

Oxygen-dependent bacterial uptake: Aminoglycosides require active oxygen-dependent transport into susceptible bacteria.

Poor systemic absorption after oral administration: Oral vancomycin acts mainly in the bowel and does not provide dependable systemic therapy. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

High oral bioavailability: Linezolid has high oral bioavailability, permitting an appropriate IV-to-oral switch in selected stable patients. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Increased bacterial drug efflux: Efflux can lower intracellular tetracycline concentrations and cause resistance. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Alteration of the ribosomal binding site: Ribosomal methylation or other target changes can reduce binding of some protein-synthesis inhibitors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
