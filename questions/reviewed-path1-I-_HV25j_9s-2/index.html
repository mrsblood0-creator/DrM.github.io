A compact dark nucleus

A pathologist examining an acutely injured tissue sees a dying cell with a markedly shrunken, intensely basophilic nucleus. The chromatin is condensed, but the nucleus remains a single structure rather than multiple fragments. Which term applies?

  1. Karyorrhexis
  2. Karyolysis
  3. Polyploidy
  4. Nucleolar prominence
  5. Pyknosis
Answer and explanation

Pyknosis

Pyknosis describes nuclear shrinkage and chromatin condensation.

Option explanations

Pyknosis: Pyknosis describes nuclear shrinkage and chromatin condensation.

Karyorrhexis: Karyorrhexis requires fragmentation of the nucleus.

Karyolysis: Karyolysis is loss of nuclear staining.

Polyploidy: Polyploidy increases DNA content and often nuclear size.

Nucleolar prominence: Prominent nucleoli do not describe global nuclear condensation.
