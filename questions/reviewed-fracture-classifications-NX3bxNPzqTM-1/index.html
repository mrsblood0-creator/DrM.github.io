Supporting fracture healing: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient takes adequate nutrients but repeatedly loads an inadequately stabilized fracture, causing painful movement at the site. Which essential healing requirement is not replaced by a good diet or by taking additional vitamin supplements?

  1. Individualized medication review
  2. Activity matched to fracture stability
  3. Appropriate mechanical stability
  4. Adequate protein and micronutrient intake
  5. Adequate blood supply
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Appropriate mechanical stability

Nutrition cannot compensate for inadequate fracture stability.

Understanding the options

Adequate protein and micronutrient intake

Adequate blood supply

Individualized medication review
