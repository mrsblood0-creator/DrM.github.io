A mechanically untestable eye response

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 40-year-old man has severe bilateral eyelid swelling after facial trauma. His eyelids cannot open mechanically, although he is conversing and following commands. Which eye-component entry avoids confusing a local injury with impaired consciousness during Glasgow Coma Scale assessment?

  1. ENT, documenting the local obstruction
  2. E1 because no opening is seen
  3. E2 because trauma is painful
  4. E3 because the patient speaks
  5. E4 because commands are followed
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

ENT, documenting the local obstruction

Mechanical obstruction makes eye opening non-testable.

Understanding the options

ENT, documenting the local obstruction
Mechanical obstruction makes eye opening non-testable.
E1 because no opening is seen
E1 requires no response without a local factor preventing assessment.
E2 because trauma is painful
Pain alone does not establish an observed eye-opening response.
E3 because the patient speaks
Speech cannot substitute for observed eye opening.
E4 because commands are followed
Motor performance does not establish spontaneous eye opening.
