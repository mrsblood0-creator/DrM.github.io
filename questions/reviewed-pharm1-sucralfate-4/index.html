Constipation after starting a protectant

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A woman begins sucralfate for a duodenal ulcer and develops hard stools. She has no fever, weight loss, blood in the stool or abdominal distention. Which adverse effect is most consistent with this new medication?

  1. Hyperprolactinemia
  2. Irreversible hearing loss
  3. Constipation
  4. Profuse prostaglandin-mediated diarrhea
  5. Tardive dyskinesia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Constipation

Constipation is the most commonly reported sucralfate adverse effect.

Understanding the options

Constipation
Constipation is the most commonly reported sucralfate adverse effect.
Profuse prostaglandin-mediated diarrhea
Misoprostol is more characteristically associated with diarrhea.
Tardive dyskinesia
Sucralfate does not block central dopamine receptors.
Hyperprolactinemia
It does not characteristically elevate prolactin.
Irreversible hearing loss
Ototoxicity is not a typical sucralfate effect.
