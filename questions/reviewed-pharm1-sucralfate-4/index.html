Constipation after starting a protectant

A woman begins sucralfate for a duodenal ulcer and develops hard stools. She has no fever, weight loss, blood in the stool or abdominal distention. Which adverse effect is most consistent with this new medication?

  1. Hyperprolactinemia
  2. Irreversible hearing loss
  3. Constipation
  4. Profuse prostaglandin-mediated diarrhea
  5. Tardive dyskinesia
Answer and explanation

Constipation

Constipation is the most commonly reported sucralfate adverse effect.

Option explanations

Constipation: Constipation is the most commonly reported sucralfate adverse effect.

Profuse prostaglandin-mediated diarrhea: Misoprostol is more characteristically associated with diarrhea.

Tardive dyskinesia: Sucralfate does not block central dopamine receptors.

Hyperprolactinemia: It does not characteristically elevate prolactin.

Irreversible hearing loss: Ototoxicity is not a typical sucralfate effect.
