Pipkin classification: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A posterior hip fracture-dislocation includes a femoral head fracture extending superior to the fovea into the weight-bearing region. There is no neck or acetabular fracture. Which Pipkin category corresponds to the described injury?

  1. Isolated femoral neck fracture
  2. Pipkin II
  3. Pipkin I
  4. Pipkin III
  5. Pipkin IV
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Pipkin II

The femoral head fracture extends superior to the fovea into the weight-bearing region.

Understanding the options

Pipkin II
The femoral head fracture extends superior to the fovea into the weight-bearing region.
Pipkin I
The femoral head fracture lies inferior to the fovea, usually outside the principal weight-bearing surface. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Pipkin III
A type I or II head fracture is associated with a femoral neck fracture. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Pipkin IV
A femoral head fracture is associated with an acetabular fracture. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Isolated femoral neck fracture
An isolated neck fracture without a head fracture is not a Pipkin head-fracture type. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
