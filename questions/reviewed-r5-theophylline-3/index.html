Severe toxicity

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with a markedly elevated theophylline concentration has repeated vomiting and then a generalized seizure. Telemetry shows a rapid arrhythmia. Which pair of complications is characteristic of serious theophylline toxicity?

  1. Isolated constipation and bradycardia
  2. Seizures and cardiac arrhythmias
  3. Gingival hyperplasia and hirsutism
  4. Ototoxicity and tendon rupture
  5. Urinary retention and fixed pinpoint pupils
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Seizures and cardiac arrhythmias

Both are potentially life-threatening manifestations of methylxanthine toxicity.

Understanding the options

Seizures and cardiac arrhythmias
Both are potentially life-threatening manifestations of methylxanthine toxicity.
Gingival hyperplasia and hirsutism
These suggest chronic phenytoin effects.
Ototoxicity and tendon rupture
This is not a characteristic theophylline toxicity pair.
Urinary retention and fixed pinpoint pupils
This does not match the usual stimulant toxicity pattern.
Isolated constipation and bradycardia
Severe theophylline toxicity commonly produces excitation and tachyarrhythmias.
