Predicting difficulty

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An unconscious adult needs urgent airway management. Examination shows marked facial swelling, limited mouth opening and restricted neck movement from prior cervical fusion. Which conclusion best uses these findings in procedural planning?

  1. The findings prove that every supraglottic device will fail
  2. Prepare for difficult ventilation or intubation and an alternative airway strategy
  3. A normal oxygen saturation would eliminate the anatomic difficulty
  4. Limited neck movement predicts difficulty only with bag-mask ventilation
  5. One favorable airway examination feature overrides the other abnormalities
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Prepare for difficult ventilation or intubation and an alternative airway strategy

Anatomic limitations should trigger preparation, experienced assistance and rescue options rather than reliance on one technique.

Understanding the options

Prepare for difficult ventilation or intubation and an alternative airway strategy
Anatomic limitations should trigger preparation, experienced assistance and rescue options rather than reliance on one technique.
A normal oxygen saturation would eliminate the anatomic difficulty
Current oxygenation does not change the limitations of access and positioning.
Limited neck movement predicts difficulty only with bag-mask ventilation
It can also impair alignment and access during laryngoscopy.
One favorable airway examination feature overrides the other abnormalities
Difficulty assessment integrates multiple findings; one reassuring feature is insufficient.
The findings prove that every supraglottic device will fail
They indicate risk, not certainty that all rescue methods will fail.
