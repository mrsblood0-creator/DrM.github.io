Behavioral change after seizure treatment

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 24-year-old man starts levetiracetam after recurrent focal seizures. At follow-up, his partner reports new irritability and aggressive outbursts beginning after treatment. He is alert, afebrile, and has no rigidity or autonomic instability. Which adverse effect should be considered first?

  1. Antimuscarinic delirium
  2. Levetiracetam-associated behavioral disturbance
  3. Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
  4. Serotonin syndrome
  5. Persistent postictal confusion
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Levetiracetam-associated behavioral disturbance

Irritability and aggression can emerge during levetiracetam treatment.

Understanding the options

Levetiracetam-associated behavioral disturbance
Irritability and aggression can emerge during levetiracetam treatment.
Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
The exposure and characteristic rigidity and systemic illness are absent.
Serotonin syndrome
There is no serotonergic exposure, clonus, or hyperreflexia described.
Persistent postictal confusion
The sustained behavioral change does not follow a discrete recent seizure.
Antimuscarinic delirium
An alert patient without autonomic antimuscarinic findings is a poor fit.
