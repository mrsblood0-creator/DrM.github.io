ORIGINAL USMLE-STYLE PRACTICE

Pressure loading of the left ventricle

A 57-year-old patient with longstanding poorly controlled hypertension has concentric left-ventricular wall thickening. Microscopy shows enlarged cardiomyocytes containing additional contractile proteins, without a substantial increase in cardiomyocyte number. Which adaptive process explains the change?

  1. Hypertrophy
  2. Hyperplasia
  3. Metaplasia
  4. Dysplasia
  5. Hydropic degeneration
Answer and explanation

Hypertrophy

Increased workload stimulates synthesis of proteins and organelles, enlarging individual cardiomyocytes.

Option explanations

Hypertrophy: Increased workload stimulates synthesis of proteins and organelles, enlarging individual cardiomyocytes.

Hyperplasia: Adult cardiomyocytes have limited proliferative capacity; increased cell size dominates this response.

Metaplasia: Metaplasia replaces one differentiated cell type with another.

Dysplasia: Dysplasia involves disordered atypical growth.

Hydropic degeneration: Hydropic degeneration is water accumulation from injury, not increased contractile substance.
