The first antioxidant conversion

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Investigators expose cultured cardiomyocytes to a burst of superoxide after transient oxygen deprivation. Adding purified superoxide dismutase lowers superoxide concentration but increases a second reactive oxygen species unless another enzyme is added. Which product accumulates?

  1. Hydrogen peroxide
  2. Hypochlorous acid
  3. Hydroxyl radical directly
  4. Reduced glutathione
  5. Carbon dioxide
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Hydrogen peroxide

Superoxide dismutase converts superoxide to hydrogen peroxide and oxygen; peroxide then requires removal by catalase or peroxidases.

Understanding the options

Hydrogen peroxide
Superoxide dismutase converts superoxide to hydrogen peroxide and oxygen; peroxide then requires removal by catalase or peroxidases.
Hypochlorous acid
Myeloperoxidase generates hypochlorous acid from peroxide and chloride.
Hydroxyl radical directly
Hydroxyl radical can arise subsequently from peroxide with redox-active iron, not directly from SOD catalysis.
Reduced glutathione
Reduced glutathione is a reducing substrate, not the product of SOD.
Carbon dioxide
Carbon dioxide is not a product of this antioxidant reaction.
