New neurologic deterioration during infusion

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

During alteplase infusion for an eligible ischemic stroke, a patient develops a severe new headache, vomiting and a declining level of consciousness. Which complication should be urgently investigated while the infusion is stopped and the treatment team responds?

  1. Symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage
  2. Expected delayed antidepressant response
  3. Isolated injection-site irritation
  4. Uncomplicated transient taste disturbance
  5. Chronic peripheral neuropathy
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage

Acute neurologic deterioration during thrombolysis raises concern for intracranial bleeding.

Understanding the options

Symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage
Acute neurologic deterioration during thrombolysis raises concern for intracranial bleeding.
Expected delayed antidepressant response
No antidepressant effect explains this emergency.
Isolated injection-site irritation
A local reaction cannot explain deteriorating consciousness.
Uncomplicated transient taste disturbance
Taste disturbance does not account for focal or global neurologic worsening.
Chronic peripheral neuropathy
Chronic neuropathy does not fit the acute central syndrome.
