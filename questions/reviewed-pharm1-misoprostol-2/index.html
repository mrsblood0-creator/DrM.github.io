Mucosal protection beyond acid reduction

In an experimental gastric mucosa preparation, an NSAID reduces prostaglandin production. Adding misoprostol partly restores mucosal resistance to acid injury. Which combination of local effects best explains this protective action?

  1. Reduced mucus and increased pepsin secretion
  2. Increased acid and reduced bicarbonate secretion
  3. Selective destruction of parietal cells
  4. Direct eradication of Helicobacter pylori
  5. Increased mucus and bicarbonate secretion
Answer and explanation

Increased mucus and bicarbonate secretion

Prostaglandin signaling supports mucus and bicarbonate defenses while misoprostol also reduces acid secretion.

Option explanations

Increased mucus and bicarbonate secretion: Prostaglandin signaling supports mucus and bicarbonate defenses while misoprostol also reduces acid secretion.

Reduced mucus and increased pepsin secretion: Reducing mucus weakens the barrier.

Increased acid and reduced bicarbonate secretion: Increasing acid while reducing bicarbonate promotes injury.

Selective destruction of parietal cells: The drug does not protect by destroying parietal cells.

Direct eradication of Helicobacter pylori: Misoprostol is not H. pylori eradication therapy.
