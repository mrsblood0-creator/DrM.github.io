Joint injury terminology: 5

A patient develops rapid knee swelling after a ligament injury. Aspiration reveals blood within the joint rather than a superficial collection outside the capsule. Which term describes the location and content of this effusion?

  1. Muscle or tendon strain
  2. Subluxation
  3. Dislocation
  4. Hemarthrosis
  5. Ligament sprain
Answer and explanation

Hemarthrosis

Blood within a joint produces an effusion after injuries such as ligament disruption or intra-articular fracture.

Option explanations

Hemarthrosis: Blood within a joint produces an effusion after injuries such as ligament disruption or intra-articular fracture.

Ligament sprain: A sprain injures a ligament supporting a joint. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Muscle or tendon strain: A strain injures a muscle-tendon unit rather than a ligament. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Subluxation: Subluxation is partial loss of joint congruity with some articular contact maintained. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Dislocation: Dislocation is complete loss of normal joint congruity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
