Clavicle fractures: 3

A patient with a displaced clavicle fracture reports tingling in the arm. The examiner evaluates pulses, capillary refill and motor and sensory function throughout the limb. Which assessment principle addresses possible injury beneath the clavicle?

  1. Protected early motion when stability permits
  2. Assess associated injuries beyond the visible fracture
  3. Document neurovascular status before and after intervention
  4. CT to define the injury and adjacent structures
  5. Urgent specialist evaluation and controlled treatment
Answer and explanation

Document neurovascular status before and after intervention

Serial motor, sensory and perfusion examinations establish baseline function and detect new deficits after reduction or splinting.
