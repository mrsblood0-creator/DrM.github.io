Post-influenza destructive pneumonia

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An 8-year-old initially improves from influenza, then develops high fever, respiratory distress and rapidly progressive pulmonary consolidation. Imaging shows cavitation and thin-walled air-filled spaces. Which pathogen is a major concern?

  1. Pneumocystis jirovecii
  2. Staphylococcus aureus
  3. Mycoplasma pneumoniae
  4. Bordetella pertussis
  5. Chlamydia trachomatis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Staphylococcus aureus

Severe post-influenza pneumonia with tissue destruction and pneumatoceles raises concern for S. aureus.

Understanding the options

Staphylococcus aureus
Severe post-influenza pneumonia with tissue destruction and pneumatoceles raises concern for S. aureus.
Mycoplasma pneumoniae
Mycoplasma is more often associated with a less destructive atypical presentation.
Bordetella pertussis
Pertussis typically causes prolonged paroxysmal cough rather than this necrotizing pattern.
Chlamydia trachomatis
Chlamydia trachomatis pneumonia is classically a young-infant illness.
Pneumocystis jirovecii
Pneumocystis is most associated with immunocompromise and diffuse interstitial disease.
