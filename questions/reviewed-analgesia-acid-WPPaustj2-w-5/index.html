Metamizole: 5

Shortly after metamizole administration, a patient develops wheeze, facial swelling and circulatory collapse. Which serious adverse-reaction category best describes this immediate multisystem event, distinct from the delayed blood-count toxicity also associated with the medicine?

  1. Nonopioid analgesic and antipyretic
  2. Agranulocytosis
  3. Stop metamizole and obtain urgent blood-count assessment
  4. Prior uneventful use does not exclude later agranulocytosis
  5. Severe hypersensitivity or anaphylaxis
Answer and explanation

Severe hypersensitivity or anaphylaxis

Metamizole can cause serious allergic reactions requiring emergency care.

Option explanations

Severe hypersensitivity or anaphylaxis: Metamizole can cause serious allergic reactions requiring emergency care.

Nonopioid analgesic and antipyretic: Metamizole is an analgesic and antipyretic available in some countries; authorization and indications vary. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Agranulocytosis: Metamizole can cause a dangerous fall in granulocytes with serious infection risk. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Stop metamizole and obtain urgent blood-count assessment: Fever, chills, sore throat or mucosal lesions during exposure warrant urgent assessment for agranulocytosis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Prior uneventful use does not exclude later agranulocytosis: The reaction can occur despite previous tolerance and is not simply predicted by the dose. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
