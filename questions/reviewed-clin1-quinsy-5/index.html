A complication beyond the tonsil

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient treated for suspected peritonsillar infection returns with increasing neck swelling below the mandibular angle and reduced neck movement. The airway is stable. The clinician suspects extension into deeper spaces. Which investigation best defines the extent?

  1. Contrast-enhanced CT of the neck
  2. Intraoral ultrasound alone
  3. Lateral neck radiography alone
  4. Noncontrast CT of the neck
  5. Repeat throat culture
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Contrast-enhanced CT of the neck

Contrast CT delineates deep-neck collections and their relationship to adjacent structures in a stable patient.

Understanding the options

Contrast-enhanced CT of the neck
Contrast CT delineates deep-neck collections and their relationship to adjacent structures in a stable patient.
Intraoral ultrasound alone
This can assess a local peritonsillar collection but may not adequately map deeper or more extensive neck spread.
Lateral neck radiography alone
A plain film is less definitive for the extent and complications of a suspected deep-space infection.
Noncontrast CT of the neck
Contrast helps delineate abscess enhancement and vascular structures; the contrast study is preferred for this question.
Repeat throat culture
Microbiology does not establish the anatomic extent of a deep collection.
