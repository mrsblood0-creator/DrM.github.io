Naming the pattern

A rhythm strip from a stable patient repeatedly shows one normal sinus complex followed by one premature wide ventricular complex. This alternating sequence continues for thirty seconds without a sustained run of ventricular beats. Which term best describes the observed pattern?

  1. Atrial flutter with 2:1 conduction
  2. Complete atrioventricular block
  3. Ventricular bigeminy
  4. Ventricular trigeminy
  5. Sustained ventricular tachycardia
Answer and explanation

Ventricular bigeminy

Bigeminy means every second beat is premature.

Option explanations

Ventricular bigeminy: Bigeminy means every second beat is premature.

Ventricular trigeminy: Trigeminy places the ectopic beat every third beat.

Sustained ventricular tachycardia: The strip alternates sinus and ectopic beats rather than a sustained ventricular run.

Atrial flutter with 2:1 conduction: Flutter requires organized rapid atrial activity.

Complete atrioventricular block: Complete block requires atrioventricular dissociation.
