Fracture mechanisms: 4

A healthy recruit abruptly increases running mileage and develops a metatarsal fracture without a single major injury. Bone quality is otherwise normal. Which mechanism best describes failure under repeated loading beyond normal repair capacity?

  1. Axial compression
  2. Traction avulsion
  3. Fatigue fracture
  4. Torsional loading
  5. Bending force
Answer and explanation

Fatigue fracture

Repetitive abnormal loading can fracture otherwise normal bone.

Option explanations

Fatigue fracture: Repetitive abnormal loading can fracture otherwise normal bone.

Torsional loading: Twisting forces commonly produce spiral shaft fractures. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Bending force: Bending creates tension on one side and compression on the other and may produce a wedge fragment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Axial compression: Axial loading can cause impaction or collapse in susceptible bone. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Traction avulsion: A forceful tendon or ligament pull can detach a bony attachment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
