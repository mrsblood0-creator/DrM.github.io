Abdominal wall anatomy: 5

A student traces the inferior border of the external oblique aponeurosis as it rolls between the anterior superior iliac spine and pubic tubercle. Which named ligament is formed by this anatomical structure?

  1. Camper fascia
  2. Scarpa fascia
  3. Rectus abdominis
  4. Transversalis fascia
  5. Inguinal ligament
Answer and explanation

Inguinal ligament

The inguinal ligament is formed by the rolled inferior border of the external oblique aponeurosis.

Option explanations

Inguinal ligament: The inguinal ligament is formed by the rolled inferior border of the external oblique aponeurosis.

Camper fascia: Camper fascia is the fatty superficial layer of abdominal subcutaneous tissue. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Scarpa fascia: Scarpa fascia is the deeper membranous layer of lower anterior abdominal subcutaneous tissue. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Rectus abdominis: Rectus abdominis is a paired vertical anterior abdominal muscle enclosed by the rectus sheath. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Transversalis fascia: The transversalis fascia lies deep to the transversus abdominis and contributes to the deep inguinal ring. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
