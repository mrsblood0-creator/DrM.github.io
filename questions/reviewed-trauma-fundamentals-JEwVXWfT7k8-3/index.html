Fracture healing outcomes: 3

Months after a fracture, pain and movement persist at the site and serial imaging shows no meaningful progression. The clinician investigates stability, biology and infection. Which outcome term best describes the suspected healing failure?

  1. Union
  2. Delayed union
  3. Malunion
  4. Refracture
  5. Nonunion
Answer and explanation

Nonunion

Persistent lack of healing progression requires assessment of stability, biology and infection.

Option explanations

Nonunion: Persistent lack of healing progression requires assessment of stability, biology and infection.

Union: Clinical stability and radiographic bridging support successful healing. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Delayed union: Healing is progressing but more slowly than expected for the injury and patient. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Malunion: The fracture has united in an unsatisfactory position. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Refracture: A new fracture occurs through a previously healed or healing region. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
