Danis-Weber classification: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An ankle radiograph shows a distal fibular fracture entirely below the distal tibiofibular syndesmosis. The clinician is classifying the fibular level before assessing other stabilizing structures. Which Danis-Weber category describes this fracture location?

  1. Weber C
  2. Maisonneuve injury
  3. Medial-sided and syndesmotic stability assessment
  4. Weber A
  5. Weber B
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Weber A

The fibular fracture lies below the syndesmosis.

Understanding the options

Weber A
The fibular fracture lies below the syndesmosis.
Weber B
The fibular fracture is at the syndesmotic level; stability depends on associated injury. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Weber C
The fibular fracture lies above the syndesmosis and raises concern for syndesmotic disruption. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Maisonneuve injury
A proximal fibular fracture with ankle syndesmotic and medial injury is a Maisonneuve pattern. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Medial-sided and syndesmotic stability assessment
The fibular fracture level alone does not establish complete ankle stability; medial and syndesmotic structures must be assessed. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
