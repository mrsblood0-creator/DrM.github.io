A peroxide-removing enzyme

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A laboratory compares peroxisomes from normal hepatocytes with those from cells lacking an antioxidant enzyme. The deficient preparation releases much less oxygen after hydrogen peroxide is added. Which enzyme activity has most likely been lost?

  1. Catalase
  2. Superoxide dismutase
  3. NADPH oxidase
  4. Myeloperoxidase
  5. Glutathione reductase
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Catalase

Catalase decomposes hydrogen peroxide into water and molecular oxygen, limiting peroxide availability for radical formation.

Understanding the options

Catalase
Catalase decomposes hydrogen peroxide into water and molecular oxygen, limiting peroxide availability for radical formation.
Superoxide dismutase
SOD uses superoxide and generates peroxide rather than removing the added peroxide.
NADPH oxidase
This enzyme generates superoxide in activated phagocytes.
Myeloperoxidase
MPO uses peroxide and chloride to form hypochlorous acid.
Glutathione reductase
This enzyme regenerates reduced glutathione from its oxidized form.
