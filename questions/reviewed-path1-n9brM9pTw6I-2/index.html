A peroxide-removing enzyme

A laboratory compares peroxisomes from normal hepatocytes with those from cells lacking an antioxidant enzyme. The deficient preparation releases much less oxygen after hydrogen peroxide is added. Which enzyme activity has most likely been lost?

  1. Catalase
  2. Superoxide dismutase
  3. NADPH oxidase
  4. Myeloperoxidase
  5. Glutathione reductase
Answer and explanation

Catalase

Catalase decomposes hydrogen peroxide into water and molecular oxygen, limiting peroxide availability for radical formation.

Option explanations

Catalase: Catalase decomposes hydrogen peroxide into water and molecular oxygen, limiting peroxide availability for radical formation.

Superoxide dismutase: SOD uses superoxide and generates peroxide rather than removing the added peroxide.

NADPH oxidase: This enzyme generates superoxide in activated phagocytes.

Myeloperoxidase: MPO uses peroxide and chloride to form hypochlorous acid.

Glutathione reductase: This enzyme regenerates reduced glutathione from its oxidized form.
