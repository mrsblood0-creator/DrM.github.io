Elbow dislocation: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

After a fall, imaging demonstrates elbow dislocation together with radial head and coronoid fractures. The orthopedic team recognizes a characteristic unstable combination. Which named injury pattern is defined by these three associated components?

  1. Terrible triad injury
  2. Simple elbow dislocation
  3. Complex elbow dislocation
  4. Brachial artery injury
  5. Post-injury elbow stiffness
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Terrible triad injury

Elbow dislocation with radial head and coronoid fractures is the terrible triad.

Understanding the options

Simple elbow dislocation

Post-injury elbow stiffness
