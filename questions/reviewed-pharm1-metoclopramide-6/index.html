Prolactin disinhibition

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 32-year-old woman develops bilateral milky nipple discharge after several weeks of metoclopramide treatment. Pregnancy testing is negative, thyroid function is normal, and she has no visual symptoms. Which endocrine mechanism most directly links the medication to this presentation?

  1. Removal of dopamine-mediated inhibition of prolactin secretion
  2. Direct activation of estrogen nuclear receptors
  3. Inhibition of pituitary ACTH release
  4. Stimulation of pancreatic insulin secretion
  5. Blockade of hypothalamic oxytocin synthesis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Removal of dopamine-mediated inhibition of prolactin secretion

Dopamine normally restrains lactotroph prolactin release; D2 blockade can cause hyperprolactinemia.

Understanding the options

Removal of dopamine-mediated inhibition of prolactin secretion
Dopamine normally restrains lactotroph prolactin release; D2 blockade can cause hyperprolactinemia.
Direct activation of estrogen nuclear receptors
Metoclopramide is not an estrogen agonist.
Inhibition of pituitary ACTH release
ACTH suppression does not explain milk secretion.
Stimulation of pancreatic insulin secretion
Insulin release is unrelated to this pituitary effect.
Blockade of hypothalamic oxytocin synthesis
Oxytocin mediates milk ejection, not the principal prolactin mechanism.
