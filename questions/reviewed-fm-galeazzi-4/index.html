galeazzi — clinical decision 4

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An adult undergoes anatomical plate fixation of a Galeazzi fracture. Despite restored radial length and alignment, the ulnar head cannot be reduced into the sigmoid notch. Repeated gentle attempts fail. What is the most appropriate next operative step?

  1. Explore the DRUJ for interposed soft tissue or another mechanical block
  2. Accept the persistent dislocation and begin rotation exercises
  3. Shorten the anatomically restored radius deliberately
  4. Apply increasingly forceful closed manipulation indefinitely
  5. Remove the radial head
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Explore the DRUJ for interposed soft tissue or another mechanical block

When anatomical radial fixation has been confirmed, an irreducible DRUJ warrants exploration for obstruction.

Understanding the options

Explore the DRUJ for interposed soft tissue or another mechanical block
When anatomical radial fixation has been confirmed, an irreducible DRUJ warrants exploration for obstruction.
Accept the persistent dislocation and begin rotation exercises
An unreduced joint should not be accepted as a stable result.
Shorten the anatomically restored radius deliberately
This sacrifices correct bony alignment without addressing the obstructing tissue.
Apply increasingly forceful closed manipulation indefinitely
Repeated force does not address interposition and can cause additional injury.
Remove the radial head
The obstruction is distal and radial head removal does not resolve it.
