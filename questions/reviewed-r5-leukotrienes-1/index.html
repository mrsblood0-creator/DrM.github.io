ORIGINAL USMLE-STYLE PRACTICE

Receptor blockade

A 16-year-old with asthma is prescribed montelukast as part of an individualized treatment plan. The clinician explains that it acts on an inflammatory mediator pathway rather than directly stimulating beta receptors. Which mechanism best describes this drug?

  1. Direct beta-2 receptor stimulation
  2. Muscarinic receptor blockade
  3. Phosphodiesterase inhibition
  4. Cysteinyl leukotriene receptor antagonism
  5. Inhibition of 5-lipoxygenase
Answer and explanation

Cysteinyl leukotriene receptor antagonism

Montelukast blocks CysLT1-mediated leukotriene effects.

Option explanations

Cysteinyl leukotriene receptor antagonism: Montelukast blocks CysLT1-mediated leukotriene effects.

Inhibition of 5-lipoxygenase: This is the mechanism of zileuton.

Direct beta-2 receptor stimulation: This describes beta agonist bronchodilators.

Muscarinic receptor blockade: This describes drugs such as ipratropium.

Phosphodiesterase inhibition: This is associated with methylxanthines.
