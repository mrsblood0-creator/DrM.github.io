A reaction after repeated dosing

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 40-year-old woman develops generalized urticaria, throat tightness and hypotension shortly after an omalizumab injection. She previously tolerated several injections, and she has no new food exposure or other medication use that day. Which interpretation best fits this presentation?

  1. The findings are expected therapeutic effects
  2. The reaction can be excluded if wheezing is absent
  3. Anaphylaxis remains possible despite prior tolerated doses
  4. Prior tolerance excludes a reaction to omalizumab
  5. Isolated injection-site irritation explains the hypotension
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Anaphylaxis remains possible despite prior tolerated doses

Previous tolerance does not exclude a later severe allergic reaction.

Understanding the options

Anaphylaxis remains possible despite prior tolerated doses
Previous tolerance does not exclude a later severe allergic reaction.
Prior tolerance excludes a reaction to omalizumab
Reactions can occur after more than one dose.
Isolated injection-site irritation explains the hypotension
Local irritation does not explain airway and circulatory involvement.
The findings are expected therapeutic effects
Airway symptoms and hypotension are not desired effects.
The reaction can be excluded if wheezing is absent
Anaphylaxis need not include every possible respiratory sign.
