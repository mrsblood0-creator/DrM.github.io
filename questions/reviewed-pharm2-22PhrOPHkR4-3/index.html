Constipation with warning signs

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 47-year-old woman taking clozapine has had no bowel movement for 6 days and now has abdominal pain, distension and vomiting. Bowel sounds are reduced. Which complication best explains why this presentation needs urgent assessment?

  1. A mild withdrawal syndrome from an omitted single dose
  2. Benign appetite stimulation alone
  3. Severe gastrointestinal hypomotility with possible ileus
  4. Uncomplicated functional diarrhea
  5. Isolated increased salivation
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Severe gastrointestinal hypomotility with possible ileus

Clozapine-associated bowel hypomotility can progress to obstruction, ischemia or perforation; warning symptoms must not be dismissed as simple constipation.

Understanding the options

Severe gastrointestinal hypomotility with possible ileus
Clozapine-associated bowel hypomotility can progress to obstruction, ischemia or perforation; warning symptoms must not be dismissed as simple constipation.
Uncomplicated functional diarrhea
The absence of bowel movements and distension do not fit.
Isolated increased salivation
Salivation does not explain impaired bowel transit with vomiting.
A mild withdrawal syndrome from an omitted single dose
The presentation is a potential abdominal emergency, not adequately explained by that assumption.
Benign appetite stimulation alone
Appetite changes do not explain the obstructive symptom pattern.
