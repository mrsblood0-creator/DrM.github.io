Orthopedic trauma history and examination: 5

A severely deformed leg attracts immediate attention after a collision, but the patient also has abnormal breathing and abdominal tenderness. Which assessment principle prevents the conspicuous limb injury from obscuring other potentially fatal injuries?

  1. Clarify mechanism and energy of injury
  2. Inspect the skin for an open injury
  3. Document a neurovascular examination
  4. Repeat examinations when symptoms evolve
  5. Complete the broader trauma assessment
Answer and explanation

Complete the broader trauma assessment

A dramatic limb injury must not distract from other life-threatening injuries.

Option explanations

Complete the broader trauma assessment: A dramatic limb injury must not distract from other life-threatening injuries.

Clarify mechanism and energy of injury: Mechanism helps predict associated injury, instability and the need for broader assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Inspect the skin for an open injury: A small communicating wound can make a fracture open even without visible exposed bone. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Document a neurovascular examination: Motor, sensory and perfusion findings should be recorded before and after interventions. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Repeat examinations when symptoms evolve: Swelling, pain and neurologic findings can change; a normal initial examination does not end surveillance. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
