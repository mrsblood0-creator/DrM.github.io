Medication reconciliation before treatment

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient beginning enoxaparin for postoperative thromboprophylaxis reports taking ibuprofen regularly for arthritis and aspirin without a current prescription. Which issue is most important to review before continuing this combination?

  1. Additive impairment of hemostasis and bleeding risk
  2. Neutralization of enoxaparin by acidic drugs
  3. Complete loss of antithrombin binding
  4. Mandatory development of immune HIT
  5. Prevention of all gastrointestinal bleeding
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Additive impairment of hemostasis and bleeding risk

NSAIDs and antiplatelet agents can add bleeding risk during anticoagulation.

Understanding the options

Additive impairment of hemostasis and bleeding risk
NSAIDs and antiplatelet agents can add bleeding risk during anticoagulation.
Neutralization of enoxaparin by acidic drugs
Acidic oral medicines do not simply neutralize injected enoxaparin.
Complete loss of antithrombin binding
The interaction is not complete loss of antithrombin binding.
Mandatory development of immune HIT
HIT is an immune syndrome, not an inevitable consequence of this combination.
Prevention of all gastrointestinal bleeding
These agents can increase rather than prevent gastrointestinal bleeding.
