Statins: 3

A patient taking a statin develops diffuse muscle pain, weakness and dark urine with markedly elevated creatine kinase. Which serious adverse-effect category should be considered rather than treating the symptoms as routine exercise soreness?

  1. Statin-associated muscle injury
  2. HMG-CoA reductase inhibition
  3. Increased hepatic LDL receptor expression
  4. Increased statin exposure through a drug interaction
  5. Reduction of atherosclerotic cardiovascular risk
Answer and explanation

Statin-associated muscle injury

New muscle pain with marked CK elevation requires assessment for myopathy or rhabdomyolysis.

Option explanations

Statin-associated muscle injury: New muscle pain with marked CK elevation requires assessment for myopathy or rhabdomyolysis.

HMG-CoA reductase inhibition: Statins inhibit the rate-limiting enzyme in hepatic cholesterol synthesis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Increased hepatic LDL receptor expression: Reduced hepatic cholesterol promotes LDL receptor expression and circulating LDL clearance. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Increased statin exposure through a drug interaction: CYP3A4 inhibitors can increase exposure to susceptible statins such as simvastatin. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Reduction of atherosclerotic cardiovascular risk: Appropriate statin therapy reduces cardiovascular events, not merely the laboratory LDL number. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
