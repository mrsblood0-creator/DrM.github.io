Alternative diagnosis with treatment failure

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A child initially diagnosed with croup develops a temperature of 40°C, toxic appearance and worsening stridor. Two appropriately delivered epinephrine treatments produce little improvement, and thick secretions are present. Which alternative diagnosis deserves urgent assessment?

  1. Bacterial tracheitis
  2. Mild spasmodic croup
  3. Uncomplicated seasonal rhinitis
  4. Isolated exercise-induced asthma
  5. A simple postviral cough
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Bacterial tracheitis

High fever, toxicity, purulent secretions and poor response suggest bacterial tracheal infection and possible airway compromise.

Understanding the options

Bacterial tracheitis
High fever, toxicity, purulent secretions and poor response suggest bacterial tracheal infection and possible airway compromise.
Mild spasmodic croup
Spasmodic croup lacks this infectious-toxic pattern.
Uncomplicated seasonal rhinitis
Rhinitis does not explain severe stridor and systemic deterioration.
Isolated exercise-induced asthma
Exercise-induced asthma produces lower-airway symptoms associated with exertion.
A simple postviral cough
A simple residual cough does not cause this airway emergency.
