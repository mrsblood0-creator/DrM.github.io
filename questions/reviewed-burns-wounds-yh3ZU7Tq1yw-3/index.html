Burn surface-area assessment: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A young child has multiple burns, and an examiner proposes applying adult body-region percentages unchanged. Which assessment method better accounts for the child’s different head and limb proportions when estimating total burned surface?

  1. 4.5% TBSA
  2. 36% TBSA
  3. Age-adjusted Lund-Browder assessment
  4. 9% TBSA
  5. 18% TBSA
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Age-adjusted Lund-Browder assessment

Children have different body proportions, so age-adjusted assessment is more appropriate than unmodified adult proportions.
