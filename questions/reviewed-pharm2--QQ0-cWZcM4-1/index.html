Growth trajectory

An 11-year-old boy has improved classroom attention after 6 months of mixed amphetamine salts. His parents report that he rarely eats lunch. His weight percentile has fallen from the 55th to the 25th percentile. Which follow-up measure is most important for evaluating this treatment-related concern?

  1. Routine monthly absolute neutrophil counts
  2. Daily serum creatine kinase testing
  3. Scheduled serum prolactin measurement
  4. Annual weight measurement without interim review
  5. Serial height and weight measurements with nutritional and treatment review
Answer and explanation

Serial height and weight measurements with nutritional and treatment review

Appetite suppression and reduced growth velocity warrant assessment of the trajectory rather than reassurance based on a single weight.

Option explanations

Serial height and weight measurements with nutritional and treatment review: Appetite suppression and reduced growth velocity warrant assessment of the trajectory rather than reassurance based on a single weight.

Routine monthly absolute neutrophil counts: Neutropenia surveillance is not the characteristic monitoring response to stimulant-associated weight loss.

Daily serum creatine kinase testing: There are no signs of muscle injury that would justify this as the primary measure.

Scheduled serum prolactin measurement: D2-blockade hyperprolactinemia is not the expected cause of this pattern.

Annual weight measurement without interim review: The documented decline requires closer assessment.
