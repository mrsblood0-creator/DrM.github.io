Selecting a sensitive toxicity measure

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 23-year-old woman is evaluated after a possible imipramine ingestion. Her family asks whether a plasma drug level can establish that she is safe for discharge. She is drowsy but arousable. Which assessment is most useful for detecting the immediate conduction hazard?

  1. Serial ECG assessment including QRS duration
  2. A single plasma imipramine concentration alone
  3. A normal serum alanine aminotransferase alone
  4. A normal platelet count alone
  5. The presence of normal pupil size alone
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Serial ECG assessment including QRS duration

Clinical condition and ECG findings guide acute risk assessment; a drug level alone cannot establish safety.

Understanding the options

Serial ECG assessment including QRS duration
Clinical condition and ECG findings guide acute risk assessment; a drug level alone cannot establish safety.
A single plasma imipramine concentration alone
Concentration alone should not guide management of tricyclic poisoning.
A normal serum alanine aminotransferase alone
Liver enzymes do not exclude acute cardiac conduction toxicity.
A normal platelet count alone
Platelet count does not assess this poisoning mechanism.
The presence of normal pupil size alone
Absence of a classic anticholinergic sign does not exclude dangerous toxicity.
