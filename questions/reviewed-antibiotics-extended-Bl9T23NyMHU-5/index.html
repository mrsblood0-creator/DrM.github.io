Cephalosporin pharmacology: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A trainee assumes all cephalosporins are ineffective against MRSA because of PBP2a. The supervisor identifies a specific exception with affinity for that altered protein. Which listed cephalosporin is the exception relevant to a susceptible isolate?

  1. Ceftaroline
  2. Cefazolin
  3. Cefoxitin
  4. Ceftriaxone
  5. Ceftazidime
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Ceftaroline

Ceftaroline binds PBP2a and can cover MRSA, unlike most traditional cephalosporins.

Understanding the options

Ceftaroline
Ceftaroline binds PBP2a and can cover MRSA, unlike most traditional cephalosporins.
Cefazolin
Cefazolin is a first-generation cephalosporin used for MSSA and common perioperative prophylaxis indications. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Cefoxitin
Cefoxitin is a cephamycin commonly grouped with second-generation cephalosporins and has anaerobic activity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Ceftriaxone
Ceftriaxone is a third-generation cephalosporin used for selected susceptible invasive infections; it is not antipseudomonal. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Ceftazidime
Ceftazidime is a third-generation cephalosporin with antipseudomonal activity when susceptible. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
