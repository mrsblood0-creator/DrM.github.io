Hernia progression and contents: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Only a portion of the bowel wall is trapped in a small hernia defect. The patient can still pass intestinal contents, yet the entrapped wall is ischemic. Which hernia pattern explains this apparent mismatch?

  1. Bowel obstruction within a hernia
  2. Strangulated hernia
  3. Richter hernia
  4. Reducible hernia
  5. Irreducible or incarcerated hernia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Richter hernia

Entrapment of only part of the bowel circumference can cause ischemia without complete luminal obstruction.

Understanding the options

Richter hernia
Entrapment of only part of the bowel circumference can cause ischemia without complete luminal obstruction.
Reducible hernia
The protruding contents can return to the abdominal cavity. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Irreducible or incarcerated hernia
The contents cannot be returned; this does not by itself prove ischemia but requires assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Bowel obstruction within a hernia
Entrapped bowel can obstruct, producing vomiting, distension and failure to pass stool or flatus. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Strangulated hernia
Compromised blood supply threatens tissue viability and requires urgent surgical assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
