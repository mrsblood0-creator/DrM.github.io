Deterioration before imaging

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A child with suspected retropharyngeal infection develops severe stridor, increasing drowsiness, and poor air movement. A CT scan has been requested, but transport has not begun. Which intervention should now take priority?

  1. Emergency airway management with experienced anesthesia and ENT support
  2. Proceed to the scheduled CT before airway intervention
  3. Give IV antibiotics and wait for swelling to decrease
  4. Use nebulized epinephrine as definitive treatment
  5. Perform bedside needle aspiration before securing airway support
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Emergency airway management with experienced anesthesia and ENT support

Airway compromise is immediately life-threatening and takes precedence over defining the abscess radiographically.

Understanding the options

Emergency airway management with experienced anesthesia and ENT support
Airway compromise is immediately life-threatening and takes precedence over defining the abscess radiographically.
Proceed to the scheduled CT before airway intervention
Transport and positioning can precipitate complete obstruction; stabilization takes priority.
Give IV antibiotics and wait for swelling to decrease
Antibiotics are necessary but cannot promptly reverse critically compromised airflow.
Use nebulized epinephrine as definitive treatment
Temporary reduction of mucosal edema cannot reliably resolve a space-occupying deep-neck infection with respiratory failure.
Perform bedside needle aspiration before securing airway support
Unplanned instrumentation may worsen obstruction or cause aspiration; expert airway planning is essential.
