Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A newborn with gastroschisis has bowel abnormalities that complicate feeding and surgical management. Which associated intestinal problem should be considered even though the pattern of chromosomal associations differs from that of omphalocele?

  1. Assess associated structural and chromosomal abnormalities
  2. Protect exposed or vulnerable viscera and prevent heat and fluid loss
  3. Associated intestinal injury or atresia
  4. Omphalocele
  5. Gastroschisis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Associated intestinal injury or atresia

Gastroschisis can coexist with bowel damage or atresia despite fewer chromosomal associations than omphalocele.
