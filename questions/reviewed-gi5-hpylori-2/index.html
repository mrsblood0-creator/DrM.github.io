Regimen components

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient receives optimized bismuth quadruple therapy for H. pylori infection. The pharmacist reviews the medicines to confirm that all four components are present. Which combination correctly lists the components of this regimen?

  1. PPI, bismuth, tetracycline and metronidazole
  2. PPI, bismuth, aspirin and acetaminophen
  3. Bismuth, famotidine, sucralfate and an antacid
  4. PPI, clarithromycin, amoxicillin and no fourth drug
  5. Metronidazole, rifampin, isoniazid and pyrazinamide
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

PPI, bismuth, tetracycline and metronidazole

Optimized BQT combines acid suppression, bismuth and two antibiotics.

Understanding the options

PPI, bismuth, tetracycline and metronidazole
Optimized BQT combines acid suppression, bismuth and two antibiotics.
PPI, bismuth, aspirin and acetaminophen
The analgesics do not replace the eradication antibiotics.
Bismuth, famotidine, sucralfate and an antacid
This omits the required antibiotic components.
PPI, clarithromycin, amoxicillin and no fourth drug
This describes a different triple regimen rather than BQT.
Metronidazole, rifampin, isoniazid and pyrazinamide
This is not an H. pylori bismuth quadruple regimen.
