Wound-healing intention: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A wound with substantial tissue loss is left open and gradually fills with granulation tissue before contraction and epithelialization reduce the defect. Which mode of healing best describes repair across this larger open gap?

  1. Granulation tissue formation
  2. Collagen remodeling
  3. Secondary intention
  4. Primary intention
  5. Delayed primary closure
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Secondary intention

An open defect heals through granulation, contraction and epithelialization.

Understanding the options

Secondary intention
An open defect heals through granulation, contraction and epithelialization.
Primary intention
Clean edges are approximated so healing occurs across a small tissue gap. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Delayed primary closure
A wound is initially left open and later closed after reassessment and appropriate wound care. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Granulation tissue formation
New capillaries and fibroblasts form the vascular connective tissue of the proliferative phase. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Collagen remodeling
Later remodeling changes collagen organization and strength; healed tissue does not instantly regain normal tensile strength. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
