Wound-healing intention: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A clean surgical incision has closely approximated edges after closure. The tissue gap is small and healing proceeds across the aligned surfaces. Which mode of wound healing best describes this planned postoperative repair?

  1. Delayed primary closure
  2. Granulation tissue formation
  3. Collagen remodeling
  4. Primary intention
  5. Secondary intention
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Primary intention

Clean edges are approximated so healing occurs across a small tissue gap.

Understanding the options

Primary intention
Clean edges are approximated so healing occurs across a small tissue gap.
Secondary intention
An open defect heals through granulation, contraction and epithelialization. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Delayed primary closure
A wound is initially left open and later closed after reassessment and appropriate wound care. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Granulation tissue formation
New capillaries and fibroblasts form the vascular connective tissue of the proliferative phase. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Collagen remodeling
Later remodeling changes collagen organization and strength; healed tissue does not instantly regain normal tensile strength. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
