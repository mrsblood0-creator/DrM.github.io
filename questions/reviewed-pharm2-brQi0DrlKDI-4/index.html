Cough medicine with sedatives

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An older adult taking diazepam begins codeine syrup for cough and drinks wine that evening. Family members find the patient difficult to awaken with shallow respirations. Which interaction most directly accounts for the presentation?

  1. Additive central nervous system depression
  2. Competitive blockade of opioid receptors
  3. Accelerated glucuronidation of morphine
  4. Increased acetylcholine release at neuromuscular junctions
  5. Selective inhibition of peripheral histamine release
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Additive central nervous system depression

Alcohol and benzodiazepines add to opioid sedation and respiratory depression.

Understanding the options

Additive central nervous system depression
Alcohol and benzodiazepines add to opioid sedation and respiratory depression.
Competitive blockade of opioid receptors
Opioid antagonism would reverse rather than intensify depression.
Accelerated glucuronidation of morphine
Faster clearance would not explain this pharmacodynamic combination.
Increased acetylcholine release at neuromuscular junctions
Neuromuscular acetylcholine release is not the shared mechanism.
Selective inhibition of peripheral histamine release
Histamine effects do not account for profound central depression.
