Infectious mononucleosis: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An athlete with recent mononucleosis and splenic enlargement wants to return immediately to contact sport. Which complication explains the need for activity restriction and medical clearance rather than relying only on improved throat symptoms?

  1. Reactive cytotoxic T lymphocytes
  2. Early negative heterophile testing does not exclude EBV
  3. An aminopenicillin-associated rash during EBV infection
  4. Splenic rupture risk
  5. Epstein-Barr virus infectious mononucleosis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Splenic rupture risk

Splenic enlargement during mononucleosis makes contact sports or abdominal trauma hazardous until medically cleared.

Understanding the options

Reactive cytotoxic T lymphocytes
