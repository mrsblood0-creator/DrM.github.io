Suture packet identification: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Two suture packets have similar colors but different materials and absorption profiles. Which packet information should be read directly rather than relying on brand appearance to decide how long tissue support will remain?

  1. Check expiry and storage conditions
  2. Confirm the labeled suture diameter
  3. Confirm needle type and dimensions
  4. Read material and absorbability on the label
  5. Check package integrity before use
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Read material and absorbability on the label

Brand color alone is not sufficient to determine composition, absorption or support duration.
