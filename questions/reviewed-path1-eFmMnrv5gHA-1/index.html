Normal arterial oxygen tension with tissue hypoxia

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A woman with prolonged heavy menstrual bleeding has a hemoglobin concentration of 5.8 g/dL. Her arterial oxygen tension and hemoglobin oxygen saturation are normal, but she develops exertional chest discomfort. Which abnormality best explains impaired myocardial oxygen delivery?

  1. Reduced oxygen content from decreased hemoglobin mass
  2. Reduced oxygen diffusion across alveoli
  3. Complete inhibition of mitochondrial complex IV
  4. A right-to-left intracardiac shunt
  5. Reduced inspired oxygen partial pressure
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Reduced oxygen content from decreased hemoglobin mass

Most blood oxygen is hemoglobin-bound; normal saturation cannot compensate for markedly reduced hemoglobin.

Understanding the options

Reduced oxygen content from decreased hemoglobin mass
Most blood oxygen is hemoglobin-bound; normal saturation cannot compensate for markedly reduced hemoglobin.
Reduced oxygen diffusion across alveoli
A diffusion defect would tend to lower arterial oxygen tension.
Complete inhibition of mitochondrial complex IV
This describes histotoxic hypoxia, not isolated anemia.
A right-to-left intracardiac shunt
A substantial shunt generally causes arterial hypoxemia.
Reduced inspired oxygen partial pressure
Low inspired oxygen would lower arterial oxygen tension.
