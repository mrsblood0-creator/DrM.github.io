Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient taking an irreversible monoamine oxidase inhibitor is offered nefopam for pain. Which important interaction restriction should be recognized before dispensing rather than assuming all nonopioid analgesics are compatible with antidepressant therapy?

  1. MAO inhibitor interaction
  2. Central nonopioid analgesia
  3. Seizure risk
  4. Antimuscarinic-type adverse effects
  5. Tachycardia and sweating
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

MAO inhibitor interaction

Nefopam should not be combined with MAO inhibitors under its prescribing restrictions.

Understanding the options

MAO inhibitor interaction
Nefopam should not be combined with MAO inhibitors under its prescribing restrictions.
Central nonopioid analgesia
Nefopam has centrally mediated analgesic effects; monoamine handling is implicated, but its mechanism is not fully defined. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Seizure risk
Nefopam is contraindicated in patients with a history of convulsive disorders in relevant product labeling. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Antimuscarinic-type adverse effects
Dry mouth and urinary retention can occur; angle-closure glaucoma warrants caution. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
