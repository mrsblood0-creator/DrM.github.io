Gastric outlet obstruction

A patient with long-standing ulcer disease has progressive early satiety and recurrent vomiting of food eaten hours earlier. Endoscopy shows narrowing at the pyloric channel. Which ulcer complication best explains impaired gastric emptying in this setting?

  1. Uncomplicated biliary colic
  2. A primary swallowing disorder
  3. Gastric outlet obstruction
  4. Isolated reflux esophagitis
  5. A lower colonic stricture
Answer and explanation

Gastric outlet obstruction

Inflammation and scarring near the outlet can narrow the passage; malignancy must also be considered.

Option explanations

Gastric outlet obstruction: Inflammation and scarring near the outlet can narrow the passage; malignancy must also be considered.

Isolated reflux esophagitis: This does not explain the pyloric narrowing.

A lower colonic stricture: The demonstrated obstruction is at the gastric outlet.

Uncomplicated biliary colic: This does not create a pyloric narrowing.

A primary swallowing disorder: The problem is delayed gastric emptying after swallowing.
