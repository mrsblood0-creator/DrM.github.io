Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A singer with asthma develops hoarseness after starting regular inhaled corticosteroid therapy. There is no fever, stridor or new neck mass. Which adverse effect should be considered while reviewing her inhaler technique and treatment needs?

  1. Acute cholinergic poisoning
  2. ICS-associated dysphonia
  3. Aminoglycoside-associated vestibular injury
  4. Beta-blocker withdrawal
  5. Opioid-induced chest wall rigidity
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

ICS-associated dysphonia

Local steroid effects can cause hoarseness; technique and dosing should be reviewed.

Understanding the options

ICS-associated dysphonia
Local steroid effects can cause hoarseness; technique and dosing should be reviewed.
Aminoglycoside-associated vestibular injury
No aminoglycoside exposure or vestibular symptoms are described.
Beta-blocker withdrawal
The medication and symptom pattern do not support this.
Opioid-induced chest wall rigidity
That is not a typical local inhaled-steroid effect.
Acute cholinergic poisoning
The expected secretory syndrome is absent.
