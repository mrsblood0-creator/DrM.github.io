MAOI transition

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 48-year-old man stopped phenelzine yesterday because of adverse effects. He asks to begin bupropion today. He is clinically stable, and an alternative follow-up plan can be arranged. Which response best addresses the interaction?

  1. Avoid all future antidepressant treatment permanently
  2. Allow the required 14-day interval after the MAOI before starting bupropion
  3. Begin both drugs together at half doses
  4. Start bupropion after a single missed phenelzine dose
  5. Add propranolol and start bupropion immediately
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Allow the required 14-day interval after the MAOI before starting bupropion

An adequate washout avoids the contraindicated overlap and risk of hypertensive reactions.

Understanding the options

Allow the required 14-day interval after the MAOI before starting bupropion
An adequate washout avoids the contraindicated overlap and risk of hypertensive reactions.
Begin both drugs together at half doses
Dose reduction does not make the contraindicated overlap acceptable.
Start bupropion after a single missed phenelzine dose
Irreversible MAO inhibition persists beyond one dosing interval.
Add propranolol and start bupropion immediately
Adding another drug does not remove the contraindication.
Avoid all future antidepressant treatment permanently
A properly planned transition remains possible.
