Choosing a dose-adjustment variable

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 68-year-old woman with focal epilepsy is prescribed levetiracetam. She has chronic kidney disease and normal hepatic tests. The clinician reviews laboratory data before choosing her maintenance regimen. Which measurement is most directly relevant to the labeled renal dose adjustment?

  1. Estimated creatinine clearance
  2. Serum alanine aminotransferase
  3. International normalized ratio
  4. Serum albumin alone
  5. Serum alkaline phosphatase
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Estimated creatinine clearance

Levetiracetam dosing in adults with renal impairment is adjusted using creatinine clearance.

Understanding the options

Estimated creatinine clearance
Levetiracetam dosing in adults with renal impairment is adjusted using creatinine clearance.
Serum alanine aminotransferase
A hepatic injury marker does not quantify renal drug clearance.
International normalized ratio
This measures coagulation and is not the renal dosing variable.
Serum albumin alone
Albumin alone does not estimate renal filtration.
Serum alkaline phosphatase
This does not guide the labeled renal adjustment.
