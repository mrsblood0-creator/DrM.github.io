Breast enlargement with an older H2 blocker

A 52-year-old man treated for several months with high-dose cimetidine develops tender bilateral breast enlargement. Examination reveals no testicular mass, and evaluation does not identify liver disease. Which property of cimetidine best explains this recognized endocrine adverse effect?

  1. Weak antiandrogenic activity
  2. Selective estrogen receptor blockade
  3. Irreversible aromatase inhibition
  4. Direct suppression of prolactin release
  5. Inhibition of all adrenal steroid production
Answer and explanation

Weak antiandrogenic activity

Cimetidine has antiandrogenic effects and is associated with gynecomastia, especially with longer or higher-dose exposure.

Option explanations

Weak antiandrogenic activity: Cimetidine has antiandrogenic effects and is associated with gynecomastia, especially with longer or higher-dose exposure.

Selective estrogen receptor blockade: Estrogen blockade would not explain this effect.

Irreversible aromatase inhibition: Aromatase inhibition reduces estrogen synthesis.

Direct suppression of prolactin release: Prolactin suppression is not the characteristic explanation.

Inhibition of all adrenal steroid production: Cimetidine is not a global steroidogenesis inhibitor.
