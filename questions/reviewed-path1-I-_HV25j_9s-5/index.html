A receptor-mediated death signal

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

An activated lymphocyte expresses Fas ligand and contacts a target cell expressing Fas. Receptor-associated adaptor proteins assemble a death-inducing signaling complex before executioner caspases are activated. Which initiator enzyme is most directly recruited in this pathway?

  1. Lysyl oxidase
  2. Caspase-8
  3. Caspase-9
  4. Catalase
  5. DNA polymerase
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Caspase-8

Fas signaling activates the extrinsic apoptotic pathway through adaptor proteins and initiator caspase-8.

Understanding the options

Caspase-8
Fas signaling activates the extrinsic apoptotic pathway through adaptor proteins and initiator caspase-8.
Caspase-9
Caspase-9 is associated with mitochondrial apoptosome signaling.
Catalase
Catalase detoxifies peroxide.
DNA polymerase
DNA polymerase synthesizes DNA.
Lysyl oxidase
Lysyl oxidase cross-links extracellular matrix proteins.
