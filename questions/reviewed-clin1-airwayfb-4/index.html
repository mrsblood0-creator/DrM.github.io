Collapse distal to obstruction

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 3-year-old has persistent cough after an unwitnessed choking event. Imaging reveals right middle-lobe volume loss and displacement of a fissure toward the affected lobe. Bronchoscopy identifies an obstructing seed. Which process caused the radiographic change?

  1. Resorption of gas distal to complete bronchial obstruction
  2. Increased air entry through a ball-valve lesion
  3. Primary pleural air accumulation
  4. Increased pulmonary arterial flow
  5. Diffuse interstitial fluid accumulation
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Resorption of gas distal to complete bronchial obstruction

When ventilation is blocked, distal gas is absorbed into blood and the affected lung segment collapses.

Understanding the options

Resorption of gas distal to complete bronchial obstruction
When ventilation is blocked, distal gas is absorbed into blood and the affected lung segment collapses.
Increased air entry through a ball-valve lesion
A ball-valve lesion usually causes air trapping rather than volume loss.
Primary pleural air accumulation
Pleural air compresses lung but is not the mechanism described by a bronchial seed.
Increased pulmonary arterial flow
Hyperemia alone does not cause lobar volume loss.
Diffuse interstitial fluid accumulation
Diffuse edema does not explain focal obstructive collapse.
