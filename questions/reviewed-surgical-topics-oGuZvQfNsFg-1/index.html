Needle-driver control: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

During supervised simulation, a learner clamps the needle tip and then notices poor penetration. Which handling principle better preserves the point and attachment while maintaining control of the needle with the driver?

  1. Follow the needle’s curvature
  2. Use controlled grip and avoid excessive crushing force
  3. Practice controlled ratchet release on a simulator
  4. Use a controlled sharps-handling process
  5. Hold the needle securely by its body away from the tip and attachment
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Hold the needle securely by its body away from the tip and attachment

Grasping the point can blunt it, while inappropriate loading near the attachment can damage the needle-suture unit.

Understanding the options
