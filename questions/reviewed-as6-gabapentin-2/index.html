A calcium-channel binding site

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 58-year-old man has persistent burning pain after shingles and starts gabapentin. During counseling, he asks whether its structural similarity to GABA means it directly stimulates GABA receptors. Which target best describes its pharmacologic binding site?

  1. Mu-opioid receptor
  2. Cyclooxygenase active site
  3. Alpha-2-delta subunit of voltage-gated calcium channels
  4. GABA-A benzodiazepine site
  5. GABA-B receptor
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Alpha-2-delta subunit of voltage-gated calcium channels

Gabapentin binds the auxiliary alpha-2-delta calcium-channel subunit rather than acting as a direct GABA agonist.

Understanding the options

Alpha-2-delta subunit of voltage-gated calcium channels
Gabapentin binds the auxiliary alpha-2-delta calcium-channel subunit rather than acting as a direct GABA agonist.
GABA-A benzodiazepine site
This is the target of benzodiazepines, not gabapentin.
GABA-B receptor
Baclofen is a direct GABA-B agonist.
Mu-opioid receptor
Gabapentin is not an opioid receptor agonist.
Cyclooxygenase active site
Gabapentin is not an NSAID.
