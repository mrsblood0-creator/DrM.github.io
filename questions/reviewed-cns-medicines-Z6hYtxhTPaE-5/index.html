Antipsychotic principles: 5

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Soon after an antipsychotic dose change, a patient develops fever, diffuse rigidity, confusion and autonomic instability. The clinician arranges urgent assessment rather than routine outpatient dose adjustment. Which serious syndrome is the key concern?

  1. Drug-induced parkinsonism
  2. Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
  3. Parkinsonism-hyperpyrexia syndrome
  4. Acute dystonia
  5. Akathisia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Neuroleptic malignant syndrome

Fever, generalized rigidity, altered mental status, autonomic instability and elevated CK after dopamine blockade suggest NMS.

Understanding the options

Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
Fever, generalized rigidity, altered mental status, autonomic instability and elevated CK after dopamine blockade suggest NMS.
Parkinsonism-hyperpyrexia syndrome
Abrupt withdrawal of dopaminergic therapy can produce a dangerous NMS-like syndrome with rigidity, hyperthermia and autonomic instability. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Acute dystonia
Sustained painful muscle contractions such as torticollis or oculogyric crisis can follow dopamine-blocking treatment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Akathisia
Subjective inner restlessness with pacing or inability to sit still can be a drug effect rather than worsening psychosis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Drug-induced parkinsonism
Nigrostriatal dopamine blockade can cause bradykinesia, rigidity and tremor. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
