Late joint complications of fractures: 5

A clinician reviews a healed joint-adjacent fracture with persistent pain. The films are examined for a residual step in the joint surface that could alter load distribution. Which aspect of the original injury is being reassessed?

  1. Assess extension into the joint surface
  2. Obtain adequate orthogonal views
  3. Include adjacent joints when indicated
  4. Describe distal-fragment position relative to the proximal fragment
  5. Inspect soft tissues as well as bone
Answer and explanation

Assess extension into the joint surface

Articular involvement affects congruity, prognosis and treatment planning.
