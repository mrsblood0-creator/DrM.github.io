A new seizure type

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 10-year-old boy takes ethosuximide for absence seizures. His staring spells improve, but he subsequently has a generalized tonic-clonic seizure. The clinician reassesses his epilepsy syndrome and treatment. Which limitation of ethosuximide is most relevant?

  1. It permanently eliminates all generalized epilepsy
  2. It has no effect on absence seizures
  3. It does not provide reliable control of generalized tonic-clonic seizures
  4. It treats only focal motor seizures
  5. It is a rescue anesthetic with no maintenance role
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

It does not provide reliable control of generalized tonic-clonic seizures

Ethosuximide has a narrow absence-seizure spectrum; mixed seizure types require reassessment of treatment.

Understanding the options
