Observation after epinephrine

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A preschooler’s stridor resolves after nebulized epinephrine and dexamethasone. Twenty minutes later, the parent requests immediate discharge. Why is a period of observation still necessary despite the initial improvement in breathing?

  1. Symptoms may recur when the short-lived epinephrine effect wanes
  2. Epinephrine reliably produces airway swelling worse than baseline
  3. Dexamethasone works only after several days
  4. Initial epinephrine response excludes viral croup
  5. All patients receiving epinephrine require overnight admission
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Symptoms may recur when the short-lived epinephrine effect wanes

Observation establishes sustained improvement as the short-lived epinephrine effect wanes; the required interval follows local guidance.

Understanding the options

Symptoms may recur when the short-lived epinephrine effect wanes
Observation establishes sustained improvement as the short-lived epinephrine effect wanes; the required interval follows local guidance.
Epinephrine reliably produces airway swelling worse than baseline
Recurrence as the effect wanes is the concern; obligatory worsening beyond baseline is not the rationale.
Dexamethasone works only after several days
Corticosteroid benefit develops within hours; a claim of no effect for days is inaccurate.
Initial epinephrine response excludes viral croup
A response is compatible with croup and does not invalidate the diagnosis.
All patients receiving epinephrine require overnight admission
Admission depends on sustained response, severity and safe follow-up rather than the drug having been used alone.
