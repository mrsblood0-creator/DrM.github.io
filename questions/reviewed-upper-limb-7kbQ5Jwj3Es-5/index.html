Distal humerus fractures: 5

A patient recovering from an intra-articular distal humerus fracture develops marked limitation of elbow flexion and extension. Union has occurred, but prolonged protection and capsular scarring have restricted motion. Which common complication is described?

  1. Acute compartment syndrome
  2. Joint stiffness
  3. Osteonecrosis
  4. Nonunion
  5. Post-traumatic osteoarthritis
Answer and explanation

Joint stiffness

Capsular contracture, adhesions and prolonged immobilization can restrict motion after injury.

Option explanations

Joint stiffness: Capsular contracture, adhesions and prolonged immobilization can restrict motion after injury.

Osteonecrosis: Disruption of blood supply can lead to death and collapse of an injured bony segment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Nonunion: Persistent failure of fracture healing requires assessment of stability, biology, infection and patient factors. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Post-traumatic osteoarthritis: Articular incongruity or cartilage damage can cause later degenerative joint changes. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Acute compartment syndrome: Rising compartment pressure impairs perfusion; escalating pain and pain on passive stretch require urgent assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
