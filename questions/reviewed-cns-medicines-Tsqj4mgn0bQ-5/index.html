Chlorpromazine: 5

A patient develops jaundice and generalized itching after starting chlorpromazine. Laboratory testing shows a cholestatic liver pattern, and imaging does not show an obstructing stone. Which recognized adverse effect should be considered?

  1. Myocarditis
  2. Gastrointestinal hypomotility
  3. Cholestatic drug-induced liver injury
  4. Hyperprolactinemia
  5. Severe neutropenia
Answer and explanation

Cholestatic drug-induced liver injury

Chlorpromazine can cause cholestatic liver injury with jaundice and pruritus.

Option explanations

Cholestatic drug-induced liver injury: Chlorpromazine can cause cholestatic liver injury with jaundice and pruritus.

Hyperprolactinemia: D2 blockade in the tuberoinfundibular pathway can produce galactorrhea, menstrual disturbance and sexual dysfunction. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Severe neutropenia: Clozapine can cause severe neutropenia; ANC monitoring and assessment of infection symptoms remain clinically important. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Myocarditis: New tachycardia, chest symptoms and inflammatory or cardiac-marker abnormalities during clozapine initiation warrant urgent evaluation for myocarditis. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Gastrointestinal hypomotility: Clozapine-associated constipation can progress to ileus or obstruction and must not be dismissed as trivial. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
