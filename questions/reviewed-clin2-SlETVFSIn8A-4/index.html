Medication-related slowing

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 59-year-old with hypertension reports lightheadedness beginning after a recent medication increase. His ECG shows a regular rate of 42/min with a P wave before every QRS. Which medication is most likely to directly explain the sinus slowing?

  1. Amlodipine
  2. Hydralazine
  3. Metoprolol
  4. Losartan
  5. Hydrochlorothiazide
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Metoprolol

Beta blockade reduces sympathetic stimulation of the sinus node.

Understanding the options

Metoprolol
Beta blockade reduces sympathetic stimulation of the sinus node.
Losartan
Losartan does not primarily suppress sinus-node automaticity.
Hydrochlorothiazide
Thiazides can alter electrolytes but do not directly block the sinus node.
Amlodipine
Amlodipine mainly dilates arterioles rather than suppressing nodal activity.
Hydralazine
Hydralazine causes vasodilation and may provoke reflex tachycardia.
