Garden classification: 1

An older adult falls and has an intracapsular femoral neck fracture. The fracture is incomplete and valgus impacted rather than fully displaced. Which Garden category best describes the injury seen on the initial radiographs?

  1. Garden III
  2. Garden IV
  3. Extracapsular intertrochanteric fracture
  4. Garden I
  5. Garden II
Answer and explanation

Garden I

An incomplete, often valgus-impacted femoral neck fracture is Garden I.

Option explanations

Garden I: An incomplete, often valgus-impacted femoral neck fracture is Garden I.

Garden II: A complete but nondisplaced femoral neck fracture is Garden II. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Garden III: A complete femoral neck fracture with partial displacement is Garden III. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Garden IV: A completely displaced femoral neck fracture is Garden IV. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Extracapsular intertrochanteric fracture: An intertrochanteric fracture is outside the Garden femoral-neck classification. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
