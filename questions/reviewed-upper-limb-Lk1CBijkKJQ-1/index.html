Shoulder and arm trauma foundations: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient arrives with a deformed arm after a fall. Before splinting, the clinician records pulses, capillary refill, sensation and motor function, then repeats them afterward. Which assessment principle is being applied to this injury?

  1. CT to define the injury and adjacent structures
  2. Urgent specialist evaluation and controlled treatment
  3. Protected early motion when stability permits
  4. Assess associated injuries beyond the visible fracture
  5. Document neurovascular status before and after intervention
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Document neurovascular status before and after intervention

Serial motor, sensory and perfusion examinations establish baseline function and detect new deficits after reduction or splinting.

Understanding the options
