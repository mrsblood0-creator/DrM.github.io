Duration changes the cellular outcome

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Identical hepatocyte cultures receive the same concentration of a toxin. Cells exposed for five minutes recover after the toxin is removed, whereas cells exposed for several hours show irreversible membrane damage and die. Which principle is best illustrated?

  1. Adaptive responses are always permanent
  2. Morphologic changes always precede biochemical dysfunction
  3. The duration and intensity of an insult influence reversibility
  4. Every toxic exposure produces immediate necrosis
  5. Cell type alone fully determines outcome
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

The duration and intensity of an insult influence reversibility

Injury outcome depends on the nature, severity and duration of the insult as well as cellular susceptibility and repair capacity.

Understanding the options

The duration and intensity of an insult influence reversibility
Injury outcome depends on the nature, severity and duration of the insult as well as cellular susceptibility and repair capacity.
Every toxic exposure produces immediate necrosis
Brief or mild insults may be reversible.
Cell type alone fully determines outcome
Cell type matters but does not eliminate exposure-dependent differences.
Adaptive responses are always permanent
Many adaptations regress when the stimulus is removed.
Morphologic changes always precede biochemical dysfunction
Biochemical dysfunction can precede visible structural change.
