Nefopam: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with a previous convulsive disorder is considered for nefopam. The medication review identifies a specific contraindication in the product information. Which neurologic safety concern most directly explains why this history matters?

  1. Tachycardia and sweating
  2. Seizure risk
  3. Central nonopioid analgesia
  4. MAO inhibitor interaction
  5. Antimuscarinic-type adverse effects
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Seizure risk

Nefopam is contraindicated in patients with a history of convulsive disorders in relevant product labeling.

Understanding the options

Seizure risk
Nefopam is contraindicated in patients with a history of convulsive disorders in relevant product labeling.
Central nonopioid analgesia
Nefopam has centrally mediated analgesic effects; monoamine handling is implicated, but its mechanism is not fully defined. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
MAO inhibitor interaction
Nefopam should not be combined with MAO inhibitors under its prescribing restrictions. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Antimuscarinic-type adverse effects
Dry mouth and urinary retention can occur; angle-closure glaucoma warrants caution. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Tachycardia and sweating
Nefopam can produce autonomic adverse effects including tachycardia and sweating. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
