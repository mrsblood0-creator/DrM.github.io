Maintenance rather than acute mania

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 36-year-old patient with bipolar I disorder is stable after treatment of an acute mood episode. Lamotrigine is being considered for ongoing care. Which description most accurately distinguishes its established role from treatment of an acute manic episode?

  1. Maintenance therapy to delay subsequent mood episodes
  2. Rapid tranquilization during acute mania
  3. Immediate reversal of lithium toxicity
  4. Treatment of acute mania by dopamine receptor blockade
  5. A stimulant for acute bipolar insomnia
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Maintenance therapy to delay subsequent mood episodes

Lamotrigine has a bipolar I maintenance indication; it is not established as an acute antimanic treatment.

Understanding the options

Maintenance therapy to delay subsequent mood episodes
Lamotrigine has a bipolar I maintenance indication; it is not established as an acute antimanic treatment.
Rapid tranquilization during acute mania
Lamotrigine requires titration and is not a rapid tranquilizer.
Immediate reversal of lithium toxicity
It does not reverse lithium poisoning.
Treatment of acute mania by dopamine receptor blockade
Lamotrigine is not a dopamine-blocking antipsychotic.
A stimulant for acute bipolar insomnia
It is not prescribed as a stimulant for insomnia.
