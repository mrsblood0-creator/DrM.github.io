Normal chest radiograph anatomy: 4

During a systematic chest-film review, a student examines the acute lateral junction between the diaphragm and chest wall for blunting that might indicate pleural fluid. Which named anatomical landmark is being assessed at that location?

  1. Normally higher right hemidiaphragm
  2. Patient rotation
  3. Costophrenic angle
  4. Right atrium
  5. Left ventricle
Answer and explanation

Costophrenic angle

The lateral junction of diaphragm and chest wall is normally sharp; blunting may indicate pleural fluid or another abnormality.
