Wound-healing intention: 3

A contaminated wound is initially left open for treatment and reassessment, then closed once conditions are appropriate. Which closure strategy distinguishes this staged approach from immediate approximation and permanent healing by an open method?

  1. Collagen remodeling
  2. Delayed primary closure
  3. Primary intention
  4. Secondary intention
  5. Granulation tissue formation
Answer and explanation

Delayed primary closure

A wound is initially left open and later closed after reassessment and appropriate wound care.

Option explanations

Delayed primary closure: A wound is initially left open and later closed after reassessment and appropriate wound care.

Primary intention: Clean edges are approximated so healing occurs across a small tissue gap. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Secondary intention: An open defect heals through granulation, contraction and epithelialization. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Granulation tissue formation: New capillaries and fibroblasts form the vascular connective tissue of the proliferative phase. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Collagen remodeling: Later remodeling changes collagen organization and strength; healed tissue does not instantly regain normal tensile strength. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
