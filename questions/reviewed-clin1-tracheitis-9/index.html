Falling noise with worsening condition

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A child with severe tracheitis initially has loud stridor. Later the stridor becomes quieter, but he is drowsy with shallow breathing and markedly reduced air entry. What is the most concerning interpretation of this change?

  1. Impending respiratory failure with reduced airflow
  2. Recovery from obstruction with improved airway caliber
  3. A transient change in pitch without physiologic significance
  4. Isolated lower-airway bronchospasm replacing the tracheal obstruction
  5. Reduced oxygen consumption after fever treatment
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Impending respiratory failure with reduced airflow

A quieter airway can reflect insufficient airflow to generate sound, especially when accompanied by exhaustion.

Understanding the options

Impending respiratory failure with reduced airflow
A quieter airway can reflect insufficient airflow to generate sound, especially when accompanied by exhaustion.
Recovery from obstruction with improved airway caliber
Recovery should improve alertness and air entry; drowsiness and shallow breathing indicate deterioration.
A transient change in pitch without physiologic significance
The accompanying mental-status and ventilation changes make this clinically significant.
Isolated lower-airway bronchospasm replacing the tracheal obstruction
The course and poor airflow suggest exhaustion from upper-airway obstruction rather than evidence of a new isolated process.
Reduced oxygen consumption after fever treatment
This would not explain drowsiness, shallow respirations and markedly reduced air entry.
