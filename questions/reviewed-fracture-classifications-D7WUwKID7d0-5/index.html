Diet and fracture healing: evidence-aware review: 5

A patient with a healing fracture reports heavy alcohol use, frequent missed meals and difficulty following the treatment plan. Which assessment focus best addresses the combined nutritional and behavioral concerns without promising a precise food-based healing timetable?

  1. Appropriate mechanical stability
  2. Adequate blood supply
  3. Individualized medication review
  4. Assess harmful alcohol use and nutritional displacement
  5. Adequate protein and micronutrient intake
Answer and explanation

Assess harmful alcohol use and nutritional displacement

Heavy alcohol use can accompany nutritional deficiency, falls and poor adherence and merits individualized support.

Option explanations

Assess harmful alcohol use and nutritional displacement: Heavy alcohol use can accompany nutritional deficiency, falls and poor adherence and merits individualized support.

Adequate protein and micronutrient intake: Healing needs adequate nutrition; correcting deficiency is more defensible than promising benefit from excessive supplementation. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Appropriate mechanical stability: Nutrition cannot compensate for inadequate fracture stability. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Adequate blood supply: Biological healing requires viable perfused tissue as well as mechanical support. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Individualized medication review: The risks and benefits of medicines during healing should be reviewed; prescribed drugs should not be stopped indiscriminately. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
