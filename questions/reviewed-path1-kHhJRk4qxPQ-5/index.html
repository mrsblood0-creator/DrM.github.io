Loss of protein synthesis during reversible injury

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

Hepatocytes subjected to a short period of hypoxia remain viable but show dilated rough endoplasmic reticulum and ribosomes detached from its surface. A pulse-labeling experiment is performed during this stage. Which cellular function is most directly reduced?

  1. DNA replication within the nucleus
  2. Synthesis of secreted and membrane proteins
  3. Breakdown of hydrogen peroxide
  4. ATP generation by the mitochondrial respiratory chain
  5. Lysosomal degradation of glycogen
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Synthesis of secreted and membrane proteins

Ribosome detachment reduces translation, especially of proteins normally synthesized on rough endoplasmic reticulum.

Understanding the options

Synthesis of secreted and membrane proteins
Ribosome detachment reduces translation, especially of proteins normally synthesized on rough endoplasmic reticulum.
Breakdown of hydrogen peroxide
Peroxide removal involves catalase and peroxidases rather than rough-ER ribosomes.
ATP generation by the mitochondrial respiratory chain
Respiratory-chain ATP production is mitochondrial; it may also fall but is not the direct function illustrated.
Lysosomal degradation of glycogen
Lysosomal degradation is not performed by attached ribosomes.
DNA replication within the nucleus
DNA replication is distinct from ribosomal translation.
