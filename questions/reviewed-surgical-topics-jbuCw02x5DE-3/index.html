Hernia treatment principles: 3

A surgeon plans a mesh-based repair for an appropriate abdominal-wall hernia. Which mechanical purpose explains the implant’s role rather than suggesting that mesh treats a systemic disorder or substitutes for assessment of bowel viability?

  1. Preperitoneal plane
  2. Individualize mesh and repair choice to contamination and patient factors
  3. Reinforcement of the abdominal-wall defect
  4. Urgent surgical assessment for suspected strangulation
  5. Shared decision-making about watchful waiting in selected patients
Answer and explanation

Reinforcement of the abdominal-wall defect

Mesh can provide durable support in suitable repairs; technique and patient factors matter.

Option explanations

Reinforcement of the abdominal-wall defect: Mesh can provide durable support in suitable repairs; technique and patient factors matter.

Urgent surgical assessment for suspected strangulation: Severe pain, tenderness and systemic or ischemic signs must not be managed as a routine elective bulge. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Shared decision-making about watchful waiting in selected patients: Some minimally symptomatic men with inguinal hernia can choose observation after counseling; this is not appropriate for every hernia. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Preperitoneal plane: Preperitoneal mesh lies outside the peritoneal cavity, between peritoneum and the abdominal-wall fascial layers. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Individualize mesh and repair choice to contamination and patient factors: Contamination, bowel viability, defect and expertise influence repair; neither universal mesh use nor a universal ban is appropriate. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
