Hernia treatment principles: 3

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A surgeon plans a mesh-based repair for an appropriate abdominal-wall hernia. Which mechanical purpose explains the implant’s role rather than suggesting that mesh treats a systemic disorder or substitutes for assessment of bowel viability?

  1. Preperitoneal plane
  2. Individualize mesh and repair choice to contamination and patient factors
  3. Reinforcement of the abdominal-wall defect
  4. Urgent surgical assessment for suspected strangulation
  5. Shared decision-making about watchful waiting in selected patients
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Reinforcement of the abdominal-wall defect

Mesh can provide durable support in suitable repairs; technique and patient factors matter.

Understanding the options

Shared decision-making about watchful waiting in selected patients
Some minimally symptomatic men with inguinal hernia can choose observation after counseling; this is not appropriate for every hernia. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Preperitoneal plane
Preperitoneal mesh lies outside the peritoneal cavity, between peritoneum and the abdominal-wall fascial layers. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.

Contamination, bowel viability, defect and expertise influence repair; neither universal mesh use nor a universal ban is appropriate. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
