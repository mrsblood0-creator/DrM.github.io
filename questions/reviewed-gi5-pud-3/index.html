Perforation

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with chronic epigastric pain suddenly develops severe generalized abdominal pain and a rigid abdomen. Upright imaging shows free air beneath the diaphragm. Which complication of peptic ulcer disease best explains this abrupt deterioration?

  1. Uncomplicated mucosal healing
  2. Simple reflux without tissue injury
  3. Functional dyspepsia
  4. An isolated medication taste disturbance
  5. Perforation with peritoneal contamination
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Perforation with peritoneal contamination

A full-thickness defect can release luminal contents and cause acute peritonitis.

Understanding the options

Perforation with peritoneal contamination
A full-thickness defect can release luminal contents and cause acute peritonitis.
Uncomplicated mucosal healing
Healing does not cause free intraperitoneal air.
Simple reflux without tissue injury
Reflux does not explain a rigid abdomen and free air.
Functional dyspepsia
A functional disorder does not account for these objective findings.
An isolated medication taste disturbance
This is unrelated to the acute abdomen.
