Neurovascular and visceral fracture complications: 1

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient with a humeral shaft fracture has wrist drop and reduced sensation over the dorsal first web space. Which associated neural injury best accounts for this characteristic examination pattern after the upper-arm fracture?

  1. Arterial injury with threatened perfusion
  2. Radial nerve injury
  3. Axillary nerve injury
  4. Sciatic nerve injury
  5. Bladder or urethral injury
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Radial nerve injury

A humeral shaft fracture can injure the radial nerve and cause wrist drop.

Understanding the options

Radial nerve injury
A humeral shaft fracture can injure the radial nerve and cause wrist drop.
Axillary nerve injury
Shoulder dislocation can impair deltoid function and lateral shoulder sensation. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Sciatic nerve injury
Posterior hip dislocation can injure the sciatic nerve, particularly its peroneal division. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Bladder or urethral injury
Pelvic trauma with hematuria or blood at the urethral meatus requires assessment for urinary tract injury. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
Arterial injury with threatened perfusion
A cool limb, abnormal pulses and impaired perfusion after trauma demand urgent vascular assessment. This does not best answer the specific question in this case.
