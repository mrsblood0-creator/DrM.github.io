Empiric antimicrobial coverage

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A child with suspected bacterial tracheitis is admitted to intensive care after airway stabilization. Cultures are pending. Which organism is especially important to cover empirically, with resistance patterns considered when selecting the regimen?

  1. Staphylococcus aureus
  2. Mycoplasma pneumoniae
  3. Bordetella pertussis
  4. Mycobacterium tuberculosis
  5. Chlamydia trachomatis
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Staphylococcus aureus

S aureus is an important pathogen; empiric treatment also covers other likely respiratory bacteria and may need MRSA activity.

Understanding the options

Staphylococcus aureus
S aureus is an important pathogen; empiric treatment also covers other likely respiratory bacteria and may need MRSA activity.
Mycoplasma pneumoniae
This is associated with atypical lower respiratory infection rather than the classic purulent obstructive tracheitis syndrome.
Bordetella pertussis
Pertussis usually causes paroxysmal cough rather than an acutely toxic purulent tracheal obstruction.
Mycobacterium tuberculosis
Tuberculosis does not usually present as rapidly progressive postviral bacterial tracheitis.
Chlamydia trachomatis
This is associated with afebrile infant pneumonia and is not the characteristic target in this setting.
