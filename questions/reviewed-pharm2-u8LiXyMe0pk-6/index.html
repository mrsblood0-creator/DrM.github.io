Blood pressure and bleeding risk

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient evaluated for thrombolytic treatment has repeated markedly elevated blood pressure readings that remain uncontrolled despite initial measures. No active external bleeding is seen. Why does this finding materially affect treatment eligibility?

  1. It increases the risk of serious intracranial bleeding
  2. It prevents alteplase from binding any plasminogen
  3. It guarantees that the vascular event is hemorrhagic
  4. It converts plasmin into a platelet activator
  5. It makes all anticoagulant laboratory tests normal
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

It increases the risk of serious intracranial bleeding

Severe uncontrolled hypertension is an important bleeding-related contraindication.

Understanding the options

It increases the risk of serious intracranial bleeding
Severe uncontrolled hypertension is an important bleeding-related contraindication.
It prevents alteplase from binding any plasminogen
Blood pressure does not abolish molecular binding to plasminogen.
It guarantees that the vascular event is hemorrhagic
Hypertension alone does not prove hemorrhage.
It converts plasmin into a platelet activator
Plasmin is not converted into a platelet agonist by pressure.
It makes all anticoagulant laboratory tests normal
Coagulation tests do not become predictably normal because of hypertension.
