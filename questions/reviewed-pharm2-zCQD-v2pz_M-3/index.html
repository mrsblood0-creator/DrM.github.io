Factors affected by warfarin

A researcher measures newly synthesized hepatic proteins after vitamin K antagonism. Several procoagulant factors lose normal function, while factor V is relatively unaffected by this mechanism. Which group contains the principal vitamin K-dependent procoagulant factors?

  1. II, VII, IX and X
  2. I, V, VIII and XIII
  3. V, VII, VIII and XI
  4. I, II, V and XII
  5. VIII, XI, XII and XIII
Answer and explanation

II, VII, IX and X

Factors II, VII, IX and X require vitamin K-dependent carboxylation.

Option explanations

II, VII, IX and X: Factors II, VII, IX and X require vitamin K-dependent carboxylation.

I, V, VIII and XIII: These are not the classic vitamin K-dependent group.

V, VII, VIII and XI: V, VIII and XI are not in the targeted set.

I, II, V and XII: Fibrinogen, V and XII are not the principal vitamin K-dependent factors.

VIII, XI, XII and XIII: These factors are not the classic vitamin K-dependent set.
