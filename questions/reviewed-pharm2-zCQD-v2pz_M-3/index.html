Factors affected by warfarin

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A researcher measures newly synthesized hepatic proteins after vitamin K antagonism. Several procoagulant factors lose normal function, while factor V is relatively unaffected by this mechanism. Which group contains the principal vitamin K-dependent procoagulant factors?

  1. II, VII, IX and X
  2. I, V, VIII and XIII
  3. V, VII, VIII and XI
  4. I, II, V and XII
  5. VIII, XI, XII and XIII
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

II, VII, IX and X

Factors II, VII, IX and X require vitamin K-dependent carboxylation.

Understanding the options

II, VII, IX and X
Factors II, VII, IX and X require vitamin K-dependent carboxylation.
I, V, VIII and XIII
These are not the classic vitamin K-dependent group.
V, VII, VIII and XI
V, VIII and XI are not in the targeted set.
I, II, V and XII
Fibrinogen, V and XII are not the principal vitamin K-dependent factors.
VIII, XI, XII and XIII
These factors are not the classic vitamin K-dependent set.
