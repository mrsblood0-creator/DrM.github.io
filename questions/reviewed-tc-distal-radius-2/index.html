distal radius — pattern 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A 45-year-old man falls onto a flexed wrist. Radiographs show an extra-articular distal radial fracture with the distal fragment angulated toward the palm. There is no rim fragment carrying a subluxed carpus and no isolated styloid fracture. Which distal radius fracture pattern is described?

  1. Die-punch fracture
  2. Colles fracture
  3. Smith fracture
  4. Barton fracture
  5. Chauffeur fracture
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Smith fracture

Smith fracture: An extra-articular distal radius fracture with volar displacement or angulation.

Understanding the options

Smith fracture
Smith fracture: An extra-articular distal radius fracture with volar displacement or angulation.
Barton fracture
Barton fracture describes an intra-articular rim shear fracture with associated radiocarpal subluxation or dislocation. The case instead matches Smith fracture.
Chauffeur fracture
Chauffeur fracture describes an intra-articular fracture of the radial styloid. The case instead matches Smith fracture.
Die-punch fracture
Die-punch fracture describes an impacted depression of the distal radial lunate fossa. The case instead matches Smith fracture.
Colles fracture
Colles fracture describes an extra-articular distal radius fracture with dorsal displacement or angulation. The case instead matches Smith fracture.
