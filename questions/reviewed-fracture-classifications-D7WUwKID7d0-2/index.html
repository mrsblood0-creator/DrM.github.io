Diet and fracture healing: evidence-aware review: 2

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

A patient recovering from a fracture has lost weight and eats very little protein. Rather than banning all named foods, the clinician assesses calorie and nutrient adequacy. Which nutritional principle is most directly relevant to supporting healing?

  1. Activity matched to fracture stability
  2. Adequate protein and micronutrient intake
  3. Appropriate mechanical stability
  4. Adequate blood supply
  5. Individualized medication review
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Adequate protein and micronutrient intake

Healing needs adequate nutrition; correcting deficiency is more defensible than promising benefit from excessive supplementation.

Understanding the options

Adequate blood supply
