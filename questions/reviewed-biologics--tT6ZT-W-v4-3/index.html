An infusion complication

Choose the single best answer, then reveal the explanation.

During a reslizumab infusion, a 55-year-old man develops flushing, wheezing, throat tightness and hypotension. The infusion had been started for maintenance treatment of eosinophilic asthma. Which action is most appropriate regarding the infusion?

  1. Treat this as an isolated local infusion-site reaction
  2. Wait for a later eosinophil count before acting
  3. Stop the infusion and initiate emergency management for anaphylaxis
  4. Continue at the same rate because wheezing is part of asthma
  5. Increase the infusion rate to complete treatment sooner
Reveal answer and explanation
Correct answer

Stop the infusion and initiate emergency management for anaphylaxis

The multisystem reaction requires prompt treatment, not continued exposure.

Understanding the options

Stop the infusion and initiate emergency management for anaphylaxis
The multisystem reaction requires prompt treatment, not continued exposure.
Continue at the same rate because wheezing is part of asthma
The accompanying hypotension and throat symptoms indicate a systemic reaction.
Increase the infusion rate to complete treatment sooner
This increases exposure during a suspected serious reaction.
Treat this as an isolated local infusion-site reaction
The symptoms are systemic rather than local.
Wait for a later eosinophil count before acting
Emergency treatment must not be delayed for a laboratory result.
