Amlodipine is antihypertensive medication that works by blocking all types of voltage gated Calcium channels in blood vessels to dilate these vessels and reduce blood pressure

Here we will talk about the serious side effects that comes with amlodipine

Heart failure and pulmonary edema

Rare side effect

Heart failure manifest as cough, SOB, fatigue, edema ,

It is caused by excessive vasodilatation leading to severe hypotension and reduction in blood flow to the vital organs including the heart which lead to ischemia, heart failure

Also caused by negative inotropic effect on the heart at higher doses

Acute kidney injury

Rare

Aki manifest as nausea and vomiting, confusion, reduced urine output, abdominal pain

It may lead to AKI Due to hypotension

Amlodipine induced liver injury

rare

Amlodipine lead to a mixed hepatocellular and cholestatic pattern of liver injury

Acute liver injury manifest as nausea, vomiting confusion, yellowish dislocarattion of skin and mucous membranes, abdominal swelling

Amlodipine liver injury is reversible and complete recovery occur after 6 weeks of stopping the medication

Rhabdomyolysis

Rare

Rhabdomyolysis is muscle breakdown which manifest as severe muscle pain and swelling, exahstion, dark red urine and fever

Amlodipine cause rhabdomyolysis by direct muscle toxicity and due to hypotension
