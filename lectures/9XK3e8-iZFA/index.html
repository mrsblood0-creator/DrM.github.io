Scoring — eye opening

0:26 in the video

  • 1: None
  • 2: Open to pain
  • 3: Open to voice
  • 4: Open spontaneously

Scoring — verbal response

0:26 in the video

  • 1: None
  • 2: Incomprehensible sounds (like moaning)
  • 3: Inappropriate words (random)
  • 4: Confused or disoriented
  • 5: Oriented

Scoring — motor response

0:26 in the video

  • 1: None
  • 2: Extensor posturing
  • 3: Flexor posturing
  • 4: Withdrawal in response to pain
  • 5: Localize pain
  • 6: Follows commands

Interpretation

2:04 in the video

  • GCS 13–15 = mild injury
  • GCS 9–12 = moderate injury
  • GCS 3–8 = severe injury

Limitations

2:36 in the video

  • A variety of independent factors may interfere with the applicability of the GCS to trauma patients such as intoxication, sepsis or shock
  • Interobserver reliability is imperfect
