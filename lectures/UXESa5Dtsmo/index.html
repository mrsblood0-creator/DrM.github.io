Overview

CURB65 is a score used to evaluate the severity of pneumonia and help you to decide if you hospitalize the patient or not

Developed by the British thoracic society in 2002

CURB-65

C: confusion, if pt confused gets one point

U: Blood Urea nitrogen greater than 19 mg/dL, gets one point

R: Respiratory rate of 30 or more gets one point

B: Blood pressure: if systolic less than 90 or diastolic less than 60, patient gets one point

65: Age higher than 65 gets one point

You add all the points together and you get the score, If the score is equal or higher than 2 then hospitalize your patient

Risk of death from pneumonia

Risk of death increase as the score increases

Score 0 = 0.7% risk of death

Score 1 = 3.2%

Score 2 = 13%

Score 3 = 17%

Score 4 = 41.5%

Score 5 = 57%
