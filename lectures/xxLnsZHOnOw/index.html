Mediastinum

  • Heart
  • Great vessels
  • Trachea
  • Esophagus
  • thymus
  • Nerves
  • Lymph nodes

Pulmonary vessels

  • Pulmonary vessels branch to upper and lower lobes from the lung hilum
  • Upper lobes vessels are smaller in diameter than lower lobes vessels on erect chest x ray
  • In pulmonary hypertension, the opposite happen

Trachea

  • Aortic arch
  • Descending thoracic aorta
  • Superior vena cava
  • Left ventricle
  • Right atrium
  • Right hemidiaphragm
  • Left hemidiaphragm

Clavicle

  • Posterior and anterior rib
  • Right and left costophrenic angles
  • Right and left lung hilum
  • Lung apex
