Mechanism of action

  • Kidney
  • Mannitol increase the intravascular osmotic pressure

Therapeutic effects

  • Brain edema
  • Acute closed angle glaucoma

Adverse effects

  • Dehydration
  • Dilutional Hypernatremia
  • Extracellular water expansion
