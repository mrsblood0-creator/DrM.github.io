hypotension

  • Hypotension is decrease in blood pressure
  • Hypotension lead to lightheadedness, fainting, blurred vision, fatigue, nausea and vomiting , sudden drop in BP when standing up from a seated position (orthostatic hypotension)
  • Hypotension is related to the dose given (too high)
  • Coadministration of amlodipine and clarithromycin or erythromycin increase risk of hypotension (decrease metabolism of CYP3A4)

Reflex tachycardia

  • Reflex tachycardia lead to palpitation (heart racing)
  • If blood pressure decreases, heart beats faster in attempt to raise it
  • Occur in the beginning of the treatment

Peripheral Edema

  • Edema is accumulation of fluid and swelling of the lower limbs
  • Edema due to hypotension and vasodilatation
  • Dose dependent
  • Reduced by mobilization

Headache

  • Tension (tight band around the head)
  • Occur in the beginning of the treatment

Flushing

  • Marked reddens of the face
