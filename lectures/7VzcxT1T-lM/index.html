Anderson and D’Alonzo Classification of Odontoid Process Fractures

  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Definition

  • 3 types classification system for odontoid process fractures
  • It takes into account the level of the fracture in relation to the odontoid process and the C2 vertebral body
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Classification

  • Type 1: fracture at the tip of the odontoid process
  • Type 2: fracture at the base of the odontoid process
  • Type 2a: comminuted fracture at the base of the odontoid process
  • Type 3: fracture at the body of the C2 vertebrae
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Type 1

  • Fracture location: at the tip of the odontoid process
  • Fracture type: simple avulsion fracture
  • Mechanism of injury: lateral force applied when head is extended
  • Rare
  • Stable
  • Treatment: immobilization until pain subsides
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Type 2

  • Fracture location: at the junction of the odontoid process and the body of the axis
  • Fracture type: simple
  • Mechanism of injury: hyperflexion => anterior displacement of the dens, hyperextension => posterior displacement
  • Most common
  • High non union rates
  • Unstable
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Treatment

  • Young patients with undisplaced fracture: traction followed by collar or halo vest
  • Elderly patients with undisplaced fracture: traction followed by collar, surgery has poor risk to benefit ratio
  • Patients with displaced fracture: Surgery by inserting anterior lag screw from C2 body into the dens
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Type 2a

  • Fracture location: at the base of the odontoid process
  • Fracture type: comminuted
  • Mechanism of injury: same as type 2
  • Even higher nonunion rates compared to type 2
  • Represent 5- 10% of type 2 fractures
  • Treatment: surgery (posterior C1- C2 fusion)
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Type 3

  • Fracture location: through the odontoid and into the lateral masses of the axis vertebrae
  • Fracture type: simple
  • Mechanism of injury: unclear
  • Relatively stable
  • Best prognosis for healing
  • Treatment
  • Undisplaced fractures: immobilization for 8- 12 weeks
  • Displaced fractures: reduction by traction then immobilization for 8- 12 weeks
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden
