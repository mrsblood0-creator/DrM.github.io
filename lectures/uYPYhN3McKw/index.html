Mechanism of action

2:45 in the video

  • Aspirin inhibits cyclooxygenase enzyme and this includes both COX-1 and COX-2.
  • It irreversibly inhibits COX-1 and COX-2 enzymes.
  • Reduces thromboxane and PG levels in the body.

Aspirin uses

3:35 in the video

  • Analgesic effect.
  • Antipyretic effect: Reduce prostaglandin synthesis in the CNS.
  • Anti-inflammatory.
  • Antiplatelet: through inhibiting COX-1 in the platelets

Dosage

5:05 in the video

  • Low dose (less than 325mg) works on platelet aggregation.
  • Medium dose (325-1250mg) works as antipyretic and analgesic.
  • High dose (1250-4000) works as anti-inflammatory.

Adverse effects

5:35 in the video

  • Increase bleeding risk.
  • Gastrointestinal complications.
  • Increased renal risk.
  • Reye’s syndrome.
