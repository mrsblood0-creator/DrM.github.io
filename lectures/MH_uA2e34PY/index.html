Amlodipine is antihypertensive medication that works by blocking all types of voltage gated Calcium channels in blood vessels to dilate these vessels and reduce blood pressure

  • Here we will talk about the 6 contraindications of amlodipine

Hypersensitivity reactions (allergic reactions)

  • Patient with known Hypersensitivity to amlodipine
  • Allergic reactions include: Rash, itching, hives, swelling of the face, lips tongue or throat , SJS
  • This is an absolute contraindicated

Unstable angina

  • Relative contraindication
  • Amlodipine cause reflex tachycardia which would be harmful to the heart in unstable angina leading to increase in myocardial oxygen demand and worsening ischemia

Severe hypotension

  • Relative contraindication
  • Amlodipine further reduce blood pressure leading to vital organ hypoperfusion and syncope

Cardiogenic shock

  • Relative contraindication
  • In cardiogenic shock , the heart can’t pump blood effectively and this is exacerbated by the vasodilatation caused by the amlodipine,
  • Because vasoditation means more blood flow required and more stress on the heart

Severe aortic stenosis

  • The left ventricle is already exhausted from pumping blood through the stenosed aortic valve
  • Amlodipine causes vasodilatation (more blood flow required to be pumped through the stenosed valve leading to ventricular collapse and dysfunction

Hepatic impairment

  • Relative contraindication
  • Metabolized by hepatic CYP enzyme system (CYP 3A4 ) , in hepatic impairment can’t be metabolized and will cause accumulation and toxicity
  • Can be solved by dose adjustment
