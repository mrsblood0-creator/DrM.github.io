Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitors

  • Pharmacokinetics
  • Mechanism of Action
  • Therapeutic Uses
  • Adverse Effects

Pharmacokinetics

Mechanism of action

  • Diuretic
  • Decrease aqueous humor production
  • Decrease CSF secretion

Therapeutic uses

  • Not used as diuretic anymore due to metabolic acidosis and kidney compensating very quickly
  • Glaucoma
  • Acute mountain sickness (respiratory alkalosis)
  • Metabolic alkalosis

Adverse effects

  • Metabolic acidosis
  • Renal stones
  • Potassium Depletion
  • Hypersensitivity reactions
