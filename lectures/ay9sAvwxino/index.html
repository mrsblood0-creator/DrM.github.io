    Garden classification of femoral neck fractures

      Definition

      • 4 grades classification system for femoral neck fractures
      • It classify these fractures according to their prognosis and incidence of complications
      • Based on the pre reduction displacement of the femoral head

      Grade 1

      • Incomplete, nondisplaced
      • Valgus impacted fracture
      • Disruption of lateral cortex while the medial cortex is preserved

      Grade 2

      • Complete, non displaced fracture

      Grade 3

      • Complete, partially displaced fracture

      Grade 4

      • Complete, completely displaced fracture
