Mechanism of Action

  • Celecoxib is highly selective COX-2 inhibitor from the NSAIDs group.
  • COX-2 is expressed during inflammation and it is responsible for the pain and fever that comes with it
  • COX-1 is expressed normally and it has a lot of functions like the secretion of mucus that protect the stomach and the PG- mediated autoregulation of the renal system
  • Non selective NSAIDs inhibit both of them (COX-1 and COX-2) and that lead to side effects on the stomach and kidneys and other organs so that made the idea of creating a selective COX-2 NSAID comes and upon this Celecoxib was made
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden
  • In practice there was fewer side effects regarding the GIT and renal systems but there was increase in cardiovascular events like myocardial infarctions and strokes which didn’t happen with the non selective NSAIDs

Indications

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Juvenile Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Ankylosing spondylitis
  • Acute pain
  • Primary dysmenorrhea
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Adverse effects

  • Cardiovascular thrombotic events
  • Gastrointestinal side effects
  • Hepatotoxicity
  • New onset or worsening of existing hypertension
  • Heart failure and edema
  • Renal toxicity
  • hyperkalemia
  • Anaphylactic reactions
  • Hematological toxicity
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden
