Prazosin, Doxazocin, Terazosin

  • Competitive, selective alpha 1 antagonism => less tachycardia
  • Used for treatment of hypertension => relaxes arterial and venous vascular smooth muscles
  • Used for treatment of benign prostatic hyperplasia => relaxes prostate smooth muscles
  • Hypotension is an adverse effect
  • Youtube: Mustafa Salahalden
  • May inhibit ejaculation
  • Half lives
  • Prazosin: 3 hours
  • Terazosin: 9- 12 hours
  • Doxazocin: 22 hours
  • Youtube: Mustafa Salahalden

Tamsulosin

  • Competitive, selective alpha 1 antagonist, has higher affinity for alpha 1a and alpha 1d
  • Has more effect on prostatic smooth muscles
  • Has less effect on blood pressure
  • May inhibit ejaculation
  • Half life 9- 15 hours
  • Youtube: Mustafa Salahalden

Yohimbine

  • Competitive, selective alpha 2 antagonist
  • Used in treatment of orthostatic hypotension
  • Used to reverse the anti hypertensive action of clonidine
  • Youtube: Mustafa Salahalden
