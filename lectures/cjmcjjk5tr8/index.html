In This Video

  • Management of Asthma according to Global initiative for asthma (GINA) Guidelines

Treatment Goal

  • The long term objective for asthma management is symptoms control and reduction of asthma exacerbations, airway damage and medication side effects

Asthma treatment

  • SABA only treatment in adults or adolescents is no longer recommended. Instead all patients should receive (ICS) either regularly or in patients with mild asthma, they are given as needed ICS-formoterol
  • Studies shown that ICS containing treatment would control symptoms and reduce asthma exacerbations much better than SABA only treatment
  • For children 6-11 years with mild asthma, the same applies, taking ICS whenever SABA is taken is safer than SABA only treatment

GINA treatment tracks

  • The options for treatment of asthma for adults and adolescents are shown in two treatment tracks
  • The key difference between those tracks is the medication that is used for symptoms relief: which is as needed low dose ICS formoterol in Tracks 1 (which is the preferred track), and as needed SABA or as needed ICS SABA in Track 2

Important notes

  • During ongoing treatment, treatment can be stepped up or down along one track, using the same reliever at each step, or it can be switched between tracks according to patient’s needs
  • Before stepping up, check for common problems such as incorrect inhaler technique, poor adherence and environmental exposures and confirm that the symptoms are due to asthma
  • For most patients, Asthma Treatment can be started with step 1: which is either as needed low dose ICS-formoterol (preferred) or regular daily low dose ICS plus as needed SABA or ICS- SABA (alternative)
  • Most patients with asthma don’t need higher doses of ICS, because most of steroids benefits (e.g. prevention of exacerbation) is obtained at low doses
  • If the patient has troublesome asthma symptoms 4- 5 days/week then Consider starting at step 3 with low dose ICS- formoterol as maintenance and reliever (MART) and this is on the preferred track
  • If the patient has severely uncontrolled asthma or low lung function at initial presentation, of if the initial presentation is during an acute exacerbation then start treatment at step 4 with medium dose ICS formoterol maintenance and reliever (MART), with/without a short course of oral corticosteroids
  • Consider stepping down after asthma has been well controlled for 3 months
