Propranolol

  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Mechanism of Action

  • It is nonselective beta blocker that blocks beta 1 and beta 2 receptors
  • Beta 1 receptors found in the heart and it has a positive chronotropic and inotropic effects when activated
  • Beta 2 receptors found in the lungs and it leads bronchodilatation when activated
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Clinical Uses

  • Hypertension
  • Angina
  • Myocardial infarction
  • Hyperthyroidism
  • Migraine
  • Anxiety
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Adverse Effects

  • Bronchoconstriction
  • Arrhythmias
  • Hypoglycemia
  • CNS related problems
  • YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden
