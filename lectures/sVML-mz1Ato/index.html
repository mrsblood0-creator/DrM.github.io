Surgical needles

0:35 in the video

Used to carry the suture material into the tissue

Should be: Strong, Sharp, Slim, Sterile

Needle components: End, Body, Tip

Needle end

1:20 in the video

It is the portion where the suture material is attached to

It is either swaged (eyeless) needles (suture materials connect to the inside of the needle) and there is the eyed needles where suture material connected to the eye of the needle

In swaged needles only one strand of the suture material is attached to the needle and it meant for single use only

In eyed needles two strands of the material is carried one going into the needles and the other outgoing from the needle and the needle size is bigger compared to eyeless needles and this produce bigger holes in the tissue means more tissue trauma and more infection possibility but those are cost effective

Needle body

4:00 in the video

Needle body is either curved or straight and there is degrees to each

Needle tip

6:25 in the video

It is the part that penetrate the tissue, and it is classified according to its cross section

  • Round bodied - tapered: Muscle, fascia, peritoneum, viscera
  • Round bodied - Blunt: friable tissue
  • Cutting: Dense tissue like skin and tendons
  • Reverse cutting: Skin
