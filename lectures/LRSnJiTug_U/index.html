Facts

  • It is one subtype of non Hodgkin's lymphomas, it affects the B cells
  • Named after Denis Burkitt, the Irish surgeon who first described it in 1958
  • Most common in developing countries and affect children

Classification

  • There are three variants of the disease:
  • Endemic variant
  • Sporadic variant
  • Immunodeficiency associated variant

1. Endemic variant

  • Endemic variant almost always associated with Epstein-Barr virus infection
  • EPV causes infectious mononucleosis
  • Endemic variant expresses CD21 (also the same receptor affected by Epstein Barr virus, the virus gains entry to B cells through this receptor)
  • Endemic variant common in regions where malaria is endemic such as Africa and new guinea
  • Affects children 4-7 years , male to female ratio 2:1
  • Presents mostly with facial, mandibular masses, it also presents with abdominal masses or masses affecting nervous system

2. Sporadic variant

  • Common in America and Europe
  • Sporadic form also occur in children most of the times, male to female ratio is 4:1
  • Sporadic form mostly presents with abdominal masses in ileocecum and peritoneum, it also affects the bone marrow and nervous system

3. Immunodeficiency associated variant

  • Associated with HIV infection, post transplant patients
  • Presents in adults 40-45 years, male to female ratio is 1:1
  • Affects GI tract, lymph nodes, bone marrow and nervous system

Pathophysiology

  • Dysregulation of c-myc gene by some type of chromosomal translocation
  • C-myc is cell growth promotor
  • Translocation lead to overexpression of the gene
  • This lead to uncontrolled cell growth and tumor growth

Microscopy

  • Tumor consist of cells that are similar in size and morphology with high proliferative and apoptotic activity
  • There is also scattered tangible body laden macrophages (containing apoptotic tumor cells) gives the tumor starry sky appearance on low power microscopy

Immunohistochemistry

  • Express marker of B cells
  • CD19, CD20, CD22
  • Also expresses CD10 and BCL6
  • mantle cell and diffuse large b cell lymphomas also express CD19, CD20

Treatment

  • Chemotherapy
  • GMALL-B-ALL/NHL2002 protocol,
  • the modified Magrath regimen (R-CODOX-M/IVAC)
  • COPADM
  • hyper-CVAD
  • Tumor lysis syndrome could occur in these patients due to fast response the chemotherapy
