Rockwood classification for AC joint injuries

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Definition

6 grades classification system for acromioclavicular joint injuries

A modification for Allman and Tossy’s 3 grade system, the first and second grades are same in both models, while grade three in Allman and Tossy classification is subdivided into four grades in Rockwood classification

Rockwood classification takes into account

Acromioclavicular joint

Coracoclavicular ligament

Deltoid muscle

Trapezius muscle

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Normal Anatomy

The line between the inferior aspect of the clavicle and the inferior aspect of the acromion should be straight

It is okay if the superior aspect don’t match up

The joint space should be uniform

Normal Coracoclavicular distance is between 11- 13mm and there should be no greater than 5mm difference between right and left

Grade 1

X ray: normal, the clavicle not elevated in respect to acromion

Acromioclavicular ligament: Incomplete injury

Coracoclavicular ligament: no injury

Joint capsule: no injury

Trapezius muscle: attached

Deltoid muscle: attached

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Grade 2

Clavicle elevated but not above superior border of acromion

Acromioclavicular ligament: complete injury

Coracoclavicular ligament: Incomplete injury

Joint capsule: complete injury

Trapezius muscle: minimal disruption of attachment

Deltoid muscle: minimal disruption of attachment

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Grade 3

Clavicle elevated above the superior border of acromion, the coracoclavicular distance is less than 25mm

Acromioclavicular ligament: complete injury

Coracoclavicular ligament: complete injury

Joint capsule: complete injury

Trapezius muscle: complete disruption of attachment

Deltoid muscle: complete disruption of attachment

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Grade 4

Result from posteriorly directed forces that make the clavicle penetrates the trapezius muscle, sometimes completely.

Acromioclavicular ligament: completely injured

Coracoclavicular ligament: completely injured

Joint capsule: completely injured

Trapezius muscle: complete disruption of attachment

Deltoid muscle: complete disruption of attachment

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Grade 5

Clavicle is markedly elevated and coracoclavicular distance is more than 25mm

Acromioclavicular ligament: completely injured

Coracoclavicular ligament: completely injured

Joint capsule: completely injured

Trapezius muscle: complete disruption of attachment

Deltoid muscle: complete disruption of attachment

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden

Grade 6

Inferior displacement of clavicle into the acromion or coracoid process.

Acromioclavicular ligament: completely injured

Coracoclavicular ligament: completely injured

Joint capsule: completely injured

Trapezius muscle: complete disruption of attachment

Deltoid muscle: complete disruption of attachment

YouTube: Mustafa Salahalden
