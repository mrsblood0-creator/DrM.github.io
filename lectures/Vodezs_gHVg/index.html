Third Degree Burn

Craig0927, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons · Giftrapped, CC BY-SA 4.0 <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0>, via Wikimedia Commons

Third degree burn

Full thickness injury through epidermis and dermis into subcutaneous fat

Third degree burn presentation

Leathery skin (white, dark, brown or charred

Loss of sensation (painless)

All dermis is involved

Always require surgery (excision and grafting)

Craig0927, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

Third degree burn healing

Takes months to heal and sometimes it doesn’t

Scarring, contractures will form

Amputation could be required

Excision and grafting always required
