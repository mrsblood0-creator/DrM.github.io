Xolair (Omalizumab): Mechanism of Action, Therapeutic Uses & Adverse Effects

Mechanism of action

  • Omalizumab is a monoclonal antibody and it is recombinant, it is similar to human immunoglobulin G1 and it targets human immunoglobulin E (IgE)
  • IgE immunoglobulin plays a critical role in activating mast cells in asthma (and allergic conditions in general) to release inflammatory mediators responsible for producing asthma symptoms, IgE also activates eosinophils and basophils
  • So by inhibiting IgE, Omalizumab works to inhibit mast cell degranulation and inhibit eosinophils and basophils => this lead to breaking the allergic cascade and control asthma symptoms

Pharmacokinetics

  • Omalizumab is administered subcutaneously and its absorption to systemic circulation is slow, peak plasma concentrations achieved after around 7 days
  • Omalizumab metabolized by the reticuloendothelial system and endothelial cells and eliminated through bile in around 25 days

Therapeutic uses

  • Omalizumab is FDA approved for:
  • Treatment of moderate to severe asthma in patients (older than 6) whose symptoms not adequately controlled with inhaled corticosteroids; Omalizumab is not approved for use in acute asthmatic attacks
  • In treatment of chronic idiopathic or spontaneous urticaria, in patients (older than 12) who received H1 antihistamine treatment and still experiencing symptoms
  • It is also approved for patients (older than 18) for treatment of nasal polyps (in case the nasal corticosteroids didn’t work)
  • Off label uses:
  • ANCA vasculitis
  • Bronchopulmonary aspergillosis
  • Keratoconjunctivitis
  • Drug and food allergies
  • Eosinophilic otitis media
  • Bullous pemphigoid
  • Atopic dermatitis

Adverse effects

  • Omalizumab is a targeted therapy, that is why it is considered to have an excellent safety profile
  • Omalizumab may lead to the following
  • Anaphylactic reactions (in the second dose and after in 0.001 of cases)
  • Injection site reactions
  • URTI
  • Sinusitis
  • Headache
  • Pharyngitis

Contraindications

  • Previous hypersensitivity reactions to omalizumab
  • Patients must be closely observed for several hours after omalizumab administration due to risk for anaphylaxis
