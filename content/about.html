My goal

My goal is to make medicine easier to learn, and I take a long time to figure out an easier way to teach each topic I make videos about. That is why my classes take a long time to be made and published.

All my classes are in playlists that you can find in the Playlists section of my YouTube channel. You can also explore the medical courses on this website.
